# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.0.49

**Efeitos Críticos are applied** (`docs/rules/efeitos-criticos.txt`), and the **Margem Crítica
Maior** is a real margin.

## Efeitos Críticos Ofensivos

- All 23 catalog identities are built by `effect.CriticalEffects#create` except **Desmembrar**
  (no limb model). New classes: Amaldiçoar, Amenizar, Atordoante, Cataclismo, Dilacerar, Empalar,
  Estilhaçador, Excruciante, Ferida Profunda, Fortalecer, Guilhotina, Imunizar, Inflamar, Oferenda
  Maldita, Potencializar, Prevenir, Toque do AEther.
- `effect.CriticalEffectContext` carries the attacker, the attack source, the severity (a defender's
  Falha Crítica reads as the Acerto of the same tier) and a **`util.DiceRoller`** — the caller's
  dice; this core still never rolls. No roller → a dice-bearing effect is not built and is listed
  on `DeliveredAttackResult`/`IncomingAttackResult#getUnappliedCriticalEffects()`.
- `AttackDelivery` and `AttackReceiver` now apply, beside the caller's and the Talentos':
  the attack source's **own** Efeito Crítico (a weapon's column or a Magia's), a Título's
  `criticalEffectOverride`, each Título's `resolveAdditionalCriticalEffects`, the request's
  `additionalCriticalEffectTypes`, and `resolveExtraNaturalCriticalEffectApplications` (Finalização —
  also on a non-critical hit, at Menor).
- A forged `AbstractWeapon` copy carries its template's Efeito Crítico and Margem Crítica Menor.

## Efeitos Críticos Defensivos

- `effect.DefensiveCriticalEffect` builds all nine; `AttackReceiver` reports them on a Defesa roll's
  Acerto Crítico (`IncomingAttackResult#getDefensiveCriticalEffects()`), one per worn Armadura/Escudo
  (cumulative), **Liberdade de Ação for "Corpo Exposto"**, plus `AventyrTitle
  #resolveAdditionalDefensiveCriticalEffects`. The caller calls `apply()` on each; what needs
  geometry or a second action returns on `DefensiveCriticalOutcome`.
- Ímpeto Defensivo Maior's immunity is honoured by `AttackReceiver` (the attack is defended).

## Margem Crítica Maior

- `SkillRoll#getCriticalResult(int, int, int)` takes it; `Weapon.DEFAULT_MAJOR_CRITICAL_MARGIN` = 18
  (three 6s), so existing behaviour is unchanged.
- Widened by `AventyrTitle#resolveMajorCriticalMarginIncrease` (Campeão da Taverna) and
  `Item#resolveEnhancementMajorCriticalMarginIncrease` (Decisiva's Favor, gated on Destreza 5).
- Pushed back by the target's RC (−1 per instance) and RA (−1 once), never past 18. RA's −1 now
  reaches the Margem Crítica **Menor** too (`CriticalService#getLesserCriticalResistance`).

## New sheet mechanisms

`AttackerGuard` (Defesa/margin/immunity against one attacker, read via the defence roll's
`SceneContext#getOpposedCharacter()`), `applyEffectUntilCombatEnds`, `applyEffectUntilRest`,
`DamageReceipt` (recorded by `DamageInteraction`), `Burning`/`extinguish`, `Impalement`/
`removeImpalement`, `DeterminationDrain`, `EnchantmentWard`, `HalvedHealing`,
`ModifierType.LESSER_CRITICAL_MARGIN`.

## Behaviour changes

- A heal now interrupts `ManaDrain` and `DeterminationDrain` as well as `Bleeding` — the three
  catalog clauses all say "Efeitos de cura interrompem a perda".
- `HitPointsService#getLifeMultiplier(Character, CombatantSheet)` reads a sheet-held
  `LIFE_MULTIPLIER` bonus; Mana and Determinação gain the same sheet-aware multiplier overloads, and
  `getCurrent*Points` use them.
- Senhor da Briga: Guilhotina, Finalização's Corrente, Campeão's Maior margin, Ímpeto Defensivo,
  Contra-atacante, Impacto Elemental's Cataclismo and Rolamento Ofensivo's +3 Defesas are real.

## Readings to confirm

- Ferida Profunda's lost Multiplicadores last until the combat ends (the text gives no Duração).
- Dilacerar's lost point returns at any Descanso.
- Estilhaçador's second line (printed "Maior") is read as the Menor tier.
- Dano Físico **Primordial** (Provocar, Retorno de Danos) is applied as bare PV loss.
- Repelir e Suprimir Maior silences a repelled caster for 1 Rodada (Silêncio also blocks abilities).
