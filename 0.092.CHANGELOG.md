# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.0.92

**Invocations and Subordinados** — the ALIADOS DA NATUREZA tree, end to end. Rules: `docs/rules/subordinados.txt`,
`docs/rules/magias.txt`; plan: `docs/plans/invocations-plan.md`.

## New

### Subordinados (`org.aventyrs.core.subordinate`)

- **`Subordinate`** — a `TemporaryEffect` on its commander, holding:
  - a grade (`SubordinateGrade`): Bispo, Cavaleiro, Peão, Rainha, Rei or Torre;
  - the benefit picked when gained (`SubordinateBenefit`);
  - whether it is Prodigioso;
  - its source and, optionally, the creature's id and a Duração.
- **`SubordinateService#command`** enforces the limits:
  - at most as many as Carisma (`SUBORDINATE_LIMIT_REACHED`);
  - no two of one grade unless one is Prodigioso (`SUBORDINATE_GRADE_HELD`).
- `#dismiss` and `#renewAfterLongRest` complete the service.
- `SubordinateBenefits#count` counts the commander's own Subordinados plus their allies' Prodigiosos.
- **Each benefit is read where its stat is read:**

  | Grade | Benefit | Where it is read | Combat only |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Torre | +2 Defesas | `DefenseServiceImpl` | yes |
  | Torre | RA | `DamageServiceImpl` | yes |
  | Cavaleiro | Vantagem on Ataque | `AbstractSkillInteraction` | yes |
  | Cavaleiro | Vantagem on Dano | `AbstractSkillInteraction` | yes |
  | Peão | Vantagem on other Perícias | `AbstractSkillInteraction` | no |
  | Peão | Margem Crítica −2 | `CriticalServiceImpl` | no |
  | Rainha | +1PA | `ActionPointsServiceImpl` | no |
  | Rainha | Iniciativa +2 | `InitiativeService#getTotalInitiative(sheet, ctx)` | yes |
  | Bispo | Roubo de Vida 1 | `AttackDelivery` | yes |
  | Bispo | 2PV per Rodada | `Scene` (Rodada boundary, `HealingSource.subordinate()`) | yes |
  | Rei | 2 temporary Sorte/Autocontrole | received on command, again after each Descanso Longo | no |

### Invocations in a Scene

- **`Scene#addSummon` / `#addSummons`** — a summon becomes its own participant, controlled by its caster's player
  (table ruling, 2026-10-01).
  - It is placed right after its caster, behind any earlier summons, with the caster's Iniciativa and sub-group.
  - A pending caster's summon is pending too; an absent caster is refused.
  - A `SceneSummon` records it. It leaves the Scene:
    - when its Duração runs out, at a Rodada boundary;
    - at ≤0 PV (`#settleSummons`);
    - when its caster leaves;
    - at the end of the caster's Turn, once a newer summon of its exclusivity group replaces it.
  - One cast's summons never replace each other.
- **Concentração summons**: with no countdown while the caster concentrates; `Scene#breakConcentration(caster)`
  starts the trailing Rodadas (caller-reported: the caster cast or attacked).
- **`SummonSpawner`** (`Scene#addSummonSpawner`) — one summon now, then one at each Rodada boundary while it lasts.
- **`MonsterTemplate#resolveAttackEffectChains(self)`** — Correntes a stat block puts on every attack it makes;
  `AttackDelivery` chains them like a Talento's. A `MonsterSheet` exposes them as `getAttackEffectChains()`.

### ALIADOS DA NATUREZA

- **`monster.summon.NatureSummon`** — a `SummonedMonsterTemplate` over `NatureSummonKind`, covering Aliado da
  Natureza, Predador Regional, Experimento de Lacerto, Orgulho de Lacerto and the Anciente.
  - The Graduação tiers are folded in at build time:
    - Aliado: +5PV and Defesas +2 at 4; Força and Destreza +2 at 7;
    - Lacerto creatures: Força, Destreza and Vigor +2 at 7;
    - the GD reduction at 10 (−2 níveis for the Anciente).
  - Each kind has an attack bonus equal to the Graduação.
  - Each kind's attack is an authored Arma Natural on the Dano Base scale. It has no ½ Força or size term, since the
    engine adds those itself.
  - `NatureSummonAbility` carries the RD/RA clauses; `NatureSummonTrait` carries Membros Múltiplos' even-Rodada +1PA
    and the Anciente's immunity to Efeitos Críticos Menores.
  - The Anciente holds an open-ended `EnchantmentWard` (`EnchantmentWard.openEnded()`).
- **`LacertoPower`** — the six powers, rolled distinct at cast (table ruling).

  | Power | What it does |
  | --- | --- |
  | Inocular Veneno | A new `effect.InocularVeneno` Corrente: Envenenado until a Descanso Longo; the Orgulho's adds a second −1 until the combat ends and needs a Descanso Total. |
  | Devorar Inteiro | `DevorarInteiro`, through the Ogro's `DevourService`. |
  | Bruto | +1d6 as four Dano Base scale-ups; RD (Experimento) or RA and Vigor +3 (Orgulho). |
  | Membros Múltiplos | +1PA in even Rodadas. |
  | Sopro / Aura Elemental | Reported only. |
- **`magic.invocation.NatureInvocationService`** — what each Magia does once cast, returning an `Invocation`: the
  summons placed and the extra PM to pay.
  - `invokeAliado`: Predador Regional costs +1PM.
  - `singCancaoDeFlora`: +2PM per extra animal, +1PM per strengthened animal. Fauna Flora adds 1 Aliado per 3
    Graduações in Conhecimentos with Natureza.
  - `invokeExperimento`, `invokeOrgulho`, `invokeLaboratorio` (two Experimentos), and `awakenAnciente`.
  - `raiseTotem` (a spawner) and `blessFromTotem`: +1PA, Força +2, Vigor +2 and −1 nível for one Rodada, renewed
    rather than stacked.
  - `captivate` (Cativar Animal):
    - the target must be an animal, and the grade must be Cavaleiro or Torre;
    - Falsa Matilha makes it Prodigioso for 2 Rodadas;
    - the same animal can't be targeted again until a Descanso.

  | Exclusivity group | Members |
  | --- | --- |
  | Fauna | Aliados da Natureza and Canção de Flora |
  | Experimento | Experimento de Lacerto |
  | Orgulho | Orgulho de Lacerto and Laboratório de Lacerto |
  | none | Totem's Predadores, the Anciente |
- `ModifierType#SKILL_DIFFICULTY_REDUCTION` — níveis off every Perícia, read by `AbstractSkillInteraction`.
- `CreatureType.ANIMAL` for the Aliado and Predador; new messages `CAPTIVATE_REQUIRES_ANIMAL`,
  `CAPTIVATE_GRADE_NOT_ALLOWED`, `CAPTIVATE_NEEDS_REST`.

## Fixed

- `Scene#next()` resets its cursor to the top after the Rodada boundary, so a summon leaving at index 0 can't push it
  to −1.

## Readings

- ⚠️ A summon cast mid-Rodada acts right after its caster in that same Rodada.
- ⚠️ "Dano letal" means falling to ≤0 PV; a summon never reaches Coma.
- ⚠️ Canção de Flora's "dissipa a conjuração anterior" takes effect at the end of the caster's Turn, as Aliados'.
- ⚠️ Fauna Flora counts Conhecimentos Graduações only with the Natureza Especialização.
- ⚠️ Each of the Totem's Predadores lasts an Aliados da Natureza's 3 Rodadas.
- ⚠️ The Totem's Força/Vigor reach only Perícia rolls governed by them.
- ⚠️ Falsa Matilha halves 3 Rodadas to 2 (rounded up).
- ⚠️ The Anciente's stat block (DF +18/DM +21, Graduações) is taken over its Efeito's DF +20/DM +25 and "+20".
- ⚠️ A Lacerto creature's Devorar Inteiro uses the Ogro's Vigor-sized stomach.
- The Subordinado readings: a benefit is picked when gained; Peão's crit widens the margin by 2; Prodigioso reaches
  `SceneContext` allies.
