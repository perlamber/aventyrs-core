# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.0.102

**Habilidades de Competência, Phase 3** — limited uses and Reações (`docs/plans/skill-competency-wiring-plan.md`).

## New

- **`skill.CompetencyUses`** — spends a limited-use Habilidade's uses off the ledger its window names and grants what
  a use grants: `remaining`, `use`, plus Charme Feérico (`canCharm`/`charm`) and Instinto de Luther's allies' half
  (`allyInstinctAvailable`/`useAllyInstinct`). `restoreRestScopedUses(sheet, source, uses)` is how a client reloads
  persisted counts: its own keys renew on any Descanso Longo.
- **`skill.UseWindow`** — `ROUND`, `CENA` (⚠️ per combat, the house reading), `LONG_REST`.
- **Hooks on `SkillCompetencyAbility`**: `resolveUseLimit`, `getUseWindow`, `resolveActivationBlessings`,
  `resolveSkillRollActionPointAdjustment` (summed by `ActionPointsService#getSkillRollCost`).
- **`CombatantSheet#spendRoundScopedUse`/`getRoundScopedUses`** — "uma vez por Rodada", reset each Rodada and Cena.
- **`CombatantSheet#spendOrdinaryRestScopedUse`/`restoreOrdinaryRestScopedUses`** — a rest-scoped use that any
  Descanso renews (`clearRestCooldowns`), not only a Verdadeiro.
- **`StepRules#ignoringDifficultTerrainCost`** — difficult hexes priced as ordinary ground, still reported as difficult.
- **`ItemCategory.UTILITIES`** (`ItemType.UTILITY`) and **`item.UtilityItem`** with the **Kit de Primeiros Socorros**
  — the store's new Utilidades tab (table ruling). ⚠️ Its Preço (1) and Dureza (5) are placeholders.

## Habilidades now real

- **Salto Poderoso** — 1/2/3 uses per Cena (Atletismo 5ª/10ª). The movement that ignores Terreno Difícil is priced by
  the caller.
- **Instinto de Luther** — once per Cena, 1/2/3 extra Reações (Atenção 5ª/10ª) for the Rodada it is used in. From
  the 5ª Graduação each ally may take one extra Reação once per Cena.
- **Socorro Imediato** — 1/2/3 uses per Descanso Longo; a use is a Medicina e Cura roll as an Ação Livre (priced by
  the caller).
- **Bom Doutor** — −1PA on the first Medicina e Cura roll of each Rodada while a Kit de Primeiros Socorros is
  carried or worn.
- **Recuo Rápido** — once per Rodada; the caller offers the Reação-paid Reposicionar after an enemy's damaging hit.
- **Charme Feérico** — 2PD, once per creature per Cena (table ruling); the reroll is the caller's.
