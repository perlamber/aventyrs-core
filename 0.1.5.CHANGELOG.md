# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.1.5

**Condições e Malefícios, revised** — `docs/rules/condicoes-e-maleficios-.txt` was re-imported in full (the first
import was truncated), and the catalogue is rebuilt on its two layers: **Estados** (Desprevenido, Fraqueza,
Desacordado, Favorecido) and the **Condições** made of them. Plus a base **Agarrar** manoeuvre every character can
use. Rulings and plan: `docs/plans/conditions-revision-plan.md` (2026-10-07).

## Breaking

- **`ConditionType.DESARMADO` is gone.** Disarming (`CombatantSheet#disarm`) only takes the weapon away; a fighter
  with nothing in hand attacks with an Ataque Desarmado at its own Dano Base.
- **`ConditionType#getAttackerDamageBonus` / `CombatantSheet#getAttackerDamageBonusFromConditions` are gone.**
  Flanqueado's Favorecido is on the **attack roll** now: `CombatantSheet#getAttackerAttackRollBonus`.
  `DamageContributionSource.TARGET_CONDITION` is deprecated and never contributed.
- **Desprevenido is -4 Defesas** (was -2), `ConditionType.DESPREVENIDO_DEFENSE_MALUS` public.
- **Every Condição now reaches its numbers through Estados** (`getImplied`), and an Estado conferred twice applies
  once. Agarrado no longer taxes Perícias (it is Desprevenido); Caído is Desprevenido + Fraqueza (Fraqueza taxes
  every dano roll, not only melee); Amaldiçoado, Imobilizado and Cego are Desprevenido; Envenenado, Doente and
  Devorado are Fraqueza.
- **Fear bands**: Abalado Fraqueza@Curta; Assustado Fraqueza@Média + Desprevenido@Curta; Apavorado Fraqueza@Longa +
  Desprevenido@Média, and within Curta only fleeing is allowed. One rung at a time — a weaker fear never overrides a
  stronger. A sourceless fear (Pânico) is always in range.
- **Envenenado's -1 Multiplicador de PV moved onto its sources** (Espinhos Venenos de Gaea's retaliation, Inocular
  Veneno, Veneno Vampírico) as per-instance `Condition#getExtraEffects()`; a bare Envenenado is Fraqueza alone.
- **Cego's d6** is owed only on Força/Destreza Perícias, at ≤2 personal / ≤3 adjacent / ≤5 beyond adjacent
  (`BlindCheck.Reach`; ranged was ≤4). The old `BlindCheck.*_FAILURE` constants are gone.
- **Caído halves Movimento Base** (once, with Passos Reduzidos).
- **Foes see their own Condições**: a `MonsterSheet`'s GDs and Defesas now include its Estados.
- **Confuso adds +1PA** to attack/Perícia/charge/draw/Título-activation prices.

## New

- **Estados** `ConditionType.FRAQUEZA`, `DESACORDADO` (2 Rodadas, applies a Caído that outlives it); `isEstado()`,
  `getFearRank()`/`isFear()`, `getAttackerFavours()` (`AttackerFavour.ATTACK_ROLL` / `DEFENCE_AGAINST_HOLDER`),
  `halvesMovementBase()`, `refuses(ActionKind)`, `getRestrictionRange()`, `getActionPointSurcharge()`,
  `defendsUnlessCriticalFailure()`, `preventsResourceRecovery()`.
- **The action veto**: `action.ActionKind` and `CombatantSheet#refusalForAction`/`isActionPrevented` — Imobilizado,
  Desacordado and Petrificado refuse every Ação, Agarrado every Ação de Movimento, Confuso Ações Livres and Reações,
  Apavorado everything but fleeing within Curta. Enforced on rolls (`ACTION_PREVENTED_BY_CONDITION`), movement,
  Reposicionar, draws, activations, casting and Reação options. A defence is never refused.
- **Imobilizado/Desacordado defend**: their Esquiva e Aparar succeeds unless it is a Falha Crítica.
- **Caído/Cego attackers defend with Vantagem** against that target's attacks while it stays so —
  `CombatantSheet#favoursDefenceBy`, fed by `Scene#recordAttack` → `noteAttackedBy`.
- **Agarrar** — `GrappleService` (+`Impl`, `GrappleResult`): grab a foe (2PA, Ataque CaC vs DF, no damage/crit/
  Corrente), defend a foe's grab (Esquiva vs its Ataque GD), escape (2PA, Ataque vs the captor's Ataque GD), keep a
  struggling foe (Ataque vs its GD), escape Imobilização (Furtividade vs a preset GD or the captor's), release,
  release on the captor's movement. Free hand needed and held (`item.HandBudget`, now shared with
  `CharacterSheet`), ≤2 Categorias larger, no Agarrar without arms. A hold lapses when its captor is Caído,
  Imobilizado, Desacordado or ≤0 PV (`CombatantSheet#canMaintainGrapple`, `getGrappledTargets`).
  `Manoeuvre.AGARRAR`, `LIBERTAR_SE_DO_AGARRAO`, `MANTER_AGARRAO`, `LIBERTAR_SE_DA_IMOBILIZACAO`,
  `LIBERTAR_SE_DA_PREDACAO`, `LEVANTAR_SE`; `SkillRoll#asManoeuvre`; `sheet.Immobilization`.
- **Levantar-se** — `StandUpService` (+`Impl`): 1PA, lifts Caído, reports Defender o Perímetro reactors;
  `Feat#standsUpAsFreeAction` (Submissão).
- **Grapple Talentos**: Subjugar and Submissão's Vantagem to Agarrar, Herança Anfíbia's Vantagem to resist and escape.
- **Devorado escape** (`DevourService#getEscapeDifficulty`/`escapeAttack`/`recordEscapeHit`): GD = devourer's DF
  -2 níveis (min Médio), Arma Natural or Leve only, a single hit ≥ 2×Vigor frees (Bocarra keeps its running total),
  then Abalado + Desprevenido for 1 Rodada. Damage from inside is Meio-Dano; a Devorado is shielded from everyone but
  its devourer (`CombatantSheet#isShieldedFrom`, `TARGET_INSIDE_DEVOURER`).
- **For a client holding only an identity copy of a foe**: `GrappleService#grab(captor, target, int defense, …)`,
  `#defendGrab(…, int captorAttackGd, …)`, `#escape(…, int captorAttackGd)`, `#holdAgainst(…, int escapeeAttackGd, …)`
  and `#escapeImmobilization(…, int captorAttackGd)` take the foe's bestiary figure instead of reading a `MonsterSheet`.
- **Flanqueado from positions** — `scene.grid.Flanking`: max(2, 1 + Categoria) able enemies within reach, no gap
  wider than 180° around the target (⚠️ the angle is an inference). The client derives the Condição from its board.
- **Envenenado's continuous Dano Natural** — `sheet.Poisoning`.
- **Doente's spreading** — `sheet.Disease` + `DiseaseService` (+`Impl`); the infecting creature is immune.
- **Feridas Dolorosas** also stops PM/PD recovery.
- `CombatantSheet#getHeldConditions`, `getDevourer`, `getActionPointSurcharge`, `defendsUnlessCriticalFailure`;
  `ActionCost#plusSurcharge`; `Condition#getExtraEffects`/`noteAttackedBy`.

## Still open

- Visual Atenção auto-failing under Cego (a roll does not say what it is for); a non-attack Perícia "que afete
  outros personagens" is read as personal.
- PvP grapples (two players) have no opposed-roll resolution (`OPPOSED_ROLL_UNSUPPORTED`).
- Disease immunity covers only the infecting creature, not others of its kind (no template identity on a sheet).
- The Amaldiçoar Efeito Crítico's own -5/-2 now stacks with Amaldiçoado's Desprevenido -4.
- GorgonaFeat's "sempre Amaldiçoado" stays unwired (it would be permanent Desprevenido).
