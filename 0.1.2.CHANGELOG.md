# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.1.2

**Bruxo** — the fifth Título Aventyr, end to end in core (`docs/bruxo.md`). Table rulings 2026-10-05.

## New

- **`title.bruxo`** — `Bruxo`, `BruxoDespertar` (Contrato com Além), `BruxoSpecialization` (Iluminado, Oráculo
  Abissal), `BruxoAbility` (Familiar Maior, Invocação Maior, Invocação Dupla, Pacto de Conjuração), `IluminadoAbility`
  and `OraculoAbissalAbility` (four each). In `TitleCatalog#all()`; `getIdentity()` is `TitleIdentity.BRUXO`, so the
  Élfico Talentos' prohibition is real.
- **Misticismos are mimetized Magias.** `MisticismoTeacher`/`MisticismoFilter` say which traits teach an Árvore and
  which they admit (either half of a tree's tag; Arsenal Elemental for any element). `Bruxo#chooseMisticismo`,
  `#choosePactoTrees`, `#getPendingMisticismoTeachers`, `#getMisticismoOptions` record the picks; the rest is derived:
  `#getMisticismoCeiling` (Semente, Broto at 2, Muda at 5, Emergente at 7 Habilidades de Bruxo; Florescente on the two
  Pacto trees), `#getMisticismoCost` (0/1/2/3/5 PD), and `#getGrantedMimetizedSpells` — both ramificações.
- **`AventyrTitle#getGrantedMimetizedSpells`** — folded into `Character#getMimetizedSpells()` beside the Talentos'.
- **O Grande Bruxo** — `MimetizedSpellCastingService#cast(caster, spell, payWithHitPoints)` and
  `#resolveHitPointCost`: PV = 1 + PD while Bruxo is the Primário, once per Rodada, paid through `payWithVitality`
  (locked until a Descanso Verdadeiro), never down to 0 PV. `MimetizedSpellCastingResult#getHitPointsPaid`. Hooks
  `AventyrTitle#resolveMimicryHitPointCost`/`#consumeMimicryHitPointPayment`.
- **Summon enhancements** — `magic.invocation.SummonEnhancement` (persistable figures), `InvocationOptions`
  (Invocação Maior's +1PA / +2PD, Invocação Dupla, the Nevasca comet), `NatureInvocationService#enhance` and
  `AventyrTitle#resolveSummonEnhancement`. `InvocationPlan` gained `extraActionPoints`, `extraDeterminationCost`,
  `concentrationUpkeepMultiplier`, `enhancement` (the five-argument constructor remains). `NatureSummon#withEnhancement`,
  `#getEnhancement`, and a four-argument `restore`.
- **`magic.invocation.EnhancedSummonFeat`** — an `AbstractFeat` (`CRIATURA`) carrying the enhancement into the roll
  hooks: GD reductions (attack, Esquiva e Aparar), RDS, Margem Crítica, +1d6 and Cataclismo on a critical, damage bonus,
  attacks against DM, the Fogo/Sagrado retype, and adjacent-ally RM.
- **`Feat#resolveAdjacentAllyMagicReduction`**, scanned from the recipient by `DamageServiceImpl` on a Mágico hit.
- **`effect.FogoVivo`** — a Corrente applying Inflamar Menor.
- **Familiar Maior** — `Familiar`, `FamiliarTemplate` (Categoria -4, non-combatant), `Bruxo#performFamiliarRitual`
  (returns the Character rebuilt one permanent Ego point lower), `Bruxo#summonFamiliar` (token + permanent
  Subordinado), `Bruxo#getFamiliarLeash`, and a five-argument restoring constructor.
- **`EgoPointsService#sacrificePermanent`**, **`MonsterTemplate#isNonCombatant`** / `MonsterSheet#isNonCombatant`
  (refused by `AttackDelivery`), and messages `COMET_REQUIRES_OPEN_SKY`, `SUMMON_CANNOT_FIGHT`.

## Readings (unconfirmed)

- "Elemental: Natural" also admits a tree tagged Natural on its own. "Ar" is `ElementalType.VENTO`.
- Either Invocação Maior effect makes "uma Invocação Maior" (the adjacent RM); the ward needs both. Maldições are not
  warded: no Maldição classification exists.
- Invocação Dupla halves the Duração floored, never below 1 Rodada; it doubles the upkeep only of a concentrated plan.
- Mar do Sul's regeneration ticks on the creature's own Turn. Raios' "agir no Turno em que foram invocadas" needs
  nothing: a summon is already placed right after its caster.
- Raios' Sagrado replaces only an Elemental type, so it retypes only alongside Chamas do Norte (to a Mágico Sagrado hit).
- The ritual's permanent point comes off `EgoValue#variable`. The familiar's stat block is all-1 Atributos, no
  Perícias, no weapon; its creature type is free.
