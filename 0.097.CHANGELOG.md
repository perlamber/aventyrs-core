# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.0.97

**Closing the ALIADOS DA NATUREZA gaps.**

## New

- **`feat.CriaturaFeat`** (`FeatCategory.CRIATURA`, never catalogued) — a creature's stat-block traits in a Talento's
  shape. `MonsterTemplate#getFeats()` grants them (none by default).
  - `MEMBROS_MULTIPLOS`: "3PA para realizar 2 ataques com Desvantagem". The pair is priced like Combater com 2 Armas:
    the first costs 3PA, the second nothing, both at Desvantagem, any weapon.
  - `CriaturaFeat#pairOpened`.
  - `NatureSummon` holds it for the Aliado/Predador and a Membros Múltiplos roll.
- **`monster.summon.ElementalDischarge`** — `damageAt(rolled, distanceUd)` and `dealTo(…)` through `DamageService`.
  - `NatureSummon#breath(self)` is Sopro Elemental (Experimento: cone 4UD, 2d6 + 1 + ½ Vigor, −2/UD; Orgulho: cone
    6UD, 3d6, −1/UD; 3PA, Refrigeração 1).
  - `#aura()` is Aura Elemental (Experimento: 2 Físico Elemental; Orgulho: 1d6 Elemental).
  - Both Natural (table ruling).
- **`NatureSummon#regenerationFlat(self)`** — the Anciente's Benção da Regeneração flat (½ Vigor); the caller adds 1d6.

## Readings

- ⚠️ The breath's "reduzido em N a cada UD" counts past the first UD.
- ⚠️ The Orgulho's untyped "3d6 de Dano" is read as Mágico Elemental.
