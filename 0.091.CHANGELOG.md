# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.0.91

**Healing inversion** — the undead's half of the Anatomia de Morto-Vivo.

## New

- **`MonsterTemplate#invertsHealing(source)`** — a heal that damages instead. `CombatantSheet#heal` applies what it
  would have healed, the healer's bonus included, as raw damage. A `MonsterSheet` reads its stat block's.
  - `Zumbi`: "sofrem Danos de Magias Divinas que recuperam PV ao invés de se curarem".
- **`Race#resolveInvertedSpellHealing(spell, holder, roller)`** — the PV a hit's Magia gives back in place of its
  damage. `DamageInteraction` heals the target when the hit dealt nothing, and reports it as `resourceGainValue`.
  - `Vampiro`: "Magias Profanas … ao invés disso os curam em 1d6+Metade do Vigor".
- `DamageInteraction#withDiceRoller` — the dice a hit may throw. `AttackDelivery` and `AttackReceiver` pass the
  attack's own roller. With none, an inverted hit deals nothing and heals nothing.

## Readings

- ⚠️ A Divine heal inverted on the undead lands as raw damage, with no mitigation.
