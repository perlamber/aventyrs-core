# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.1.7

**Undead invocations and the Modelo Escolhido de Macária.** This release rolls up 0.1.6.22–0.1.6.27 (see each
changelog for detail). The plan and its table rulings are in `docs/plans/undead-invocation-plan.md`.

- **0.1.6.22** — Manual de Criação de Monstros v19 (20261013) imported, and its text fixes carried into the
  descriptions.
- **0.1.6.23** — `monster.UndeadAnatomy`: the Anatomia de Morto-Vivo. Every spell-raised undead takes it in place of
  the printed "Menor".
- **0.1.6.24** — Corpses: `RaiseDeadService` and `Scene#raise`. A body may be raised at most 4 times, and a
  corpse-raised undead leaves its body when it goes. The Foco limit on invocações is `SummonLimit`.
- **0.1.6.25** — The REANIMAR tree: `UndeadInvocationService` (Reanimar, Servo Cadavérico, Criar Carniçal/Açougueiro,
  Festim dos Mortos), `Carnical`, `Abantesma`, rule-driven `SummonSpawner`s and uncontrolled summons.
- **0.1.6.26** — Modelo Escolhido de Macária, `MonsterSummoning` and the stock `CarrascoMorimbundo`.
- **0.1.6.27** — A creature's anatomy lives on every sheet (`MonsterSheet#transferAnatomyTo`).

The version moves to 0.1.7 at the user's call (2026-10-14).
