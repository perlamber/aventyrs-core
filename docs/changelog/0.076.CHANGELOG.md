# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.0.76

**Ego spending — Phase 0: pool rework (all four Egos).** Plan in `docs/plans/ego-spending-plan.md`.

## New

- **Received points and extras.** `CombatantSheet#receiveTemporaryEgoPoints(domain, source, amount)` — a
  received point refills spent temporaries first; the remainder is held as an **extra** above the ceiling,
  spent first and never recovered. `#receiveNonCumulativeTemporaryEgoPoints` — nothing while that source still
  holds its extra (Destino Favorável's "não cumulativo").
- `CombatantSheet#getExtraTemporaryEgoPoints`, `#getEgoOverflowReceived`, `#restoreExtraTemporaryEgoPoints(domain,
  heldExtras, overflowReceived)` — what a consumer rebuilding sheets persists and hands back (before any other
  Ego read).
- **No Ego above 5.** `Character.MAX_EGO`, `Character#getEgoOverflow(domain)`. `getEffectiveEgoTotal` is capped
  at 5 (pool, Iniciativa, Moral Herdada's Fama); the overflow is received once as extras.
- `EgoPointsService#grantTemporaryByNarrator(sheet, domain, amount)` (a received point, source
  `EgoPointsService.NARRATOR_GRANT`) and `#grantPermanentByNarrator(character, domain, amount)` (returns the
  rebuilt `Character`, `+amount` variable).

## Changed

- `getTemporaryEgoPoints` = points under the ceiling **plus held extras**, so it may exceed
  `getMaxTemporaryEgoPoints` (now the ceiling only: permanent remaining − penalties). The spent count under the
  ceiling is `max − (temporary − extras)`.
- `DelayedEgoGrant` delivery, Estabilidade Emocional and the Antecedente depletion grants now *receive* their
  points. Destino Favorável receives non-cumulatively instead of raising the ceiling.
- Ego loans (Transferência) are held as an extra: a still-held one goes back to its lender at the end of the Cena;
  a spent one is gone. No refund step any more.

## Removed

- `CombatantSheet#grantTemporaryEgoPointBonus` and `#grantTemporaryEgoPoints` (a persistent per-source ceiling
  widening — a spent granted point came back on the next recovery). Use `receiveTemporaryEgoPoints` /
  `receiveNonCumulativeTemporaryEgoPoints`.

## Rulings / readings

- Table rulings (2026-09-29): no Ego above 5, overflow as extras; a received point refills then becomes a
  consumed extra; session points only refill; the Narrador may grant temporary or permanent points any time.
- Reading: a non-cumulative receive still refills when the pool is short; the cap is on held extras only.
- Reading: a lowered overflow (a Talento lost) doesn't claw back held extras.

## Consumer notes

- The client's `temporaryEgoSpent = max − temporary` goes negative when extras are held; switch it to
  `max − (temporary − extras)` and persist extras + overflow mark when moving to 0.0.76 (plan Phase 5).
