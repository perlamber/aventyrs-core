# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.0.68

**Título, Destino and Vampiro relational systems** (plan Phase H). The "Despertar timeline" turned
out to need no timeline:
- "adquirido antes de Despertar" is an acquisition-time ceiling;
- an EXP mark is read when session-end acquisitions run.

Centelhas are now a count a Regalia donor really loses. Talentos can opt into a Habilidade de
Título's activation. Vampiros carry their Laços-de-Sangue.
Feat TODO lines: 240 → 229.

## New

- `FeatRequirements#maximumAwakenedTitles` and `#maximumCentelhas`, two acquisition-time ceilings.
- `Feat.meetsRequirements(requirements, character, sheet)`: the data check, for an `isEligible`
  override that adds its own clause.
- **Centelhas:**
  - `Character#getCentelhas()` (default `Character.CENTELHAS` = 3) and `#sacrificeCentelhas(n)`.
  - `RegaliaDonation#willingDonor(Character)` / the `donor` component. At the forge, a named donor
    with no Centelha is refused (`REGALIA_DONOR_LACKS_CENTELHA`); otherwise they give up one
    Centelha (Menor) or all of them (Superior).
- `Feat#resolveSessionEndAcquisitions(Character, CharacterSheet)`, which
  `CharacterSheet#applySessionEndAcquisitions` now calls.
- `feat.CentelhaGranAventyrAntecipadaFeat`: the chosen Título Secundário, awakened at a session
  end once total EXP reaches 23.
- **Título activation opt-ins:**
  - `TitleAbilityActivationRequest#activatedFeats`;
  - `Feat` hooks `resolveTitleActivationSurcharge`, `resolveTitleActivationActionPointReduction`,
    `resolveTitleAbilityDurationIncrease`, `permitsTitleActivationOptIn`;
  - `InteractionResult#titleAbilityDurationIncrease`;
  - message `TITLE_ACTIVATION_OPT_IN_EXHAUSTED`.
- `Feat#resolveDifficultyReduction(SkillType, Character, SceneContext)`, for opponent-scoped GD
  clauses. `AbstractSkillInteraction` passes the context.
- **Laços-de-Sangue:**
  - `Vampiro#getSireId()` and a constructor that takes it;
  - `character.services.BloodBondService` (+`Impl`): `getMaster`, `isBoundTo`, `canSireProgeny`,
    `sire`;
  - message `PROGENY_NOT_PERMITTED`.
- **Presença de Carmilla:** `sheet.CarmillaPresence`, and `character.services.VampiricPresenceService`
  (+`Impl`) `#drain`.
- `VampiricoFeat#isPoderVampirico()`.
- `CombatantSheet#hasActiveEffect(Class)`.

## Now real

- **Atrasar Despertar and Abdicador:**
  - `DestinoFeat#ATRASAR_DESPERTAR`: (1 + 1 per 15 EXP) × Títulos not Desperto, on every Perícia
    roll, every dano roll, RDS and Margem Crítica Menor. It must be taken before any Título awakens.
  - `DestinoFeat#ABDICADOR`: doubles that figure.
- **Centelhas:**
  - `DestinoFeat#FRAGMENTO_DA_ENCARNACAO_DE_GILGAMESH`: +2 to every Atributo per Centelha not
    possessed, or per possessed one withheld under Atrasar Despertar. Its disjunctive
    Pré-requisito is enforced.
  - `DestinoFeat#CENTELHA_GRAN_AVENTYR_ANTECIPADA`: the 23 EXP awakening above.
- **Título opt-ins:**
  - `DestinoFeat#ACELERAR_HABILIDADE`: −1PA for +2PD, once per Turn.
  - `DestinoFeat#CENTELHA_DURADOURA`: +2PD, with +2 Unidades of Duração reported.
- **Vampírico:**
  - `VampiricoFeat#LACOS_ROMPIDOS`: severs the bond, plus a Vantagem on Carisma/Atenção rolls
    against another Vampiro.
  - `VampiricoFeat#MESTRE_VAMPIRO`: that Vantagem against anyone; −1 nível of GD against Vampiros;
    gerar Prole.
  - `VampiricoFeat#PRESENCA_DE_CARMILLA`: now a Poder Vampírico. Its per-Rodada drain is
    `VampiricPresenceService#drain`.
  - `VampiricoFeat#PODER_VAMPIRICO_DURADOURO`: counts exactly "2 outros" Poderes, no longer the
    whole tree.

## Fixed

- `DevourServiceImpl` (0.0.67) reported Devoratriz's recovery as the remaining damage rather than
  the PV recovered: `CombatantSheet#heal` returns the former.

## Readings

- ⚠️ An opted-in Acelerar/Centelha Duradoura surcharge is paid before Benção de Boros' doubling,
  so it doubles too.
- ⚠️ Without Atrasar Despertar, an un-awakened Centelha is not "voluntarily" withheld.
- ⚠️ The Secundário awakens at the next reported session end, not the instant 23 EXP lands.
- ⚠️ A Prole takes its master's linhagem.
- ⚠️ "Baseadas em Carisma e Atenção" means a Carisma-governed Perícia, or Atenção.
- ⚠️ A foe with no Raça counts as living for Presença de Carmilla.

## Not built

- `DestinoFeat#FAVORITISMO_MAIOR`: "Favoritismo" is defined in no rules document in this repo.
- Abdicador's "não Desperta" is not enforced on `grantTitle`.
- Acelerar's "apenas em seu Turno" is not checked.
- A Habilidade's own Duração is not extended; the increase is reported only.
- Creating a Prole's `Character`.

## Callers

- **Regalia:** pass `RegaliaDonation.willingDonor(donorCharacter)` to have the forge take the
  Centelhas.
- **Título opt-ins:** name the Talentos on `TitleAbilityActivationRequest.builder().activatedFeat(...)`,
  and add `getTitleAbilityDurationIncrease()` to the Habilidade's Duração.
- **Presença de Carmilla:** call `VampiricPresenceService#drain(vampire, ownContext)` at the
  Vampiro's Turn start.
- **Prole:** `BloodBondService#sire(master, parentRace)` returns the Raça to build the Prole with.
