# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.1.6.19

**The attack manoeuvres.** This is Phase 6 of `docs/plans/acoes-de-combate-plan.md`, from "Manobras de Combate –
Ações" (20261013): Encontrão, Atacar Objetos / Desarmar, Imobilizar, Postura Defensiva, the Investida's line and
minimum distance, Agarrar's Categoria bonus, the push Rugido used to only report, and the Rendido Condição.

## Rulings (table, 2026-10-14)

- **Postura Defensiva:**
  - +2 Defesas, or +3 while using an Escudo (Asas Adamantinas count while not flying).
  - It lasts until the holder's own next Turn begins.
  - The Escudo is checked live, and taking the posture again renews it.
- **Rendido:**
  - A voluntary Condição that carries Desprevenido.
  - It ends when its holder takes any action other than a defence.
  - It is not a Malefício.
- **Imobilizar** accepts a Rendido or Inconsciente (fallen/coma) target.

## Readings (approved with the plan, or flagged here)

- Encontrão deals no damage.
- Agarrar's bonus is the captor's own Categoria.
- A push stops short at an obstacle, an occupant, a steep rise or the board's edge; it falls off a ledge, and it ends
  swimming in water.
- ⚠️ Atacar Objetos' "GD +1 nível" on a flat Defesa is +5 (`ObjectAttackService.LEVEL_STEP`, Difícil less Médio).

## Added

- **`sheet.DefensiveStance`** and **`character.services.DefensiveStanceService`** (2PA):
  - `DefenseService` adds the bonus, and so does a fixed stat-block foe's `MonsterSheet#getDefense`.
- **`ConditionType.RENDIDO`**, lifted by `CombatantSheet#recordAction`.
- **`character.services.ImmobilizeService`** (3PA): adjacent, Rendido or Inconsciente, and
  `Immobilization.byCaptor`.
- **`ChargeService#getMinimumDistance`**: the size table's Movimento Base.
- **`ChargeService#refusalForLine`**: `CHARGE_TOO_SHORT`, or `CHARGE_LINE_BLOCKED` via `MovementMap#isLineClear`.
- **`character.services.ShoveChargeService`** (Encontrão, `Manoeuvre.ENCONTRAO`), with the Investida's price:
  - an Ataque Corpo-a-Corpo with no damage;
  - a hit pushes 1UD, or 2UD on a critical;
  - Lançar ao Chão lands at the Corrente margin;
  - a miss gives the Investida's −2 Defesas.
- **`character.services.ForcedMovementService`** + **`HexGrid#beyond`**: a straight push and its `PushResult`.
- **`character.services.ObjectAttackService`** (Atacar Objetos, `Manoeuvre.ATACAR_OBJETOS`, 2PA):
  - the roll is against the Defesa + 5;
  - the damage is halved, then goes through `Item#applyDamage`;
  - Desarmar lands at the Corrente margin.
- **Agarrar** adds the captor's Categoria de Tamanho to its grab, in both directions.
- **`MovementReactionService#getMeleeReachUd(sheet)`** gives the melee reach in UD of what a combatant holds ready.
  A `getRouteCheckpoints` overload takes `knownReachUd` for stand-ins that carry no weapons, so a client judges a
  foe another client owns by the reach that client advertised.
- **`TranslatableMessages`**: `IMMOBILIZE_TARGET_NOT_HELPLESS`, `TARGET_NOT_ADJACENT`, `CHARGE_TOO_SHORT`,
  `CHARGE_LINE_BLOCKED`, `ITEM_NOT_EQUIPPED`.
