# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.0.100

**Habilidades de Competência, Phase 1** — the combat hooks of `docs/plans/skill-competency-wiring-plan.md`.

## New hooks on `SkillCompetencyAbility`

- **`resolveDamageBonus(skillType, ctx, target, actor, attackSource, holder)`** — the longest form; the 4-arg one now
  delegates to it, and every override moved onto it (Frieza, Brutalidade, both Goblin traits).
- **`resolveConditionalRollBonus(skillType, ctx, requestedAbility, holder)`** — likewise; Filhos da Montanha and the
  Goblin trait moved onto it. The Perícia rolled and the holder's sheet (its Condições) are now visible.
- **`resolveCriticalDamage(attackSkill, holder)`** — summed by `CriticalService#getCriticalDamage` on top of the
  baseline Vantagem.
- **`resolveCriticalHitEffects(attackSkill, attacker)`** — chained by `AttackDelivery` behind a critical hit, the
  twin of `Feat#resolveCriticalHitEffectChains`.
- **`resolveAttackActionPointAdjustment(attackSkill, holder)`** — summed by `ActionPointsService#getAttackCost`.
- **`onActionRecorded(holder, action)`** — told of every action `CombatantSheet#recordAction` files.

## Habilidades now real

- **Mirar na Cabeça** — +2 (a Vantagem) on an Ataque à Distância's critical dano. ⚠️ Stacks with the baseline
  Vantagem as +2, per the Perito ruling ("Vantagem adicional = +2").
- **Abrir Defesas** — a critical Ataque Corpo-a-Corpo hit chains `effect.AbrirDefesas`: Desprevenido for 1 Rodada.
  Not a catalog Efeito Crítico, so immunities to those don't stop it.
- **Acuidade's price** — Desvantagem on an Ataque Corpo-a-Corpo's dano with a `HEAVY` weapon. ⚠️ Whenever held, as
  the substitution itself is.
- **Morte Oculta** — +2 dano on every attack while Escondido, +3 from the 5ª and +4 from the 10ª Graduação in
  Furtividade. Traps stay the Narrador's.
- **Ação Surpresa** — Vantagem on every Perícia roll while Escondido in a Combat Scene.
- **Fintar Aprimorado, whole** — the Finta's Vantagem and a −1PA on the attack it sets up both end with the first
  Perícia de Ataque roll (`onActionRecorded`), within the Blessings' own 1 Rodada.
