# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.0.85

**Correntes de Efeitos.** Every generic Corrente in `docs/rules/efeitos-criticos.txt` is now an `effect.EffectChain`.
Four already existed (Definhar, Golpe Trovejante, Rugido, plus Sobrecura); ten are new.

## New

- **Correntes** (`org.aventyrs.core.effect`):
  - `EnrijecerMusculatura` — Desvantagem on Força/Destreza Perícia rolls for 1 Rodada.
  - `EscancararDefesas` — the attacker's next attack on that target is 1 nível easier.
  - `Excomungar` — removes one running Maldição or Encantamento.
  - `ExplosaoCataclismica` — reports a 1 UD Explosão and adds Cataclismo to the critical.
  - `FeridaInfecciosa` — 3 extra damage that only a Descanso heals.
  - `MagicaeMortis` — Roubo de Vida of 1 + the caster's Títulos Arcanos/Abençoados.
  - `Oprimir` — Roubo de Determinação 1, or 2 on a critical.
  - `RemoverAflicao` — the target heals 2PV.
  - `ToqueSombrio` — +1 damage.
  - `VenenoVampirico` — Envenenado, 2 damage at once and 2 per Rodada for 2 Rodadas, Roubo de Vida 1
    (`sheet.VampiricVenom` carries the ticks).
- `ModifierType#PHYSICAL_SKILL_ROLL_BONUS` — read only on Força/Destreza Perícia rolls and on a foe's matching GD,
  never on the Atributo.
- `CombatantSheet#lockDamageUntilRest` / `#getRestLockedDamage` — damage only a Descanso (any tier) heals; kept
  apart from `getLockedDamage`.
- `CombatantSheet#openDefensesOf` / `#hasOpenedDefensesOf` — held by the attacker, read by its next Perícia de
  Ataque roll against that target, spent by `recordAction` once an attack naming the target is recorded, cleared
  at `startNewScene`.
- `InteractionResult#getTriggeredAreaOfEffect` — an Área de Efeito a stage reports for the caller to apply.
- `Feat#resolveEffectChains(attacker, skill, source, holder, sceneContext, criticalResult)` — the longest form,
  defaulting to the shorter; `AttackDelivery` now asks this one.

## Wired

- `GorgonaFeat#MARCA_DA_MALDICAO` — its Presas Longas carry Enrijecer Musculatura. ⚠️ It now grants the Presas
  Longas itself (the Forma Monstruosa it never leaves).
- `FeralFeat#DESPREZO_NATURAL` — its Armas Naturais carry Ferida Infecciosa.
- `SobrevivenciaFeat#MESTRE_DE_CACA` — in the chosen terrain its attacks carry Oprimir.
- `MetamorfoseDraculeaFeat` — Serpente Espinhosa's Cauda Constritora carries Veneno Vampírico while worn.
- `AttackDelivery` — a triggered Explosão Cataclísmica adds Cataclismo to the critical (applied or reported).

## Rulings / readings

- Table rulings (2026-10-01):
  - Roubo de Determinação: the target loses the PD and the attacker recovers what was taken.
  - Ferida Infecciosa: any Descanso heals it, nothing else does.
  - Excomungar: the caster picks; otherwise the most recent goes.
- ⚠️ A Corrente's extra damage (Rugido, Ferida Infecciosa, Toque Sombrio, Veneno Vampírico) lands after mitigation.
- ⚠️ Veneno Vampírico's Roubo de Vida 1 applies to each of its hits, the first and every tick.
- ⚠️ Excomungar does not check "Naturais".

## Still open

- Toque Sombrio's Profano retyping — there is no Profano damage type.
- Consumers not in core: the Devoto Talentos (see `docs/plans/devoto-tiers-plan.md`) and Magicae Mortis.
- `AssassinoFeat#GOLPE_SOBRENATURAL` and `ElementalFeat#GOLPE_CATACLISMICO` — blocked on a DF→DM switch and Gana
  Elemental's activation.

## API and client

- aventyrs-api and the client carry the rest-only lock as `restLockedHitPoints`: on the status frame, on the stored
  sheet and on the sheet response, seeded back with `lockDamageUntilRest`. `null` on a frame means "not reported".
