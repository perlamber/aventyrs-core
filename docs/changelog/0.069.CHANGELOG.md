# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.0.69

**Montaria and veículo** (plan Phase G, the last planned phase). "Enquanto estiver montado ou
dirigindo" is now a state on the rider's sheet. The rules' base restriction and Ginete apply to the
roll, and all five Cavalaria Talentos are real.
Feat TODO lines: 229 → 222.

## New

- **The riding state:**
  - `sheet.Riding`: a `MONTARIA` or `VEICULO`, plus the steed's `CombatantSheet` when it has one.
    Factories `montaria(steed)` and `veiculo()`.
  - `CombatantSheet#getRiding()`, `isRiding()`, `startRiding`, `stopRiding`.
- `character.services.MountService` (+`Impl`):
  - `mount` and `dismount(rider, asReaction)`, which return the `ActionCost`;
  - `getMountMovementActionPointCost`, `getMountActionAllowance`, `mayInterruptMountMovement`;
  - `DEFAULT_MOUNT_COST` (1PA) and `COMBAT_MOUNT_MOVEMENT_COST` (2).
- **Hooks:**
  - `SkillCompetencyAbility#resolveGoverningAttributeRollBonus(domain, holder)`, summed by
    `AbstractSkillInteraction`;
  - `Feat#resolveAdditionalTargets(SkillType, Character, CombatantSheet, AttackSource)`;
  - `Feat#halvesEveryTargetDamage`;
  - `AttackTargetingService#getMaximumTargets(CombatantSheet, SkillType, AttackSource)`, which
    `AttackDelivery` now enforces;
  - `AttackTargetingService#halvesEveryTarget`.
- `DeliveredAttackResult#everyTargetHalved`.
- `Weapon#getMountedDamageBase()`, and `DamageBaseService#getDamageBase(CombatantSheet, Weapon)`.
- Messages: `ALREADY_RIDING`, `NOT_RIDING`, `MOUNT_REACTION_NOT_PERMITTED`.

## Changed

- `CombatantSheet#isSkillUsePrevented` refuses every Perícia but Dirigir e Cavalgar while riding,
  unless the rider holds Ginete: "a restrição de não usar outras Perícias enquanto dirigindo um
  veículo ou cavalgando um animal". Nothing sets the riding state automatically, so existing
  callers are unaffected.

## Now real

- `DirigirECavalgarCompetencyAbility#GINETE`: lifts the restriction; Desvantagem on Força/Destreza
  rolls while riding.
- `CavalariaFeat#GRANDE_GINETE`: that Desvantagem becomes Vantagem.
- `CavalariaFeat#ATAQUE_EM_ARCO`: while riding, one more target on a non-Magia Ataque Corpo-a-Corpo,
  and Meio-Dano on every target, the primary included.
- `CavalariaFeat#MONTAR_E_DESMONTAR_INSTANTANEO`: montar/desmontar as an Ação Livre, or desmontar as
  a Reação, once per Turn.
- `CavalariaFeat#MONTARIA_DE_COMBATE`: a movement costs the mount 2PA, leaving min(Títulos, PA left)
  other actions.
- `CavalariaFeat#DIRECAO_CAOTICA`: the permission to interrupt the mount's movement.
- `SpearItem#ALABARDA_OU_NAGINATA`: Dano Base 2d6 while mounted.

## Readings

- ⚠️ Montar/desmontar costs 1PA without the Talento. No rules text states a figure.
- ⚠️ Without Montaria de Combate, a movement consumes all of the mount's PA ("consomem apenas 2PA").
- ⚠️ "Uma vez a cada Rodada" is counted per Turn.
- ⚠️ Ataque em Arco halves every target only when an extra target is actually named.
- ⚠️ "Apenas ataques físicos" is read as "not made with a Magia".

## Not built

- A Magia acting as a mount or granting Ginete (Mula sem Cabeça, Montaria na Manga).
- Splitting a movement around an action: movement in progress is not tracked.
- The "Veículos Tecnológicos" distinction.
- "Apenas em seu Turno"-style checks, since the request says nothing about whose Turn it is.

## Callers

- Mount with `MountService#mount(rider, Riding.montaria(steedSheet))` or `Riding.veiculo()`, and
  charge the returned `ActionCost`.
- Price the mount's movement with `getMountMovementActionPointCost`.
- Ask `getDamageBase(wielderSheet, weapon)` rather than the `Character` overload when a sheet is at
  hand.
- For Ataque em Arco, name the extra target on `DeliveredAttack#additionalTargets`. The targets'
  adjacency is the caller's to check.
