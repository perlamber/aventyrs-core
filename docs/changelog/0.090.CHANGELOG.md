# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.0.90

**A Raça's own immunities.**

## New

- **Hooks on `Race`:**
  - `getDamageImmunities()` — by `DamageScope`, honoured by `CombatantSheet#isImmuneToDamage`.
  - `isImmuneToSpell(spell)` — read through the new `CombatantSheet#isImmuneToSpell`, which also covers the Talentos'
    `Feat#isImmuneToSpell`. `DamageService` and `SpellCastingService` now ask the sheet.
  - `refusesHealing(source)` — read by `CombatantSheet#heal`.
  - `isImmuneToLifeStealFrom(thief)` — read through `character.services.LifeStealGuard` by `RouboDeVida`,
    `OferendaMaldita`, `MagicaeMortis` and `VampiricVenom`.
- Each racial immunity goes silent while a Forma suppresses innate racial traits.
- **`Vampiro`** (Anatomia de Morto-Vivo):
  - "imunes a efeitos Naturais" — ⚠️ read as damage of Elemental: Natural;
  - "Magias Profanas não causam nenhum dano";
  - "não podem recuperar PV com magias Divinas".
- **`NascidoDaFloresta`** (Imunidade a Magias): immune to every Magia but Primordial and Umbral, and to Encantamentos.
- **`Troll`** (Anatomia Vegetal): immune to Roubo de Vida from anyone who is not a Troll.

## Still open

- A Vampiro "curado com Magia Profana" — the heal in place of the damage needs dice and an inversion stage.
- Dólos' Forma do Vendaval — an activated state, not a standing immunity.
