# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.1.6.18

**The movement manoeuvres.** This is Phase 5 of `docs/plans/acoes-de-combate-plan.md`: Correr, Escalar, Nadar, Saltar,
Recuo Defensivo and taking off (Voar) from "Ações de Combate – Movimentos" (20261013). Reposicionar now follows the
doc's distance.

## Rulings

- **Reposicionar** moves "metade da Categoria de Tamanho do Personagem (mínimo 1UD)". This replaces the 2026-09-23
  flat-1UD table ruling.
- **Recuo Defensivo** (table, 2026-10-14): no step may come closer to any enemy within Curta, and the end must be
  farther from each of them.
- **Readings approved with the plan:**
  - "+2UD para cada GD superada" counts beating Médio.
  - Nadar's exemption is a Natação Movimento Base.

## Added

- **`Manoeuvre`**: `CORRER`, `ESCALAR`, `NADAR`, `SALTAR`, `RECUO_DEFENSIVO`, `VOAR`, all `ActionKind.MOVEMENT`.
- **`character.services.MovementManoeuvreService`** (+`Impl`):
  - `MANOEUVRE_COST` is 2PA.
  - `refusal`:
    - movement prevented;
    - Reposicionar taken this Turn;
    - Correr in Terreno Difícil;
    - a second Recuo this Rodada;
    - taking off with no flight.
  - `requiresRoll`: Correr and Saltar always; Escalar and Nadar unless the Vertical or Natação mode.
  - `resolve(…)` returns a `MovementOutcome`:
    - Correr: 2×MB, +2UD per level from Médio.
    - Saltar: MB/2, +2UD per level.
    - Escalar/Nadar: MB/2. A failure by ≤5 doesn't move; by more, it falls or submerges.
    - Recuo: MB/2.
    - A Falha Crítica on Correr or Saltar stumbles a caller-random UD, then Caído.
  - `isRetreatPathAllowed`.
  - `isExposed`: Desprevenido while climbing or swimming under threat, unless the mode.
- **`MovementReactionService#getRouteCheckpoints`**: Correr clamps every reach to adjacent, and Recuo Defensivo
  provokes nothing.
- **`CombatantSheet#getRetreatsTakenThisRound` / `#consumeRetreatThisRound`**, reset as the holder's Turn begins.
- **`RepositionServiceImpl.getBaseDistance(sheet)`**: max(1, Categoria/2).
- **`TranslatableMessages`**: `MOVEMENT_AFTER_REPOSITION`, `RUN_IN_DIFFICULT_TERRAIN`, `RETREAT_ALREADY_TAKEN`,
  `CANNOT_FLY`, `MOVEMENT_PREVENTED`.
