# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.1.5.6

**The GM grants Subordinados** — from a menu in the Cena, to characters and monsters, as many as they want.
Rulings: table, 2026-10-08 (plan in the invocations memory).

## Added

- **`SubordinateService#grant(commander, benefit, prodigious, ctx)`** — a GM grant. No Carisma limit and no
  one-per-grade rule; no Duração, until dismissed (`dismiss`). Source `SubordinateService.GM_GRANT` ("Mestre").
  A Rei's Ego points land as with `command`. Rule sources keep `command`'s checks.
- **`SubordinateService#exceedsLimits(commander, benefit, prodigious)`** — whether `command` would refuse it; the GM's
  menu warns with it.
- **Stable Subordinado ids** — `new Subordinate(UUID id, …)` and `Subordinate.sustained(UUID id, …)` restore one under
  the id it was first held with, so a client can dismiss the one another client commands. A `null` id is a new one.
- **`SubordinateBenefits#sameKind(sheet, ally)`**.
- **`SubordinateService#shareKingsEgo(ally, king)`** — a Prodigioso Rei commanded on another client gives this client's
  ally its points (non-cumulative), as `command` does for the allies in its context.
- **`SubordinateService#renewAfterLongRest(sheet, ctx)`** — also renews what each ally's Prodigioso Rei gives sheet.
  The one-argument form is now a default method delegating with `null` (what `RestServiceImpl` still calls).

## Changed

- ⚠️ **A Prodigioso Subordinado reaches allies of its commander's kind**: a character's reaches the group's
  characters (as before), a monster's the group's monsters.
- ⚠️ **A Rei's Ego points are non-cumulative per Rei** ("apenas uma vez a cada dia") —
  `receiveNonCumulativeTemporaryEgoPoints`. A Descanso Longo before they are spent tops them back to 2, no further.
