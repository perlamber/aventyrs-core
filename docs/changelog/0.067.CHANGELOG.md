# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.0.67

**Racial traits that Talentos extend** (plan Phase I). Six Características Raciais were blocking
the Talentos built on them. All six are now real, and so are the Talentos that grant, veto, mimic
or widen them. Three races' stale "no Arma Natural catalog" notes were also closed.
Feat TODO lines: 256 → 240.

## New

- **Ferocidade de Lacerto:**
  - `sheet.LacertoFerocity` grants RD, and Vantagem em Ataque Corpo-a-Corpo e Danos, through
    `getTemporaryBonus`.
  - `CombatantSheet#getLacertoFerocities` / `isFerocious` / `endNaturalLacertoFerocity`.
  - `character.services.LacertoFerocityService` (+`Impl`):
    - `getFerocityRound`;
    - `refresh`, called at the holder's Turn start;
    - `decline`, which spends 1 temporary Autocontrole and keeps the state off for the combat;
    - `mimic`.
  - Hooks: `Race#resolveLacertoFerocityRound`, `Feat#resolveLacertoFerocityRound`,
    `Feat#preventsEnteringLacertoFerocity`.
- **Bocarra:**
  - `character.services.DevourService` (+`Impl`):
    - `declareBite`: 1PD, and reports +1PA, the Vantagem and the Corrente;
    - `swallow`, `digest`, `recordDamageFromInside`, `regurgitate`;
    - capacity, Vigor per victim, digestion damage and hours, regurgitation PA.
  - `effect.DevorarInteiro`, the Corrente.
  - `sheet.Devoured`: a `DEVORADO` that names its captor.
  - `CombatantSheet#getDevouredVictims` / `addDevouredVictim` / `removeDevouredVictim` /
    `getDevoured`.
- **Agnação Ancestral:**
  - `character.services.AncestralCounselService` (+`Impl`):
    - `perform`: spends 3PM outside combat and banks a counsel;
    - `spend`.
  - `SkillRoll#counselled()` / `isCounselled()`. A counselled roll is one nível easier, reads as an
    Especialista's, and drops the untrained penalty. It is refused (`ANCESTRAL_COUNSEL_NOT_BANKED`)
    when no counsel is banked.
- **Aprendizado Rápido:**
  - `Character#quickLearningSkills`, a creation-time pick.
  - `Race#hasQuickLearning()`, set for Humano, Pequenino, Gnomo and Goblin.
  - `Feat#resolveQuickLearningMaxGraduation`.
  - `SkillGraduationService#getUpgradeCost(Character, SkillType)`, which `upgradeGraduation` now
    spends. It takes 0.5 EXP off the 2nd and 3rd Graduação, or further when a Talento extends it.
- **Olhar de Lacerto:**
  - `race.OlharDeLacerto`, an `AttackSource`.
  - `character.services.OlharDeLacertoService` (+`Impl`):
    - `declare`: 1PD; reports 2PA, reach, dano and the Olhar Petrificador;
    - `applyHit`: −2PA for 2 Rodadas, plus petrification in Forma Monstruosa, permanent at 0 PV.
  - `ConditionType#PETRIFICADO`, applied as `sheet.Petrification`. It is an Encantamento, **not a
    Malefício**.
- **Cuidado para não Quebrar:** `Race#resolveGoverningAttributeRollBonus`, a roll bonus scoped by
  the roll's governing Atributo. It is silenced under physical-trait suppression.
- `Race#getGrantedNaturalWeapons` for `Ogro` (Presas Longas), and for `Indomito` and `Aviano`
  (Garras Afiadas).
- Messages:
  - `LACERTO_FEROCITY_NOT_HELD`, `LACERTO_FEROCITY_MIMIC_NOT_GRANTED`,
    `LACERTO_FEROCITY_MIMIC_TOO_EARLY`, `LACERTO_FEROCITY_MIMIC_ALREADY_USED`;
  - `BOCARRA_NOT_HELD`;
  - `ANCESTRAL_COUNSEL_NOT_BANKED`, `ANCESTRAL_COUNSEL_NOT_HELD`, `ANCESTRAL_COUNSEL_IN_COMBAT`;
  - `OLHAR_DE_LACERTO_NOT_HELD`.

## Changed

- `SkillGraduationService#upgradeGraduation` spends the race-aware cost. `getUpgradeCost(CharacterSkill)`
  is still the race-less base.
- `ConditionType.maleficios()` now excludes both `ESCONDIDO` and `PETRIFICADO`.
- `GorgonaFeat#CABELO_SERPENTINO` now applies its Desvantagem em Persuasão. It was withheld until
  the paired Vantagem had a target.

## Now real

- `IndomitoFeat#RENEGAR_A_LACERTO`: no Ferocidade while an ally in Distância Curta has 1+ PV.
- `GorgonaFeat#ACEITAR_A_SELVAGERIA`: the Ferocidade from the third Rodada.
- `BestialFeat#ACEITAR_A_LACERTO`: mimics the Ferocidade. The +1d6 on Armas Naturais is reported
  on `InteractionResult#getExtraDamageDice`.
- `MonstruosoFeat#SELVAGERIA`: converts FEROCIDADE's dano bonus into Dano Base. That TODO was stale:
  the "Bônus de Ferocidade" is the Talento's own bonus.
- The `OgricoFeat` tree:
  - `MANDIBULA_DESARTICULADA`: swallows the holder's own Categoria and one above;
  - `DEVORATRIZ`: Roubo de Vida, and kills a victim digested to 0 PV;
  - `DOIS_ESTOMAGOS`: +2 capacity;
  - `PODEROSO_GLUTAO`: −1 Vigor per victim, half the digestion hours.
- `OrquicoFeat#AGNACAO_ANCESTRAL_SUPERIOR`: the Peão is reported (`pawnSubordinateGranted`).
- `GnomoFeat#SABICHAO`: Aprendizado Rápido extends to the 7th Graduação.
- `HumanoFeat#APRENDIZADO_RAPIDO_E_CONTINUO`: to the 5th Graduação, or the 7th with a Título.
- `GorgonaFeat#MARCA_DA_MALDICAO`: Olhar de Lacerto reaches Distância Curta.
- `GorgonaFeat#CABELO_SERPENTINO`: Vantagem on the gaze's attack and dano.
- `GiganteFeat#ZELO_PELOS_FRAGEIS`: lifts Cuidado para não Quebrar. Its Movimento half stays a
  purpose-scoped TODO.

## Readings

- ⚠️ "Turno de combate" is read as the combat Rodada.
- ⚠️ "Danos" is read as every dano roll.
- ⚠️ One Autocontrole point avoids the Ferocidade for the rest of the combat.
- ⚠️ SELVAGERIA converts at BRUTALIDADE's 1:1 rate.
- ⚠️ Digestion damage is applied unmitigated.
- ⚠️ A counselled untrained roll counts as 0 Graduações.
- ⚠️ Petrification attempts are counted per combat.

## Not built

- The Ferocidade's forced targeting and concentration block.
- Petrification refusing Perícia rolls: no condition can veto a roll.
- A Subordinado acting for its holder.
- Digestion time: there is no hour clock.
- `HomemFera`'s Forma Híbrida as an `ActiveAbility`.

## Fixed

- `Feat#resolveCriticalResistance`'s javadoc had been separated from its method (a Phase B slip).

## Callers

- **Ferocidade:** call `LacertoFerocityService#refresh(sheet, ownContext)` at each combatant's Turn
  start. Offer `decline` and `mimic` as player actions.
- **Bocarra:**
  1. `declareBite` before the bite.
  2. Put `devorarInteiro` on `DeliveredAttack#effectChains`, and add the roll bonus.
  3. `digest` once per Rodada.
  4. `recordDamageFromInside` after a swallowed character hits the captor.
- **Agnação Ancestral:** `perform`, then build the roll with `.counselled()`, then `spend` after the
  roll resolves.
- **Olhar de Lacerto:** `declare`, then deliver a `DeliveredAttack` with
  `OlharDeLacerto.INSTANCE` against the target's DM, then `applyHit` on a hit.
- **Aprendizado Rápido:** at creation, set `Character.builder().quickLearningSkills(...)`.
