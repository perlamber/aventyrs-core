# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.1.6.14

**Danos de Queda e de Asfixia.** This is Phase 1 of `docs/plans/acoes-de-combate-plan.md`, which was re-planned on
2026-10-14 against the 20261013 revision of the Ações de Combate doc (`docs/rules/acoes-de-combate.txt`, re-imported).
Both damages are Dano Primordial that nothing reduces; that part was already true from 0.1.6.13.

## Added

- **`character.services.FallService`** (+`Impl`):
  - `getFallResistance(sheet)`: twice the effective Categoria de Tamanho, minimum 2UD.
  - `preview(sheet, heightUd)` measures a fall without applying it. `fall(sheet, heightUd, dice, ctx)` applies it:
    - the damage is half the excess height, plus 1d6 for every full 10UD of excess;
    - any such die also brings Feridas Dolorosas until the next Descanso of any tier;
    - an excess beyond 40UD is instant death (curse damage, the `RealExecution` depth, but revival is still possible);
    - every fall leaves the faller Caído, even one too short to hurt.
  - `FallResult` reports the figures.
- **`character.services.AsphyxiaService`** (+`Impl`), holding its state in **`sheet.Asphyxia`** on the sheet
  (`CombatantSheet#getAsphyxia`/`#setAsphyxia`):
  - Methods: `stopBreathing`, `breathe`, `isBreathless`, `getRoundsPerInstance` (Vigor, minimum 1) and `tick`, which
    returns an `AsphyxiaTick`.
  - An instance lands every Vigor Rodadas. It deals 1d6, then 2d6, then 3d6 thereafter. The 4th instance adds Feridas
    Dolorosas until any Descanso, and the 6th is death.

## Rulings (table, 2026-10-14)

- **Asfixia outside combat:** the caller ticks it once per Rodada. In combat that happens at the start of the
  combatant's Turn; out of combat it is the Narrador's "+1 Rodada". Neither core nor the sheet ticks it on its own.

## Readings (not table rulings)

- ⚠️ "Quando um personagem cai ele sofre … a Condição Caído" (from Escalar) is applied to every fall.
