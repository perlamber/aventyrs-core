# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.1.4

**The ordinary Despertar** — a Título awakens at an EXP mark, picked by the player then.

## New

- **`title.TitleAwakeningService`** (`TitleAwakeningServiceImpl`) — Título Primário at
  `PRIMARY_AWAKENING_EXPERIENCE` (15) total EXP, Secundário at `SECONDARY_AWAKENING_EXPERIENCE` (30);
  the Terciário has no mark and is never offered.
  - `dueSlot(sheet)` — the slot due now, filled in order (no Secundário while the Primário is empty). Not
    due when a held Talento presets that slot, `Feat#permitsTitleSlot` refuses it (Centelha Dormente's
    25 EXP, Herança de Gilgamesh, Abdicador), no Centelha is left to awaken from, or nothing may fill it.
  - `optionsFor(sheet, slot)` — every `TitleCatalog` family not already held that
    `TitleAcquisitionService#isPermitted` allows for the slot.
  - `awaken(sheet, title)` — grants into the due slot through `TitleAcquisitionService#grantTitle`;
    refuses with `TITLE_AWAKENING_NOT_DUE` / `TITLE_AWAKENING_CHOICE_NOT_ELIGIBLE`.
  - `mayPostpone(character)` — Atrasar Despertar's "apenas quando quiser e se quiser". Nothing is stored;
    the caller decides how a postponed offer comes back.
- **`Feat#presetsTitleAwakening(slot)`** — `DespertarAntecipadoFeat` (Primário) and
  `CentelhaGranAventyrAntecipadaFeat` (Secundário) answer it: their own pick awakens at a session end.

## Changed

- **`DestinoFeat#ABDICADOR` closes every Título slot** (`permitsTitleSlot` → false): "Você não Desperta
  Títulos Aventyr" is now enforced by `TitleAcquisitionService`, a pre-picked `TitleAwakening` and the
  new picker alike. `Character#grantTitle`, the unchecked mutator, still doesn't ask.
