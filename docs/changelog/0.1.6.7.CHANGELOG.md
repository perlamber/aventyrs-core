# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.1.6.7

**Pedras do Poder, V19.** `docs/rules/pedras-do-poder.txt` (2026-10-11) replaces the old section. Table rulings
2026-10-12 (plan: items-remaining, Phase 2).

## Rulings

- **Vinculação means socketing**:
  - It happens **in combat only** and costs **1PA**, or an Ação Livre with Conexão Veloz. The PA are reported.
  - Each one spends 1 Carga and deals the stone's Danos to the host Equipamento. The host's own item reduction applies,
    and a **Regalia takes none**.
  - Effects last the Duração. Then **stone and host** both enter Resfriamento.
  - Ending a combat ends every binding and clears every cooldown. Spent Cargas stay spent.
- **Encaixe is removed.** Any **Obra-Prima** Arma, Armadura or Escudo takes a stone.
- Stones are standalone inventory items, bought by Raridade or granted by the Narrador. Joalheria refining and
  AEthernum recharge come later.
- ⚠️ Readings:
  - Vitalitas Immanis is an Ação Livre, in or out of combat.
  - Arcanum and Anima Selvagem Cargas mix with real PM/PD.
  - Solve-Vidas does nothing on a Regalia host.

## Changed

- **`PowerStone` is an `Item`** (`ItemCategory.POWER_STONE`):
  - It has per-copy `remainingCharges`/`lastChanceUsed`, `spendCharges`, `isUsable` and `restored(...)`.
  - Its getters are renamed `getStoneType`/`getStoneMasterpiece`/`getStoneImprovement`, since they clashed with
    `Item`'s.
  - Identity, not value, equality.
  - An Aprimoramento needs an Obra-Prima, so `withImprovement` is gone.
- **`PowerStoneType`** now carries the V19 texts:
  - The Base, Protetivo (`getDefensiveEffect`) and Violento (`getOffensiveEffect`) prose.
  - The bonuses that can now be expressed.
  - New `resolveBonus(type, host, hostWielded)`, `resolveMajorCriticalMarginIncrease`, `resolveCriticalDamage`,
    `getViolentCriticalEffect`, `createViolentEffectChain` and `isViolentRestrictedToCastingArtifacts`.
- **`PowerStoneQuality`** carries the V19 table: Incomum/Raro/Épico/Mítico, Preço 5/12/20/30, Cargas 6/10/20/14,
  Danos 3/2/1/3. It adds `getName` and `reducesCooldownOnEarlyRemoval` (Relíquia).
- **`PowerStoneMasterpiece`** uses the V19 PE prices and drops its rarity. Refinada floors at 1.
- **`PowerStoneImprovement`** now has 7 constants: Anima Selvagem, Arcanum, Conexão Veloz, Dissipador de Calor,
  Mecanismo de Sobrecarga, Última Carga and Vitalitas Immanis, each with its price.
- `DefensiveImprovement.ENCAIXE` and `OffensiveImprovement.ENCAIXE` are **removed**.
- `AbstractItem#setPowerStone` now checks `Item#acceptsPowerStone`. Esfera de AEther refuses any weapon that is not
  a `CASTING_ARTIFACT`.

## Added

- **`item.PowerStoneService`** (+`Impl`): `readyStones`, `hostsFor`, `getBindCost`, `bind` (→ `BindResult`),
  `unbind`, `heal` (Vitalitas, 2PV per Carga) and `drawCharges` (Arcanum/Anima).
- **`sheet.PowerStoneBinding`** and the following `CombatantSheet` members:
  - `startItemCooldown`/`getRemainingItemCooldown`;
  - `getPowerStoneBindings`/`startPowerStoneBinding`/`endPowerStoneBinding`;
  - `claimFirstPowerStoneUseThisCena`.
  A lapsing binding unbinds itself and cools both down.
- **Stone effects reaching services:**
  - Atributos, through `Character#getEquipmentAttributeBonus` (in `getEffectiveAttributeTotal` and the Perícia roll);
  - RDS in `DamageServiceImpl`;
  - RC in `getTotalCriticalResistance`;
  - +1PA in `ActionPointsServiceImpl`;
  - Margem Menor/Maior and Dano Crítico through `Item`'s crit hooks, with the Violento scoped to the host weapon;
  - an extra Efeito Crítico and a Corrente in `AttackDelivery`.
- **Store:** `PowerStoneSpecification`, `ItemStore#getOfferedPowerStoneQualities`/`offers(PowerStoneSpecification)`,
  and `ItemPurchaseService#getPurchasePrice`/`purchase(store, PowerStoneSpecification, buyer)`.
- **Anima Selvagem:** `TitleAbilityActivationRequest#determinationStone`/`determinationStoneCharges`.
- `ItemCategory.CASTING_ARTIFACT` (populated in 0.1.6.8).
- `TranslatableMessages`: `POWER_STONE_NOT_IN_COMBAT`, `_NOT_CARRIED`, `_HOST_INVALID`, `_COOLING_DOWN`,
  `_NO_CHARGES`, `_IMPROVEMENT_REQUIRED`, `_NOT_BOUND`.
