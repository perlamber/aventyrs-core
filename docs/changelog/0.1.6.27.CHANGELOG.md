# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.1.6.27

**A creature's anatomy travels with it.** The client acts for an invocation through a `CharacterSheet` built from the
creature's `Character`. A `MonsterSheet`-only fact therefore never reached the sheet the table actually plays: the
Anatomia de Morto-Vivo, a stat block's damage immunities, and the Abantesma's Duração-for-PV. These now live on every
sheet and can be handed over. This came up while wiring the client for `docs/plans/undead-invocation-plan.md`
(Phase 6).

## Added

- **`CombatantSheet#grantUndeadAnatomy()`**, **`#markDamageShortensDuration()` / `#isDamageShortensDuration()`** and
  **`#addDamageImmunities(Set<DamageScope>)`**, all held by `AbstractCombatantSheet`.
- **`MonsterSheet#transferAnatomyTo(CombatantSheet)`** — copies all three onto the sheet a client acts through.

## Changed

- Every Anatomia de Morto-Vivo clause is answered by `AbstractCombatantSheet` from its own flag, where 0.1.6.23 had
  `MonsterSheet` overrides. A `MonsterSheet` sets the flag from its template or blueprint at spawn. The clauses are:
  - Efeito Crítico immunities;
  - Profano and Asfixia immunity;
  - Magia Profana damage immunity;
  - Profano healing;
  - curative heals dealing Dano Sagrado;
  - immunity to doença, maldição and veneno.
- A stat block's `getDamageImmunities()` is granted onto the sheet. `MonsterSheet` no longer overrides
  `isImmuneToDamage`.
- `Scene#settleSummons` shortens any summon sheet that `isDamageShortensDuration()`, not only a `MonsterSheet`.
- `ZumbiAbility`'s and `DefensiveEnhancements#isLiving`'s "living" also excludes a holder of the Anatomia.
