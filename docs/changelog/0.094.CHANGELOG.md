# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.0.94

**VIDA, Magia by Magia** — step 2 of `docs/plans/spell-casting-audit.md` (core half).

## New

- **`SpellCastRequest#opposedBranchLevel`** — a rung-scaled GD follows what the Magia removes. Toque Curativo and
  Remover Maldição use `Spell#getCastingDifficultyAgainst`; a mundane affliction counts as Semente, a Monstruosa/Aventyr
  one as Emergente.
- **`SpellCastRequest#additionalTargets`**, with `Spell#getMaxAdditionalTargets` and `#getAdditionalTargetManaCost`:
  - Benção Bifurcada: one more target.
  - Corrente Abençoada: any number, +3PM each.
  - Over the cap, or a non-participant, is refused.
- **`Spell#isCasterAffected`** — whether a caster-centred area reaches the caster:
  - Nova Rejuvenescedora: yes.
  - Cura em Massa: yes; now a 1-UD circle on the caster (⚠️ "até 2m").
  - Fonte da Juventude: no.
- **`SpellHealing`** gains three columns:
  - `restoringAllPools` (PV, PM, PD);
  - `withHostileRest` (a hostile's own Descanso tier);
  - `oncePerLongRestPerTarget` (Aliviar a Dor).
- **Fonte da Juventude is authored** — Descanso Curto for every pool, Mínimo for hostiles.
- **Wards** (`Spell#getWard`, `SpellWard`, `effect.SpellWardEffect`):
  - Corpo Fechado leaves a `sheet.MaleficioWard`, which refuses every Malefício.
  - It is `sheet.Sustained` by the caster's Concentração; `Scene#breakConcentration` now also releases sustained
    effects on every participant (`CombatantSheet#releaseSustainedBy`), and the ward then lasts 2 Rodadas.
  - Procrastinar Ferimento leaves a `sheet.PostponedWoundWard`. The next attack damage (`DamageInteraction`, with an
    attacker) spends it and lands a Rodada later.
- **Estancar** (`SpellChainKind#ESTANCAR`, `effect.Estancar`) — the postponed hit leaves a `sheet.StanchedWound`, which
  refuses a `Bleeding` for that Rodada.
