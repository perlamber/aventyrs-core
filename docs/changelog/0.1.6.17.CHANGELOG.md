# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.1.6.17

**The Reações every character has.** This is Phase 4 of `docs/plans/acoes-de-combate-plan.md`, covering the
"Manobras de Combate - Reações" section of the 20261013 doc. Core still fires nothing: it reports who may react, where,
and with what, and the API/client run the reaction windows.

## Rulings (table, 2026-10-14)

- **Defender o Perímetro:**
  - A hostile reacts the first time any position of the route lies inside its melee reach. Leaving the start counts.
  - Each hostile reacts once per movement.
  - The move pauses there and continues unless the mover can no longer move. That last part is the caller's.
- **Impedir Conjuração:** −2 on the casting roll per 5 points of damage taken after mitigation.
- **Sacrifício:**
  - Once per combat, for an adjacent ally.
  - The attack lands with no defence, applies at least the Efeito Crítico Menor, and fires every Corrente.
- **Preparação:**
  - 3PA to name an action of at most 2PA.
  - It is fired once with a Reação of its own before the holder's next Turn begins. The trigger is narrated.

## Added

- **`ReactionTrigger`** gains `ENEMY_MOVES_IN_REACH`, `ENEMY_CASTS_IN_REACH` and `PROVOKING_MANOEUVRE`.
- **`action.GenericReaction`** lists the six generic Reações and the triggers each answers. **`action.GenericReactionOption`**
  is the generic twin of `ReactionOption`.
- **`ReactionOptionsService#getGenericReactions(context)`** offers:
  - Defender o Perímetro, to a reactor with a melee weapon or Arma Natural in hand;
  - Impedir Conjuração, when the caster is within that reach;
  - Sacrifício, when the ally is adjacent and it is still unused;
  - one Conjuração Reativa per castable Magia cast as a Reação;
  - a held Preparação.
- **`MovementReactionService#getRouteCheckpoints(mover, route, manoeuvre, ctx, positions, chargeTarget)`** returns
  `action.ReactionCheckpoint(routeIndex, at, reactors)`:
  - distance is in 3D, less both bodies' radii;
  - the existing exemptions apply: Reposicionar, held exemptions, and an Investida's own target.
- **Impedir Conjuração:** `SpellCastRequest#disruptionDamage`, and `SpellCastingService.disruptionPenalty(damage)` on
  the casting roll.
- **Sacrifício:**
  - `character.services.SacrificeService`: once per combat via `markAffectedThisCombat`, ally adjacent, spends the
    Reação.
  - `IncomingAttack#intercepted` / `DeliveredAttack#intercepted`: no defence, at least a Menor, Correntes forced; an
    immunity still holds.
- **Preparação:**
  - `character.services.PreparationService` with `PREPARATION_COST` = 3PA and a 2PA ceiling.
  - `sheet.PreparedAction` lapses at its holder's next Turn start and is fired once.
  - `CombatantSheet#getPreparedAction` / `#firePreparedAction`.
- **`TranslatableMessages`**: `PREPARED_ACTION_TOO_COSTLY`, `NO_PREPARED_ACTION`, `SACRIFICE_ALREADY_USED`,
  `SACRIFICE_ALLY_NOT_ADJACENT`, `NO_REACTION_AVAILABLE`.
