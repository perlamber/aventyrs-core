# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.0.70

**Duelista, fully wired.** Every `DuelistaFeat` TODO is closed. Most of the tree is a choice made on
one attack, so this version adds the machinery for that: Talentos a roll *spends*, a lowest-die
reroll, an attack's price, a per-target and per-Talento action log, a Roubo-de-Vida-recoverable PV
lock, and Cego's 1d6. **Artilharia, fully wired** on the same machinery, plus a reach step bought by
an activated Talento and repeated Efeitos for an extra projectile. **Escudeiro, fully wired**: a Escudo can be swung (`item.ShieldAttack`), and the Turn-end,
opening-Rodada, Reação, damage-negation and mitigation-trade mechanisms its Talentos need.
**Metamágico, fully wired**: an incoming Magia's identity reaches the defence and the damage, so
every "Magias que você conheça / seja capaz de conjurar" clause is real, and the Barreira Mágica
reads its holder's other Talentos.

Table rulings (2026-09-27): Tiro Múltiplo scales per projectile; an attack costs a Perícia roll's price (2PA); Combater com 2 Armas' dano
Desvantagem applies only when neither drawn weapon is Leve; Explorar Pontos Fracos widens exactly the
second attack on a target; Dominar Armas discounts the first attack each Rodada made with the chosen
method. Metamágico (2026-09-27): "capaz de conjurar" and "conheça" both mean learned plus mimetized;
Arcanista's RM is one instance; Aptidão Mágica Dracônica's reserve is current PD ≥ 10, and its
immunity means defended outright, with no damage and no effects.

## New

- **Activation**
  - `Feat#permitsActivation(SkillType, SkillRoll, AttackSource, CombatantSheet)` — true by default.
  - `AbstractSkillInteraction` refuses a roll that activates a Talento not held
    (`ACTIVATED_FEAT_NOT_HELD`) or one `permitsActivation` denies (`FEAT_ACTIVATION_NOT_PERMITTED`).
  - Longest overloads taking the roll: `Feat#resolveSkillRollBonus(…, CombatantSheet, SkillRoll)` and
    `Feat#resolveDamageBonus(…, CombatantSheet, SkillRoll)`.
- **Rerolls**
  - `SkillRoll#rerollingLowestDie(int)`, `getRerolledFromFace()`, `isRerolled()`.
  - `Feat#grantsLowestDieReroll(SkillType)`; an unpaid reroll is refused (`REROLL_NOT_GRANTED`).
  - `Feat#resolveDamageLowestDieRerolls` → `InteractionResult#getDamageLowestDieRerolls()`.
- **Attack price**
  - `ActionPointsService#getAttackCost(CombatantSheet, SkillType, AttackSource, Set<Feat>, int)`.
  - `Feat#resolveAttackActionPointOverride` and `Feat#resolveAttackActionPointAdjustment`.
- **Action log**
  - `CombatantAction#targetId` and `#activatedFeats` (the six-argument constructor stays), plus
    `activated(Feat)` and `isAttack()`.
  - `AttackDelivery`/`AttackReceiver` fill both on `getRecordedAction()`.
  - `CombatantSheet#getActionsThisTurn()`, `getActionsOfLatestOwnTurn()`,
    `countFeatActivationsThisRound/ThisTurn(Feat)`, `countAttacksAgainstThisRound(UUID)`.
- **Dano riders**
  - `Feat#resolveExtraDamageDice`, summed onto `InteractionResult#getExtraDamageDice()`.
  - `Feat#halvesAttackDamage`, folded into `DeliveredAttackResult#isEveryTargetHalved()`.
  - `Feat#resolveLockedSelfDamageOnHit` → `DeliveredAttackResult#getLockedSelfDamage()` (pay it with
    `payWithVitality`).
  - `Feat#resolveUnhealableCriticalDamage` → `DamageInteraction#lockingDamage(int)`.
- **PV locks**: `CombatantSheet#lockDamage(int, boolean lifeStealRecovers)` and
  `getLifeStealRecoverableLockedDamage()`. `getLockedDamage()` now sums both locks, and
  `releaseVitalityLock()` clears both.
- **Margem Crítica by target**: `Feat#resolveCriticalMarginIncrease(…, CombatantSheet holder,
  CombatantSheet attackTarget)` and `CriticalService#sumCriticalMarginIncrease(…, target)`.
- **Golpe Trovejante**: `effect.GolpeTrovejante`, an `EffectChain` that makes `AttackDelivery` apply the
  natural Efeito Crítico once more on a critical past the Corrente threshold.
- **Defender-se Atacando**
  - `IncomingAttack#defenseSkill`, `#enchantmentOrCurse`, `isEnchantmentOrCurseAttack()`.
  - `IncomingAttackResult#getCounterWeaponDamage()` and `isAttackerDamageAdvantage()`.
  - `Feat#permitsDefenseSubstitution(DefenseType, boolean)`, refused as
    `DEFENSE_SUBSTITUTION_NOT_PERMITTED`.
- **Cego's 1d6**
  - `sheet.BlindCheck` (2 personal / 3 melee / 4 ranged) and `CombatantSheet#getBlindCheckThreshold`.
  - `Feat#exemptsFromBlindCheck`.
  - `SkillRoll#withBlindCheck(int)`.
  - `InteractionResult#getBlindCheckThreshold()`/`getBlindCheckFailed()`. A failed die fails the roll,
    misses in `AttackDelivery`, and fails the defence in `AttackReceiver`.
- **Artilharia**
  - `Feat#resolveAttackRangeIncrease(Character, AttackSource, Set<Feat>)`, and
    `AttackRangeService#getEffectiveRange(Character, Weapon, Set<Feat>)` /
    `getEffectiveRangeInUnidadesDeDistancia(Character, Weapon, Set<Feat>)` (a widened band reports
    its own upper UD).
  - `Feat#resolveAttackEffectRepetitions`: `AttackDelivery` repeats each triggered Corrente and
    Efeito Crítico that many extra times as an `effect.RepeatedEffect`.
  - `Feat#exemptsFromBlindCheck(SkillType, SceneContext)`, for an exemption scoped to the target.
- **Escudeiro**
  - `item.ShieldAttack`: the `Weapon` view of an equipped Escudo, or `wings()` (a Escudo Médio). It has
    1d6+1/+2/+3 by weight, Margem 17, Atordoante, and the Defesa it is rolled against (`against`).
    Refused without Atacar com Escudos or the shield in hand (`SHIELD_ATTACK_NOT_PERMITTED`).
  - `AttackDelivery` re-aims a shield attack at its own `DefenseType`. `DeliveredAttack` has
    `toBuilder`.
  - `item.ShieldDefenseRetention` and `Feat#resolveShieldDefenseRetention`: `DefenseService` cuts a
    shield's Defesa after it attacks, until its wielder's next Turn (`ShieldAttack#retained`).
  - `feat.ShieldSpecialty`: Especialista em Escudo's choice. It uses the same names as `ShieldItem`,
    plus `ASAS_ADAMANTINAS`.
  - `effect.Rugido`, the Corrente: a reported push (`InteractionResult#getPushedBackUd`), +2 PV on a
    critical.
  - `Feat#resolveCriticalHitEffectChains`, the critical-gated Corrente hook.
  - `Feat#resolveTurnEndBlessings`, granted by `CombatantSheet#finishTurn`.
  - `scene.InitiativePosition`, on `SceneContext#getInitiativePosition()` and
    `Scene#initiativePositionOf`.
  - `Feat#ignoresDefenseMaluses` and `CombatantSheet#getTemporaryMalus`.
  - `Feat#ignoresReactionPrevention`, read by `ReactionsService`.
  - `Feat#resolveDamageNegationBudget`, spent by `DamageService`, and the sheet's rest-scoped uses
    (`getRestScopedUses`/`spendRestScopedUse`/`restoreRestScopedUses`), cleared by `completeTrueRest`.
  - `Feat#forgoesDamageMitigation`: no RDS/RA/RD/RM (nor Pele de Pedra, nor RA's crit step), turned
    into Defesa.
  - `Feat#resolveAttackReachIncrease` (UD) and
    `AttackRangeService#getEffectiveRangeInUnidadesDeDistancia(CombatantSheet, Weapon, Set)`.
  - `Feat#resolveAttackCostDifficultyReduction(…, CombatantSheet holder)` and
    `CombatantSheet#isInOwnTurn()`.
- **Metamágico**
  - `magic.SpellFamiliarity#canCast(Character, Spell)`: learned or mimetized, an Efeito Alternativo
    through its base.
  - `Feat#resolveSpellDefenseBonus`, added by `AttackReceiver` to the defence total and by
    `AttackDelivery` to the Defesa a Magia must beat.
  - `Feat#resolveSpellResistanceDifficultyReduction`, applied by `AttackReceiver`; `AttackDelivery`
    reports it as `DeliveredAttackResult#getUnappliedSpellResistanceReduction()`.
  - `Feat#isImmuneToSpell`: defended in `AttackReceiver`, a miss in `AttackDelivery`, zero damage in
    `DamageService`, and `SpellCastingResult#isTargetImmune()`.
  - `Feat#resolveSpellMagicReduction`, an RM scoped to the Magia, and
    `DamageInteraction#fromSpell(Spell)` with the matching eight-argument
    `DamageService#calculateFinalDamage`. `AttackDelivery`/`AttackReceiver` mark a Magia's chain head.
  - `Feat#resolveSpellHealingBonus` → `SpellCastingResult#getHealingBonus()` and
    `SpellEffectContext#healingBonus` (read by `SpellHealingEffect`).
  - `ActiveAbility#getActionPointCost(Character)`/`#getMagicPointCost(Character)`, which
    `ActiveAbilityService#activate` now pays.
  - `SpellDurationService#resolveNonSpellDurationIncrease(Character)`: every extension hook is asked
    with a `null` Magia.
  - `BarreiraMagicaActiveAbility` is public: its bonus is sourced (`SOURCE`), with `isActiveOn`,
    `resolveAllyDefesasBonus` and `resolveDurationInRounds(Character)`.
  - `CombatantSheet#hasEffectFrom(String)`. `DefenseService` scans adjacent allies' Barreiras.
  - `SpellCastingService#resolveManaCost(SpellCastRequest)`: a cast's PM, asked before casting.
- **Defender o Perímetro** (table ruling, 2026-09-28): an Investida names its target first and then
  moves as one atomic action. The target cannot react to the charger's movement; everyone else in
  reach can.
  - `MovementReactionService#getProvokedReactors(…, chargeTarget)` and `ChargeService#begin(…, target)`.
  - `Feat#permitsPerimeterDefenceAsChargeTarget` — `EscudeiroFeat#MESTRE_ESCUDEIRO`, while using a Escudo.
- Messages: `ACTIVATED_FEAT_NOT_HELD`, `FEAT_ACTIVATION_NOT_PERMITTED`, `REROLL_NOT_GRANTED`,
  `DEFENSE_SUBSTITUTION_NOT_PERMITTED`.

## Changed

- The ENCANTADORA Aprimoramento and Arcanismo Elemental's Duração hook answer 0 for a `null` Magia.

- A roll naming a Talento its roller does not hold is now refused. Before this version it resolved.
- `ATAQUE_GIRATORIO` refuses a second activation in the same Rodada.

## Now real

- `LUTADOR_NATO`: reroll + 1PA. `ATAQUE_CONCENTRADO`: -2 attack / +2 dano, or +1d6 with Lutador Nato.
- `LUTAR_ENGAJADO`: dano reroll against an adjacent target. `COMBATER_COM_2_ARMAS`: the 3PA pair, both
  Desvantagens.
- `DEFESA_COM_2_ARMAS`: the zero lasts until the holder's next Turn. `DOMINAR_ARMAS`: -1PA, and the
  Magias Ofensivas exclusion.
- `MAESTRIA_EM_ARMA`: Golpe Trovejante. `ATAQUE_RAPIDO`: +2 attack / -2 dano.
- `ATAQUE_REPENTINO`: 1PA, Meio-Dano, once per Turn. `COMBATER_AS_CEGAS`: no 1d6 except on ranged.
- `FORCA_EXCESSIVA`: +½ Vigor, and 2 locked PV on a hit. `ATAQUE_GIRATORIO`: once per Rodada.
- `UM_DOIS`: after a landed Ataque Rápido, 1PA. `EXPLORAR_PONTOS_FRACOS`: +2 on the second attack.
- `FERIDAS_ARDENTES`: the lock. `DEFENDER_SE_ATACANDO` and `_SUPERIOR`: the substitution.
- `ArtilhariaFeat#MIRA_IMPECAVEL`: reroll + 1PA. `ConditionType#CEGO`: the 1d6.
- `ArtilhariaFeat#TIRO_LONGO`: the second step with Mira Impecável. `TIRO_RAPIDO`: after a shot with
  the same weapon, 1PA, Desvantagem, once per Rodada.
- `TIRO_DUPLO`: +1d6, Desvantagem, Correntes/Efeitos Críticos twice, once per Rodada, not as a Reação.
  `TIRO_MULTIPLO`: the same per projectile — table ruling, N = clamp(Títulos Despertos, 2, 4) — sharing
  Tiro Duplo's use.
- `DISPARO_AS_CEGAS`: requires Combater às Cegas; no Cego 1d6 on a shot up to Distância Média.
  `MIRA_MORTAL`: requires Acerto Crítico Aprimorado chosen for a ranged weapon.

- `EscudeiroFeat`, every constant:
  - Atacar com Escudos, Espartano, Ataque Múltiplo, and Arte do Escudo Atacante (+2 margin, Rugido).
  - Domínio (-1 nível; Alcance Estendido after the Rugido "Empurrão Violento").
  - Defesa Tartaruga, Início Defensivo, Criar Refúgio, Asas Adamantinas (wings as a Escudo Médio),
    Bastião de Vidro.
  - Mestre Escudeiro's Reações through prevention, and Defender o Perímetro as an Investida's target.
  - Escudo Veloz counts the wings.
- `ArtesMarciaisFeat#DOMINAR_ARTE_MARCIAL_TIGRE_E_SERPENTE`: the Rugido on criticals.

- `MetamagicoFeat`, every constant:
  - Arcanista's RM against known Magias.
  - The Barreira's Duração extension, and Mestre Arcanista/Desafiador's +1/+3 to adjacent allies.
  - Artesão de Barreiras: the Ação Livre, and -1 nível while a Barreira runs.
  - Conjuração Rápida's Desvantagem on healing.
  - Aptidão Mágica Ampla +3 / Assombrosa +5 DM, and Suprema's -1 nível.
  - Engenheiro do Mana on the Barreira, and Aptidão Mágica Dracônica's immunity.
- `ElementalFeat#ARCANISMO_ELEMENTAL`: "+2" on healing.

- `PeritoFeat`, every constant:
  - "Vantagem adicional" is another +2 (table ruling) — Discreto, Exibicionista, Trabalho em Equipe.
  - Foco em Perícia's Pré-requisito (trained; 4 Graduações for a Perícia de Ataque): options filtered,
    and re-checked on `FocoEmPericiaFeat#isEligible`.
  - Discreto: no ally or neutral in Curta; dano Vantagem while Escondido from every enemy.
    Exibicionista: 4+ neutral characters in the Scene.
  - Perito Veloz -1PA, Lembrar Como se Faz 1PA + Vantagem (not a retry of a retry), Maestria ×3 price
    and -2 níveis out of combat, its reroll in combat, never with Persuasão/Atenção.
  - Mestre Perito: Ação Livre (Rodadas 1–2) or Reação (Rodada 1), once per Rodada; 5 Graduações.
  - Lembrar, Revisar e Aprimorar -1 nível on the retry. Leitura Comportamental -1 nível on Discernir
    Motivação. Mestre em Atuação as an opt-in (table ruling), Artes 2 required.
  - Controle da Situação's and Treinado em Perícias' "Graduação 4 em 3 Perícias"; Treinado's
    "recém-criados" branch and its declared choice (3 trained Perícias, either trait kind).
  - Analista Tático: `Scene#deferToLast`, +1 Ação Livre and Reação while last in combat. Grande
    Analista Tático: +1 Margem Crítica Menor per Título, Corrente resistance ±1, while last.
  - Trabalho em Equipe (2 trained allies in Curta) and its Aprimorado (-1 nível instead).
  - Criança do Mar / Rei da Montanha: `PeritoActiveAbility`, 1PD per Rodada.
- New engine pieces for them:
  - A neutral allegiance: `Scene#setAggressive`/`#declareNeutral`/`#getNeutrals`/`#areHostile` and

  - A Perícia roll's price: `ActionPointsService#getSkillRollCost(sheet, skill, feats, turn, ctx)` and
    `Feat#resolveSkillRollActionPointOverride`/`Multiplier`/`Adjustment` (reaching attacks too).
  - `Feat#resolveDifficultyReduction(…, SkillRoll)`, `#permitsActivation(…, SceneContext)`,
    `#grantsLowestDieReroll(skill, roller, ctx)`.
  - `Feat#resolveReactionsIncrease`/`#resolveFreeActionsIncrease`, `#permitsDeferringToLast`,
    `#resolveEffectChainResistanceIncrease`/`#resolveTargetEffectChainResistanceReduction` and
    `EffectChainService#getRequiredMargin(attacker, position, defender, position)`.
  - `Feat#isEligibleAtCreation` (starting slots ask it), `#resolveRequiredSkillTraitKinds`.
  - `sheet.SourcedState`, `CombatantSheet#canBreatheUnderwater`/`#canClingToSurfaces`,
    `Feat#allowsUnderwaterBreathing(Character, CombatantSheet)`/`#clingsToSurfaces`.
