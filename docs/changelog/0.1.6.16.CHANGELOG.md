# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.1.6.16

**The board gets a vertical axis and painted terrain.** This is Phase 3 of `docs/plans/acoes-de-combate-plan.md`. It
supplies the geometry that Voo, Escalar, Nadar, Queda and the straight-line manoeuvres need. As before, positions stay
the caller's.

## Rulings (table, 2026-10-14)

- **Signed elevation:** every token has an elevation in UD.
  - Positive means flying or climbing; negative means under water.
  - Reach and range use `max(hex distance, |Δelevation|)`.
  - Movement costs the path plus |Δelevation|.
- **Terrain cells:** the Narrador paints each hex as one of these kinds:
  - GROUND, WATER, CLIMBABLE or OBSTACLE;
  - optionally Terreno Difícil;
  - with a ground level and an optional GD.

## Added

- **`scene.grid.BoardPosition(hex, elevation)`**, plus `HexGrid#distance3d` and `RangeBand#fromBoardDistance`.
- **`HexGrid#line(a, b)`**: the hexes a straight line crosses, ends included.
- **`scene.grid.TerrainKind`** and **`scene.grid.TerrainCell(kind, difficult, groundLevel, difficulty)`**, whose GD
  defaults to Médio.
- **`MovementMap`** changes:
  - a sixth component, `cells`;
  - `cellAt(hex)`;
  - `isLineClear(from, to, mover)`, meaning no obstacle, Terreno Difícil or other occupant strictly between the ends.
  - `isDifficultTerrain` also reads a cell's flag.
  - The five- and four-argument constructors are unchanged.
- **`EnvironmentalState#withFlightAndSubmersion(flying, submerged)`**, for a caller deriving both from the board.
- **`StepRules#canStep(from, to)`** is an edge check, true by default. The pathfinder now honours it, and both
  decorators forward it.
- **`MovementTerrainService#stepRules(…, Integer travelElevation)`**:
  - **Walker** (`null`):
    - an OBSTACLE is closed;
    - WATER is open only with a Natação Movimento Base;
    - a step rises or drops at most `MAXIMUM_STEP` (1 UD).
  - **Flyer** (a number):
    - hexes whose ground reaches the flight are closed;
    - it passes over obstacles below it, over occupants and over Terreno Difícil at cost 1;
    - it still stops only where a walker could, because two tokens never share a hex.

## Readings (not table rulings)

- ⚠️ `MAXIMUM_STEP` = 1 UD: no rules text gives a step height.
