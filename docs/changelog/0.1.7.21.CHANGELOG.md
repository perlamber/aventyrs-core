# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.1.7.21

**MobilidadeFeat wiring, Phase 4: INICIATIVA APRIMORADA, LIDERAR O AVANÇO, PORTA-ESTANDARTE.**

## New

- **`feat.VantagemDeIniciativaAprimoradaFeat`** — Iniciativa Aprimorada's acquired form, the Autoconhecimento shape.
  The bare constant now declares a `FeatChoice<EgoAdvantage>` and is refused by `FeatService#grantFeat`.
- **`Scene#applyInitiativeBlessings(Function<CombatantSheet, List<Blessing>>)`** — every winner of every winning
  group at once, each keeping its own Blessings.
- **`Scene#applyFirstToActBlessings()`** and **`Feat#resolveFirstToActBlessings(CombatantSheet)`** — Blessings for
  whoever holds the first turn-order slot, and their allies when ally-scoped.
- **`scene.MovementHeading`**, **`Scene#getHeadingMovementBonus`**, **`Feat#resolveApproachMovementIncrease`** /
  **`#resolveAllyApproachMovementIncrease`** — extra UD per Ponto de Ação for a move ending nearer to someone.

## Talentos now real

- **Iniciativa Aprimorada** (table ruling 2026-10-17) — the Vantagem de Iniciativa picked at acquisition, granted
  while the Iniciativa total is 4 or more.
- **Porta-Estandarte** (table ruling 2026-10-17) — while the holder holds the first slot: +1PA for 2 Rodadas and +1PA
  for 1 (so +2PA, then +1PA) to the holder and allies; +2UD in core Rodadas 0–1 for the holder moving nearer an enemy
  and for an ally moving nearer the holder.
