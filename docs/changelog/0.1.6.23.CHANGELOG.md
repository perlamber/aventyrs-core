# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.1.6.23

**Anatomia de Morto-Vivo.** The undead anatomy from the Monster manual's Escolhido de Macária (v19 20261013) is now
modelled. Every spell-raised undead takes it in place of the "Anatomia de Morto-Vivo Menor" its Magia prints (table
ruling, 2026-10-14). Phase 1 of `docs/plans/undead-invocation-plan.md`.

## Added

- **`monster.UndeadAnatomy`** — the anatomy's clauses:
  - Efeito Crítico immunities: Atordoante, Dilacerar, Empalar, Ferida Profunda, Oferenda Maldita and Sangramento.
  - Resistência a Correntes +2.
  - Immunity to doenças, maldições and venenos.
  - Profano effects heal 1d6 + Metade do Vigor and deal no damage.
  - Curative effects made for the living deal 2d6 + Metade do Vigor Dano Sagrado instead of healing.
  - No asfixia.
- **`CombatantSheet#hasUndeadAnatomy()`** — the question every consumer asks. It is true for a foe whose stat block
  (`MonsterTemplate#hasUndeadAnatomy()`) or held Habilidade Monstruosa (`MonstrousAbility#grantsUndeadAnatomy()`, read
  by `MonsterBlueprint#hasUndeadAnatomy()`) grants it. It implies `MonsterSheet#isUndead()`.
- **`CombatantSheet#isImmuneToSpellDamage(Spell)`** — immunity to a Magia's damage alone, leaving its effects free to
  land, unlike `isImmuneToSpell`. `DamageService` reads it.
- **`CombatantSheet#resolveInvertedHealing(spell, descriptor, roller)`** — the Raça's inverted heal (Vampiro) plus the
  Anatomia's. `DamageInteraction` now asks the sheet, so a Profano hit with no Magia heals the dead too.
- **`HealingSource#dice()` / `#withDice(DiceRoller)`**, **`SpellEffectContext#diceRoller()` / `#withDiceRoller`**,
  **`SpellHealingEffect#withDiceRoller`** — the caller's dice reach the heal, for the Anatomia's 2d6 Dano Sagrado. With
  no dice supplied, the PV the heal would have given still stand in, as before.

## Changed

- **`Zumbi`** takes the full Anatomia (`hasUndeadAnatomy()`). `ANATOMIA_DE_MORTO_VIVO_MENOR` is removed, along with its
  Natural damage immunity and its Amaldiçoar and Excruciante immunities (ruling 2026-10-14). Its Profano immunity and
  the Divine-heal inversion come from the Anatomia now.
- **`EffectChainService#getRequiredMargin(attacker, pos, defender, pos)`** adds the Anatomia's +2.
- **`AsphyxiaService#stopBreathing`** does nothing for a holder of the Anatomia.
- **`DiseaseService#propagationCandidates`** skips anyone immune to `DOENTE`.

## Readings (⚠️)

- "Efeitos curativos feitos para os vivos" are read as a Magia that isn't Profana, or a Habilidade de Título. A
  Descanso, a Regeneração and Roubo de Vida still heal the dead, and so does a healer that is itself undead ("podem
  curar um Morto-Vivo, desde que eles também sejam Mortos-Vivos"). On a relayed heal the healer is not known, so the
  exemption can't apply there.
- "Efeitos diversos de sangramento" is covered by the Sangramento Efeito Crítico immunity. Other bleeding effects
  (`sheet.Bleeding` from a Corrente) are not blocked yet.
- The Dano Sagrado lands raw, like the 0.0.91 inversion it replaces.
