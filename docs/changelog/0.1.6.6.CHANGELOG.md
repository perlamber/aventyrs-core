# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.1.6.6

**Projéteis are ammunition.** An Arco or Besta fires a projectile or nothing. Table rulings, 2026-10-12
(plan: items-remaining, Phase 1).

## Rulings

- Every projectile is consumable. An Arco fires **Flechas**, a Besta fires **Virotes**, and each of the six
  `equipamentos.txt` Projéteis exists in both forms with identical stats and Preço.
- An Arco's projectile is picked **per shot**, from the bag. A Besta's is picked **at reload**, and it fires what its
  magazine holds.
- A Besta's loaded state **persists** across Cenas. Reloading costs its "Tempo de Ação da recarga" in combat and
  nothing outside it. A partial reload takes whatever units are left.
- At the end of a combat, floor(half) of each kind fired comes back to the bag. A **Bodkin** never does.
- Zarabatanas (Dardos) and Shurikens stay TODO. The Dardo Preço is being remade.

## Added

- **`item.ProjectileHead`** (the 6 rows: Efeito Crítico, Margem, Corrente, recoverable, lot), **`item.ProjectileItem`**
  (12 constants, `FLECHA_*`/`VIROTE_*`, registered in `ItemCatalog`), **`item.AmmunitionKind`** (ARROW/BOLT/DART).
- **Stackable copies**:
  - `ItemTemplate#getLotSize()` (default 1) and `AbstractItem#quantity` (`getQuantity`/`addUnits`/`takeUnits`).
  - `Item#isStackable()`: a lot template with no enhancement or damage.
  - `AbstractCombatantSheet#addToInventory` merges a stackable copy into the stack already carried.
  - A forged or bought projectile is one whole lot.
- **`Weapon#getAmmunitionKind`/`#getMagazineCapacity`/`#requiresReload`/`#getReloadCost`/`#getFavoredReloadCost`**:
  - Every `BowItem` fires ARROW.
  - Every `CrossbowItem` fires BOLT from a magazine: Mão and Pesada hold 1 and reload for 1PA; Repetição holds 5 and
    reloads for 2PA, or 1PA under its Favor.
  - `AbstractWeapon` carries these columns plus a per-copy `loadedProjectiles` magazine (`load`/`nextLoaded`/`fireLoaded`).
- **`combat.AmmunitionService`** (+`Impl`):
  - `availableFor` and `firableFrom` list what a weapon can use.
  - `requireFirable` gates a shot; `consume` spends it from the bag or the magazine and notes it for recovery.
  - `getReloadCost` and `reload` handle the magazine.
- **`DeliveredAttack#projectile`**. For a character's Arco or Besta, `AttackDelivery` refuses a shot with
  `AMMUNITION_REQUIRED`, `PROJECTILE_INCOMPATIBLE`, `WEAPON_NOT_LOADED` or `NOT_ENOUGH_AMMUNITION` (Tiro Duplo/Múltiplo
  fire one each). It adds the projectile's Efeito Crítico ("Projétil (17)": the number is the weapon's, the effect is
  the projectile's) and its Corrente. `DeliveredAttackResult#getFiredProjectile`/`#getProjectilesToConsume` report what
  to spend, hit or miss. A foe's stat block fires freely.
- **`SkillRoll#withProjectile`/`#getProjectile`**. A Bodkin widens the Margem Crítica Menor by one número.
- **Four Correntes**:
  - `effect.DisparoEsmagador` (Esférica): 1 damage to the target and to each worn Equipamento Defensivo.
  - `effect.TrespassarLonginquo` (Folha Cortante): reports a Penetrante line, Curta.
  - `effect.Desestabilizar` (Ponta Dupla): −1UD Movimento, not cumulative, for ⚠️ 2 Rodadas (read); a flyer is
    grounded for 2 Rodadas, reported on `InteractionResult#getGroundedRounds`.
  - `effect.Incendiar` (Porta Fogo): +1d6 with the attack's roller, retyped Físico Elemental: Fogo.
- **Recovery**: `CombatantSheet#recordFiredProjectiles`/`getFiredProjectilesThisCombat`, and `#isInCombat`.
  `CombatantSheet#endCombat()` now returns the `item.RecoveredProjectiles` it put back. `Scene#endCombat()` returns
  them per participant.
- `TranslatableMessages`: `AMMUNITION_REQUIRED`, `PROJECTILE_INCOMPATIBLE`, `WEAPON_NOT_LOADED`,
  `NOT_ENOUGH_AMMUNITION`, `RELOAD_NOT_POSSIBLE`.

## Changed

- `CombatantSheet#endCombat()` and `Scene#endCombat()` return the recovered projectiles. Callers that ignore the return
  value are unaffected.
- A Projétil cannot take an Obra-Prima.
