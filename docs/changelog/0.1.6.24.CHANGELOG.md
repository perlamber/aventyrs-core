# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.1.6.24

**Corpses, raising and the Foco limit.** This is Phase 2 of `docs/plans/undead-invocation-plan.md` (table rulings,
2026-10-14).
- Reanimar and Criar Carniçal touch a real body.
- A body can be raised four times.
- An undead raised from a corpse leaves the corpse behind.
- A caster controls at most as many invocações as their Foco.

## Added

- **`magic.invocation.RaiseDeadService`** (+`Impl`): `isRaisable` / `requireRaisable(scene, corpse)`.
  - A body is any of:
    - a combatant at status DEAD, a player character included;
    - a fallen undead (≤ 0 PV);
    - a sheet marked `isCorpse()`.
  - It is refused when it has been raised `MAX_RAISES` (4) times, is marked beyond revival, or is a standing
    invocação.
- **`Scene#raise(caster, corpse, raised, group, rounds, concentration)`**:
  - The corpse leaves the Scene (a PC's sheet is kept).
  - The undead joins the Scene as `addSummons` would place it, carrying the body's `timesRaised + 1`.
  - Its record is `SceneSummon#isRaisedFromCorpse()`.
- **`CombatantSheet#getTimesRaised` / `#setTimesRaised` / `#isCorpse` / `#markCorpse`.**
- **`magic.invocation.SummonLimit`** and **`Scene#countControlledSummons` / `#remainingSummonRoom`**.
  - "Número máximo de invocações" = Foco. Every controlled summon counts.
  - Uncontrolled summons don't count, and neither do those marked to leave or those the new cast replaces.
- **`SceneSummon#isUncontrolled()`** and **`Scene#releaseControlOf`**, in place for Festim dos Mortos (0.1.6.25).
- **`TranslatableMessages.SUMMON_LIMIT_REACHED` / `RAISE_REQUIRES_CORPSE` / `CORPSE_EXHAUSTED`.**

## Changed

- **`Scene#dismissSummon`**: a summon raised from a corpse leaves its body instead of the Scene. The record goes, and
  the sheet is marked a corpse and stays where it stood. This covers falling (`settleSummons`), its Duração ending, its
  caster leaving and an explicit dismissal.
- **`NatureInvocationServiceImpl#place`** refuses a plan past the caster's Foco. **`Scene#addSummonSpawner`** refuses
  when the caster is at the limit, and a spawner's per-Rodada creature is skipped while the caster is at it.

## Readings (⚠️)

- The body is left behind however the raised undead goes, not only at ≤ 0 PV: the Magia ending drops the body.
- A body marked beyond revival (`markBeyondRevival`) can't be raised.
- Every summon counts against the limit, a Bruxo's Familiar Maior included.
