# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.1.6.4

**Every Vantagem de Ego is wired.** The last TODOs in `org.aventyrs.core.ego` are closed. Table rulings, 2026-10-10:
Barganhista applies at all times, character creation included. Ímpeto's "Efeitos de Magia" are a Magia's Dano and Cura.

## Added

- **`EgoAdvantage#resolveSpellEffectBonus(SceneContext)`**. `SpellCastingServiceImpl#castSpell` sums it into both
  the spell-damage bonus and the healing bonus.
  - `InitiativeAdvantage#IMPETO` gives +2 to each in the first two Rodadas once initiative is won.
- **`EgoAdvantage#exemptsFromMovementReactions(Manoeuvre, SceneContext)`**. It is ORed into
  `MovementReactionServiceImpl#isExempt` beside the Habilidade de Atributo pass.
  - `InitiativeAdvantage#POSICIONAMENTO_ESTRATEGICO` exempts every movement in the first two Rodadas of a Cena de
    Combate, whether or not initiative was won.
- **`ego.GrantedMove`, `EgoAdvantage#resolveEgoSpendMove(EgoPointSpend)` and `EgoPointsService#getSpendMoves`**.
  - A granted move is a one-shot movement with a total distance. It costs no Pontos de Ação and no movement of the
    Rodada.
  - It is reported, never applied; the caller offers it.
  - `SorteAdvantage#AS_NA_MANGA` grants a 2UD move that ignores Terreno Difícil and provokes no Reações.
- **`ego.EquipmentPointExpense` and `EgoAdvantage#resolveEquipmentPointDiscount(EquipmentPointExpense)`**. The kinds
  are PURCHASE, PRODUCTION, UPGRADE and TITLE_ABILITY. BARGANHISTA takes −2PE off the first two and −1PE off the
  others.
- **`character.services.EquipmentPointPricing#discounted`**. It is the one place a PE price takes its holder's
  discounts, floored at 1PE. Both `ItemPurchaseService` and `EquipmentCraftingService` use it.
- **`EquipmentCraftingService` spends PE.** Its new `CombatantSheet` forms are:
  - `forge`
  - `forgeRegalia`
  - `installImprovement`
  - `repair`

  Each debits the wallet once every gate has passed. A refused or unaffordable job costs nothing. Two new price
  queries go with them:
  - `getFabricationCost(Character, ItemSpecification)`: half the specification's total value, less PRODUCTION
    discounts.
  - `getImprovementInstallCost(Character, Improvement)`: half the Aprimoramento's Preço, read from "usa a mesma base
    de cálculo", less UPGRADE discounts.

  A repair pays its assessed cost and takes no discount. The `Character` forms stay report-only.
- **`SpellCastingService#isEffectChainTriggered(result, caster, target, castTotal)`**. A Magia's Corrente now reads the
  same full margin as an attack: Resoluto, the target's resistance Talentos, Vontade Fraca / Atrair a Morte, and the
  caster's reductions. The three-argument form delegates to it with no caster.
- **`MonstruosoFeat#ANATOMIA_UNICA`** gets its "+2 resistência às Correntes de Efeitos" through
  `Feat#resolveEffectChainResistanceIncrease`, the same reading Devoto's +2 uses.

## Changed

- `ResourcesAdvantage#BARGANHISTA`'s description drops "após a criação do Personagem, em campanha", and
  `docs/rules/ego.txt` records the ruling.
- `SorteAdvantage#AS_NA_MANGA` no longer grants a 1-Rodada `MOVEMENT` bonus. That bonus had over-granted 2UD on every
  Ponto de Ação spent moving.

## Removed

- `EgoAdvantage#resolveEgoSpendBlessings`, `EgoPointsService#getSpendBlessings`, and the blessing grant in
  `useEgoPointsForEffect`. Ás na Manga was their only user.
- `EgoAdvantage#resolveEquipmentPurchaseDiscount()`, replaced by `resolveEquipmentPointDiscount(EquipmentPointExpense)`.

## Still open

- No Habilidade de Título has a PE price, so nothing spends BARGANHISTA's TITLE_ABILITY −1PE.
- Cambalacho and Profissão Focado/Lenda still have no discount hook for `EquipmentPointPricing` to scan.
- An Aprimoramento's install *time* is still unpriced.
