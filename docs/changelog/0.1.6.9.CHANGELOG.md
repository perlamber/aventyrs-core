# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.1.6.9

**Every Aprimoramento Ofensivo is wired.** All 17 `OffensiveImprovement` constants now reach the attack, the damage
or the Magia. The Ataque/Danos columns ride a new pass scoped to the weapon the attack is made with,
`item.WeaponEnhancements`, so a sheathed weapon never helps.

## Rulings and readings

- Table rulings (2026-10-12):
  - **Benção Elemental**: the hit is fully both types. Immunity to the chosen element stops it, that element's RE
    comes off on top of RD, and a Fraqueza to it applies.
  - **Machado Anexo / Baioneta**: the ranged weapon counts as a Machado/Lança for every category-scoped clause. It
    also gains a melee mode with its own Dano Base and Efeito Crítico.
  - **Inquisição**: the target's Magias are +2 níveis harder until the attacker's next Turn starts.
- ⚠️ Roubo de Mana N mirrors the Roubo de Determinação ruling: the target loses N PM and the attacker regains what was
  taken.
- ⚠️ "Favor +N" stacks with the Adicional (Solve-Vidas = Roubo de Vida 3, Ladra do AEther = 2 Mana and 2 Determinação).
  "muda para" replaces it (Pente Alongado = +3).
- ⚠️ Benção Vulcana's Corrente acts on the hit that triggered it: RE and RM are ignored at the chain head.
- ⚠️ Corrente Atacante's +1d6 lands after mitigation, like Rugido's +2.
- ⚠️ Machado Anexo is a Lâmina Leve (this core has no Machado category; a Machado de Mão is a Lâmina Leve).
- ⚠️ Benção Elemental's Favor reaches any elemental Magia, whichever element.
- ⚠️ Pente Alongado widens a Besta's magazine. An Arco has no magazine, so it does nothing there.

## Added

- **`item.WeaponEnhancements`**, the weapon-scoped scan. It feeds:
  - the Ataque and Danos columns of an offensive host's Obra-Prima and Aprimoramentos (attack roll; dano roll as
    `DamageContributionSource#WEAPON_ENHANCEMENT`);
  - Alcance Estendido's +1 Ataque/Dano against a target the Scene places beyond Adjacente, and its +1UD reach
    (`AttackRangeServiceImpl`);
  - Roubo de Vida/Mana/Determinação and the Correntes, in `AttackDelivery`;
  - the Conjuração columns and the Magia clauses of every **drawn** weapon (`SpellCastingServiceImpl`): Arcanista's
    damage and healing +1, Benção Elemental's Conjuração and damage +1 on an elemental Magia.
- **`Weapon#host()`**, **`Weapon#countsAs(ItemCategory)`** (read by `AttackMethod#matches`) and
  **`Weapon#getEffectiveMagazineCapacity()`** (read by `AmmunitionServiceImpl`).
- **`item.AttachedMeleeAttack`**: the melee mode of a ranged weapon fitted with Machado Anexo or Baioneta. It is a
  view like `ShieldAttack`, and the host-scoped enhancement checks see through it.
- **`item.OffensiveItemImprovement`**: Benção Elemental with its chosen element. `Improvement#offensiveDefinition()`
  unwraps either form.
- **Effects:** `RouboDeMana`, `RouboDeDeterminacao`, and three Correntes: `Inquisicao`, `IgnoraResistencias` and
  `CorrenteAtacante`.
- **`character.BypassedReduction`** (RD/RE/RM), `DamageInteraction#bypassing`/`#withAdditionalElement`, and the
  matching longest `DamageService#calculateFinalDamage`.
- **`CombatantSheet#markSpellDifficultyIncrease`/`getSpellDifficultyIncrease`/`expireMarksAnchoredTo`**:
  `Scene#next()` lifts the marks anchored to whoever's Turn begins, and `endCombat()` lifts them all.
- `HandBudget#isTwoHanded` counts Corrente com Peso.
- `AttackEffectsPreview` lists the weapon's Correntes.
