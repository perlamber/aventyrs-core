# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.1.6.25

**The REANIMAR tree's invocations.** This is Phase 3 of `docs/plans/undead-invocation-plan.md`. Reanimar, Servo
Cadavérico, Criar Carniçal, Açougueiro and Festim dos Mortos (with Horda Faminta) now turn a cast into creatures. Every
creature takes the Anatomia de Morto-Vivo (0.1.6.23). Corpses, the four-raise limit and the Foco limit come from
0.1.6.24.

## Added

- **`magic.invocation.UndeadInvocationService`** (+`Impl`) — the `NatureInvocationService` twin.
  - `planReanimar` / `planCarnical(…, butcher)`: an `UndeadPlan` sized from the corpse.
  - `raise`: checks the corpse is raisable, the Açougueiro exclusivity and the caster's Foco, then places the creature
    with `Scene#raise`.
  - `servoCadaverico`: the corpse becomes a Cavaleiro/Torre `Subordinate`, held by Concentração + 2, raise count +1.
    Escudeiro Cadavérico makes it Prodigioso for its first 2 Rodadas.
  - `openSarcophagus`: Festim dos Mortos.
- **`magic.invocation.UndeadPlan`** — its own record, since `InvocationPlan` is typed to `NatureSummon` for its
  callers.
- **`magic.invocation.FestimDosMortos`** — the sarcophagus as a pure table, so a server can run the same rule:
  - `kindFor(face)`: 1–3 Zumbi, 4–5 Carniçal, 6 Abantesma.
  - `spawnsFor(face, horda, room)`: past the Foco, or under Horda Faminta, a spawn is uncontrolled; Horda Faminta adds a
    Zumbi.
  - `alliedUndeadDifficultyReduction` (−2 níveis at Grad 10).
- **`monster.summon.Carnical`** (+`CarnicalAbility`) — its magias.txt stat block, with the tiers at 7 and 10.
  Açougueiro gives +1PA, Vantagem on attack and damage, and Roubo de Vida 1.
- **`monster.summon.Abantesma`** (+`AbantesmaAbility`):
  - "Nula" Força and Vigor read as 0.
  - Flight, Defesas +18, 4PA.
  - Immune to plain Físico damage.
  - Possessão Furiosa on its attacks.
  - No PV: `MonsterTemplate#damageShortensDuration` makes `Scene#settleSummons` take a Rodada off it and wipe the
    damage.
- **`monster.summon.UndeadSummonKind`** — ZUMBI / CARNICAL / ABANTESMA, with `restore(kind, grad, bodySize,
  butcher)` for persistence.
- **`effect.PossessaoFuriosa`** — `POSSESSAO` for 2 Rodadas.
- **`Zumbi#naturalAttack()`** ("1d6+3 (Base 1+ Metade da Força)") and **`Zumbi.clamp`**.
- **`Subordinate.sustainedBody`**, **`#prodigiousThrough(round)`** / **`#isProdigiousAt(round)`**. `SubordinateBenefits`
  reads a group-reaching Subordinado by `isProdigiousAt` the Scene's Rodada.
- **`TranslatableMessages.SERVO_CADAVERICO_GRADE_NOT_ALLOWED` / `BUTCHER_CARNICAL_EXCLUSIVE`.**

## Changed

- **`SummonSpawner`** is driven by a `Rule(firing, room) → List<Spawned>`. A spawn may be uncontrolled; it then stands
  in the spawner's own hostile group and is `SceneSummon#isUncontrolled`. A spawner may also:
  - hold its creatures back a Rodada;
  - take them with it when it ends;
  - end with the Cena de Combate.

  The `Supplier` form (Totem de Gaea) is unchanged in behaviour. Spawners now fire after the Rodada's pending merge.
- **`Scene#endCombat`** ends combat-scoped spawners.

## Readings (⚠️)

- Açougueiro's "não poderá controlar mais nenhum outro Carniçal": neither kind may join while the other is held.
- Servo Cadavérico's corpse stays on the board as the servant.
- Carniçal's Defesas are 3 + 11 = 14, summed the way the Zumbi's are. Its trained Perícias present Médio, and the
  Abantesma's present Difícil; no stat block gives a GD.
- An Abantesma loses one Rodada per settle, so the caller settles after each hit.
- Invocar Abantesma cast on its own is still TODO; only Festim dos Mortos invokes one so far.
