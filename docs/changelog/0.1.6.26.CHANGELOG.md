# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.1.6.26

**Modelo Escolhido de Macária.** Phase 4 of `docs/plans/undead-invocation-plan.md`. This is the seventh Modelo, from
manual v19 20261013.
- Its eight Habilidades are honestly statused.
- Every one of them grants the Anatomia de Morto-Vivo.
- Its monster-side summons invoke generated Carrascos Moribundos (table ruling, 2026-10-14).

## Added

- **`monster.model.escolhidodemacaria.EscolhidoDeMacariaAbility`** and **`MonsterModel.ESCOLHIDO_DE_MACARIA`**.
  Mestre das Marionetes is read as Apex (table ruling), so the Modelo splits 2/2/2/2.
  - **Renascido** [Presa]: Força and Instinto +2, RDS. Sanguenomancia Inata gives Roubo de Vida 2 and +1 Vigor for 3
    Rodadas.
  - **Carrasco Morimbundo** [Presa]: Vigor +2 and Roubo de Vida 1, rising to 3 at Deviante. Instinto Predatório Mortis
    gives +2 Iniciativa and +2 PA for 2 Rodadas, plus Margem +3 at Apex.
  - **Senhor dos Mortos** [Deviante]: Procissão dos Desencarnados reports its Carrascos. Predador adds Carisma +2.
  - **Espectral** [Deviante]: Incorpóreo, so plain Físico damage is refused. Attack Margem +1. Flies from Predador.
    Garras Espectrais' price only.
  - **Filactéria** [Predador]: Proteger a Filactéria (+1 nível for the Cena). Apex adds RA and Metade do Vigor PV each
    Rodada; Abominação adds Meio-Dano except Sagrado, Primordial and Fogo.
  - **Anunciador da Escuridão** [Predador]: RA, RD and RM. Marca da Escuridão gives +1 nível on attacks.
  - **Cuspidor de Pragas** [Apex]: its spawning, plus Praga Zumbi's price.
  - **Mestre das Marionetes** [Apex]: Títere's own immunity to damage.
- **`monster.model.MonsterSummoning`** and **`MonstrousActiveAbility#getSummonOptions()`** — what an activation
  invokes, reported for the caller.
- **`monster.summon.CarrascoMorimbundo#stock(MonsterCategory)`** — a legal stock blueprint at the Categoria's floor GP.
- **`UndeadInvocationService#invokeCarrascos`** (Procissão, capped at the monster's Carisma) and **`#startPlagueSpitting`**
  (Cuspidor: 2 Deviantes on odd Rodadas, gone with combat). **`Scene#endSpawnersOf(caster)`**, for a destroyed
  Cuspidor.

## Changed

- `MonsterModelTest` counts 7 Modelos.

## Readings (⚠️)

- The stock Carrasco's numbers are authored: the spell Zumbi's profile within 10 points, three trained Perícias and
  Garras Afiadas.
- Carrasco's Apex Roubo de Vida 4 needs proximity, so it is held at 3.
- Marca da Escuridão's +1 nível lasts 1 Rodada.
- Títere's immunity lasts 1 Rodada.
- "Rodada Ímpar" counts from 1, so core Rodadas 0, 2, 4…
