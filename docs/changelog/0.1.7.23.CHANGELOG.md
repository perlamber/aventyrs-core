# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.1.7.23

**BestialFeat fully wired.** Every Herança now grants its Perícia trait in play, and the clauses that were TODOs are
real, except Felina's Visão no Escuro (no senses concept).

## Fixed

- **A Herança's free Perícia trait was never granted.** The bare constants declared no choice, so `grantFeat` accepted
  them plain and nothing ever built `HerancaBestialFeat`. Each Herança now declares its pick through
  `Feat#resolveRequiredChoices` — one `FeatChoice<SkillTrait>` per trait kind, offered from `SkillTraitCatalog` minus
  the traits already held — so the bare constant is refused (`FEAT_REQUIRES_CHOICE`).
- **`HerancaBestialFeat` dropped Anfíbia's grapple Vantagem.** It forwarded the Atributo, Arma Natural, movement and
  flight hooks but not `resolveSkillRollBonus`. It now forwards every hook a Herança overrides, and
  `HerancaBestialFeatTest` fails if one is missed.
- **Metamorfose Selvagem without a Herança.** "Um animal que pertence a uma categoria de Talento Bestial que você
  possua" was claimed in a comment and checked by nothing; `MetamorfoseSelvagemActiveAbility#isUsableBy` now refuses
  it (`ACTIVE_ABILITY_CONDITION_NOT_MET`).

## Changed

- **`HerancaBestialFeat` holds a list of traits** (`getChosenTraits()`, `of(BestialFeat, List<SkillTrait>)`) — Canina
  grants "uma Especialização e uma Habilidade de Competência de Atenção", two picks in that order. The single-trait
  constructor and `getChosenTrait()` (the first) stay.
- **`Asphyxia` records `submerged`**; `AsphyxiaService#stopBreathing(sheet, submerged)` is the new entry point (the
  one-argument form is out of the water).

## New

- **`Feat#resolveActivationDeterminationCost(SkillType)`** → **`InteractionResult#getActivationDeterminationCost()`** —
  the PD twin of `resolveActivationManaCost`, reported for the caller to spend.
- **`Feat#breathesUnderwater(Character)`** and **`Feat#resolveSubmergedBreathMultiplier(Character)`**, read by
  `AsphyxiaService`.

## Talentos now real

- **Herança Anfíbia** — breathes under water: a submerged holder never starts the Asfixia clock (any other loss of
  breath still does).
- **Herança Canina** — Faro Apurado, Vantagem on Atenção. ⚠️ Read broadly, on every Atenção roll: this core never
  tracks what a roll is for (the same reading Avianos' Visão Além do Alcance and Sempre Alerta take).
- **Herança Cetácea** — Ecolocalização: activated on an Atenção roll, -1 nível and 2PD reported. "Mapeia todo o
  terreno … em Distância Média" is interpretative (table ruling, 2026-10-17): the Narrador describes what is there.
  Holds its breath twice as long under water (Rodadas between Asfixia instances ×2 while submerged).
- **Every Herança** — its Habilidade de Competência or Especialização, picked at acquisition.
