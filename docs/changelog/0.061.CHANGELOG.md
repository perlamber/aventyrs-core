# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.0.61

**Talentos whose TODOs named a system that already existed.** A sweep of `org.aventyrs.core.feat`
(plan Phase A) found Talentos blocked only by out-of-date TODO text: a Escudo is classifiable, the
action log and Forma exist, Correntes have a hook, `anyOf` and Especialização prerequisites are
real. Where the rules could now be applied, they are. Every other TODO checked now names its real
blocker. Feat TODO lines: 312 → 286.

## New

- `Feat#resolveRetaliation(holder, attackSource, attacker, criticalHit)`: what a defender's Talento
  deals back to whoever **landed** a melee attack on them. Default `null`.
- `DeliveredAttackResult#getOnHitRetaliations()` / `IncomingAttackResult#getOnHitRetaliations()`:
  one `Retaliation` per answering Talento, only on a landed Ataque Corpo-a-Corpo. The existing
  `getRetaliation()` (thorns, hit or miss) is unchanged. Reported, never dealt.
- `feat.EspecialistaEmEscudoFeat`: the acquired form of `EscudeiroFeat#ESPECIALISTA_EM_ESCUDO`,
  carrying the chosen `ShieldItem`. `chosenBy`, `isChosenShield`, `isUsingChosenShield`.
- `feat.EspecialistaEmArmadurasFeat`: the acquired form of
  `SobrevivenciaFeat#ESPECIALISTA_EM_ARMADURAS`, carrying the chosen `ItemWeightClass`.
  `chosenBy`, `wearsArmor`, `wearsArmorOf`.
- `feat.AtributoRacialEscolhidoFeat`: "+1 de Bônus Racial no Atributo escolhido", keyed on its
  `talento`. Serves `MonstruosoFeat#ALFA`, `#DUAS_CABECAS` and `HumanoFeat#LIMIAR_DA_EVOLUCAO`.

## Now real

- `DuelistaFeat#EXPLORAR_PONTOS_FRACOS`: Metade da Gnose added to a critical's dano (`CriticalDamage`).
- `DuelistaFeat#DEFESA_COM_2_ARMAS`: +1/+2/+3 DF while two weapons are drawn, +2 more while neither
  attacked this Rodada, 0 once both attacked (unless 10 Graduações). Needs a sheet.
- `DuelistaFeat#CORACAO_DE_FERRO`: 1 Dano Físico back to a melee attacker, 3 on an Acerto Crítico.
- `MonstruosoFeat#SANGUE_ACIDO`: 1 Dano Físico Elemental: Natural back, +2 against an Arma Natural.
- `MonstruosoFeat#ALFA`, `#DUAS_CABECAS`, `#CERBERO`, `HumanoFeat#LIMIAR_DA_EVOLUCAO`: their Bônus
  Racial. ALFA offers the Atributos carrying a Bônus Racial, plus Força. CERBERO grants whichever of
  Gnose/Instinto Duas Cabeças did not pick.
- `ArtesMarciaisFeat#DOMINAR_ARTE_MARCIAL_SUBMISSAO`: +2 DF while Caído.
- `EscudeiroFeat#ESPECIALISTA_EM_ESCUDO`: +1/+2/+3 Defesas while a copy of the chosen Escudo is equipped.
- `EscudeiroFeat#ASAS_ADAMANTINAS`: +2 Defesas while not flying (`EnvironmentalState#flying`).
- `SobrevivenciaFeat#VITALIDADE`: RD 2 (`DamageService#DEFAULT_DAMAGE_REDUCTION`, the clause states no
  figure) while current PV ≤ Multiplicador de PV.
- `SobrevivenciaFeat#ESPECIALISTA_EM_ARMADURAS` / `#DOMINAR_ARMADURAS`: the +1/+2/+3/+5 ladder in the
  chosen armour tier, widened to any armour by Dominar, which adds +2 in the chosen tier.

## Changed — acquisition

- **16 more Talentos are creation-only** (`Feat#isAcquirableOnlyAtCreation`), as their Pré-requisito
  says "Apenas … recém-criados/as": `DraconicoFeat#ARMAMENTO_DRACONICO`, `#ASAS_DE_DRAGAO`, the four
  `ElficoFeat` Guardiões, `GnomoFeat#DUENDE`, `MesticoFeat#CARACTERISTICA_RACIAL_ADICIONAL`,
  `MonstruosoFeat#ANATOMIA_INCOMUM`, `#OSSOS_OCOS`, `FeericoFeat#FAUNO`, `#LUPERCAL`, `#PIXIE`,
  `#SIRENIDEO`, `GorgonaFeat#MARCA_DA_MALDICAO`, `#ACOLHIDA_POR_FLORA`.
  `FeatService#grantFeat` now refuses them (`FEAT_ONLY_AT_CREATION`); take them in a starting slot.
- **5 more constants declare a choice** and refuse their bare form (`FEAT_REQUIRES_CHOICE`):
  `ESPECIALISTA_EM_ESCUDO` (`ShieldItem`), `ESPECIALISTA_EM_ARMADURAS` (`ItemWeightClass`), `ALFA`,
  `DUAS_CABECAS`, `LIMIAR_DA_EVOLUCAO` (`AttributeDomain`).
- Prerequisites now enforced: `PeritoFeat#CRIANCA_DO_MAR` (Pulmão de Aço + Anfíbio),
  `#REI_DA_MONTANHA` (Levantamento de Peso + Alpinista Veloz), `MobilidadeFeat#INVESTIDA_AQUATICA`
  (Triatleta), `MonstruosoFeat#DUAS_CABECAS` (Bestial, or Ogro, or Monstruosa with a Título),
  `SobrevivenciaFeat#PERMANECER_CONSCIENTE` (Vigor or Instinto 5; Duro de Matar).

## Callers

- After an attack, apply each `getOnHitRetaliations()` entry to the attacker's sheet with
  `DamageService#applyDamage`, the same as `getRetaliation()`. Skip them if the hit dealt no damage
  after mitigation.
- A client offering Talentos must route the 5 new choice declarations (3 new types). Characters
  already holding a bare `ESPECIALISTA_EM_ESCUDO`/`ESPECIALISTA_EM_ARMADURAS`/`ALFA`/`DUAS_CABECAS`/`LIMIAR_DA_EVOLUCAO`
  keep it, but it grants nothing until replaced by its acquired form.
- A character that already holds a now creation-only Talento keeps it. Only new grants are refused.
