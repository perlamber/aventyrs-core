# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.1.6.11

**Every Aprimoramento Defensivo is wired.** All 16 `DefensiveImprovement` constants now reach the attack, the damage,
the Magia or the Scene. The new scan scoped to the wearer is `item.DefensiveEnhancements`, the twin of
`WeaponEnhancements`. It reads every equipped, intact, non-offensive item. This release builds on the damage taxonomy
of 0.1.6.10.

## Rulings (table, 2026-10-12)

- **A defensive host's Ataque/Danos/Conjuração columns** reach every attack and every Magia the wearer makes.
- **Benção Ymiriana** fires when the wearer is the target of any attack, hit or miss. Its Danos +1 becomes +2 until
  the end of the wearer's next Turn. A second attack renews the window; it does not stack.
- **Espinhosa / Rigidez Refletora** fire on every melee attack against the wearer as the *primary* target, hit or
  miss. They never fire for an additional target. Espinhosa's Favor makes its figure 2, dealt both to the attacker
  (through their mitigation) and to their weapon. Rigidez deals 2 to the weapon and destroys a projectile.
- **Solve-Vidas** triggers on any source of damage. The victim dies an ordinary death, not beyond revival.
- **Benção Elduriana's "Danos Mágicos"** covers any Mágico hit, whatever its qualifier.
- **Ladra do AEther** triggers on any non-semente Magia cast by someone else.

## Readings (not table rulings)

- ⚠️ Elduriana's −1 Rodada can take a hostile Encantamento/Maldição down to 0 Rodadas: a 1-Rodada effect then does
  not last at all.
- ⚠️ Camuflada's "Vantagem adicional" is +2, the same reading the Perito Talento uses.
- ⚠️ "Vivo" for Solve-Vidas is the Presença de Carmilla reading: not a Renascido and not an undead foe. A foe with no
  Raça counts as living.
- ⚠️ The Vulcana/Elduriana −1 is a reduction of its own, separate from RD/RM/RE. Benção Vulcana's weapon bypass does
  not reach it.

## Added

- **`item.DefensiveEnhancements`**:
  - `attackRollBonus` / `damageRollBonus` / `castingRollBonus`, read by `AbstractSkillInteraction` and
    `SpellCastingServiceImpl`. The dano roll reports them as the new `DamageContributionSource#DEFENSIVE_ENHANCEMENT`.
  - `onAttacked` (Ymiriana), called by `AttackDelivery` for the primary and additional targets and by
    `AttackReceiver` for its defender.
  - `retaliationAgainst` (Espinhosa/Rigidez).
  - `damageTakenReduction` (Vulcana/Elduriana), read by `DamageServiceImpl`.
  - `hostileDurationReduction` (Elduriana), applied by `SpellCastingServiceImpl` against a hostile target.
  - `reapSouls` (Solve-Vidas), a caller-driven sweep run after any damage with the wearer's own context. It splits
    into `soulReapingReach`, `reap` and `recoverReapedVigor` for a client that holds only a stand-in for the victim.
  - `onAffectedBySpell` (Ladra do AEther), caller-driven once per Magia landing.
- **`combat.EquipmentRetaliation`**:
  - `#applyTo` and `#damageWeapon`.
  - Reported on `DeliveredAttackResult#getEquipmentRetaliation()` and `IncomingAttackResult#getEquipmentRetaliation()`.
  - `DeliveredAttackResult#getProjectilesDestroyed()` gives how many projectiles were destroyed.
- `AmmunitionService#consume(…, int destroyed)` spends every shot but records only the survivors for recovery.
  `CombatantSheet#discardFiredProjectiles` takes projectiles back off the ledger when the attacker's client learns
  of the destruction later.
- `ItemImprovement#camuflada(TerrainType)` and `#getSelectedTerrain()`; `DefensiveImprovement#requiresTerrainChoice()`.
  The choice is optional, so a bare Camuflada still loads.
- Terrain-aware cascading overloads, `Improvement#resolveBonus(…, SceneContext)` and
  `Item#resolveEnhancementBonus(…, SceneContext)`. The roll's Scene reaches equipment bonuses.

## Fixed

- `ItemImprovement` now forwards `resolveCriticalMarginIncrease`, `resolveCriticalDamage` and `preventsDisarming`.
  This makes **Benção Selvagem**'s Favor real: Margem Crítica Menor +1 with Armas Naturais.
- `DamageInteraction` (the attack path) now notifies the victim's items of the damage they took. Before this,
  **Benção de Proteção**'s +5 extension only fired from `DamageService#applyDamage`.
