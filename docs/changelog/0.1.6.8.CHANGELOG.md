# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.1.6.8

**Artefatos de Conjuração.** The 9 casting foci from `equipamentos.txt` are authored, with their Correntes and
Efeitos Ativos. This is the full scope of the table ruling of 2026-10-12 (plan: items-remaining, Phase 3).

## Rulings and readings

- An artefact helps **only while drawn**, and only while its wielder meets its Requisitos. That gate covers its
  Favor *and* its Efeitos Adicionais, per the item convention. The "/" in its Requisitos means either Atributo.
- ⚠️ The Adaga de Cerimônias' "Dano Base +1" reaches a Magia's damage and healing as a flat +1, because a Magia has no
  Dano Base scale.
- ⚠️ The Varinha's "Custos de Bônus Bases" is read as the Magia's PM.
- ⚠️ Two artefacts granting Vantagem still give one Vantagem. The post-cast Defesas bonus takes the largest.

## Added

- **`ItemCategory.CASTING_ARTIFACT`** and **`item.CastingArtifactItem`** (9 constants, `ItemTemplate` + `Weapon`):
  - Adaga de Cerimônias, Báculo, Bisarma Canalizadora, Bola de Crista, Cajado do Amparo, Cajado de Guerra, Gládio
    Canalizador, Grimório and Varinha.
  - **`Weapon#canAttack()`**: Bola de Crista, Grimório and Varinha are pure foci. `AttackDelivery` refuses an attack
    with one (`WEAPON_CANNOT_ATTACK`).
- **`item.CastingArtifacts`**, the one scan over the drawn artefacts. It feeds:
  - the Domínio do Mana Vantagem and Margem Crítica das Magias (`CriticalServiceImpl`, Conjuração roll only);
  - damage and healing +N, including an artefact's bound Esfera de AEther (+3);
  - Defesas +1 for 1 Rodada after casting, applied by `castSpell`;
  - the first Magia of each Rodada's −1PA (Grimório) and −1PM (Varinha);
  - Bola de Crista's +1 Distância step (`AttackRangeServiceImpl`), its reported +1UD on Toque, and its suppression of
    sum-triggered Menor casting criticals;
  - each artefact's Corrente, appended to `SpellCastingResult#getGrantedEffectChains`.
- **Correntes**:
  - `effect.ToqueDivino`: +2 to damage or healing.
  - `effect.FuturoPrevisto`: the Magia's Efeito Crítico at Menor.
  - `effect.MarcasDaProtecao`: the caster's Defesas bonus becomes +3; never on a Semente.
  - `effect.MarcasDaGuerra`: the damage/heal and Defesas bonuses become +2.
- **`SpellCastingService#getCastingCriticalEffectApplications`**: 2 with a Grimório (Otimização Metamágica).
- **`item.ArtifactActiveAbility`**, the 1PA + 2PM, 3-Rodada buffs, offered through `Character#getActiveAbilities()`
  while the artefact is drawn and usable only by someone who can cast or mimetize:
  - Adaga: `ModifierType.DAMAGE_BASE_INCREASE` +1, or +2 under its Favor.
  - Bisarma: Vantagem on both Ataques and on Domínio do Mana.
  - Gládio: Margem Crítica +2, or +3 under its Favor.
- **`ModifierType.DAMAGE_BASE_INCREASE`**, read by `DamageBaseServiceImpl`'s sheet overload and by
  `SpellCastingServiceImpl`.
