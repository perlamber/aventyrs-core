# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.0.48

**Senhor da Briga, the second Título Aventyr** (`docs/rules/titulos.txt:974-1090`, *Centelha
Bruta* → `TitleArchetype.BRUTO`) — all eighteen traits, in `org.aventyrs.core.title.senhordabriga`.
The caller's guide is [`docs/senhor-da-briga.md`](docs/senhor-da-briga.md).

## Why this needed new machinery

Santo's traits act on Defesas, Blessings and Auras. Senhor da Briga's act on **the attack itself**
with Armas Naturais, and no roll path scanned a held Título before. Each mechanism below has at
least two real consumers.

`TitleCatalog#all()` now offers Senhor da Briga (Despertar Antecipado can pick it).

## Table rulings (2026-09-22)

- **Fantasma do Ringue** is a conditional passive — the printed 2PD/2PA and "Efeito Ativo" are not an activation.
- **Fingir Fraquezas'** Defesas bonus lasts 2 Rodadas.
- **Campeão da Taverna's** +1 Defesas per crit **stacks** until the GM ends the combat.

## Readings to confirm

- Impacto Elemental under Grande Mestre ("causam danos mágicos") is typed `DamageType.ELEMENTAL` of the chosen element, since `MAGICO` cannot carry one.
- Fantasma do Ringue's three Defesas clauses are cumulative (up to +5).
- "Rodada Ímpar" is 0-based round 0, 2, … — the table's Rodada 1, 3, … (same as `DestinoFeat`).

## Behaviour changes

- `Santo#consumeAttackCharges` spends one Furor de Sylph charge — callers may replace their own
  per-attack `consumeEnhancedAttack` loop with `TitleAttackModifiers.consumeCharges`.
- `CombatantSheet#startNewScene()` also drops combat-scoped state.
