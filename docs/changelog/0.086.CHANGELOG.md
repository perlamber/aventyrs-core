# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.0.86

**Talentos de Devoção.** The devotion tier and the twelve Talentos de Devoção of the Panteão de Tellus. The plan is
`docs/plans/devoto-tiers-plan.md`.

## New

- **The tier:**
  - `character.DevotionTier` (`ADEPTO` < `FIEL` < `FUNDAMENTALISTA`).
  - `Character#devotionTier` and `#isDevotedAtLeast(rung)`.
- **Rung picks:**
  - `Character#devotionPicks` (`feat.DevotionPick`), with `#getDevotionPicks(talento, rung)` and
    `#replaceDevotionPicks`.
- **`character.services.DevotionService`:**
  - `setTier` — the player at creation, the Narrador afterwards; it validates and costs nothing.
  - `owedPicks` — the reached rungs still waiting for a pick.
  - `recordPicks` — validated against the rung's options; errors `DEVOTION_PICK_NOT_OWED` and
    `INVALID_DEVOTION_PICK`.
- **`feat.DevotoFeat`** (`FeatCategory.DEVOTO`, `Type.DEVOTO` — catalogued and bought, offered by no starting slot):
  - Acólito da Luz Primordial
  - Adepto da Escuridão Profunda
  - Armamento Vulcano
  - Astúcia de Sylph
  - Bênção de Surt'Eldur
  - Caminhar de Epona
  - Escolhido de Gaea
  - Impacto Ymiriano (its Fiel rung is `DevotoFeat.ImpactoYmirianoActiveAbility`: 2PD, once per Rodada)
  - Mentalidade de Tesla
  - Tocado por Undine e Haloi
  - Abraçado pela Esquecida
  - Cultista Umbral
- `FeatRequirements#requiredDeityCategory` — Cultista Umbral's "Cultista de um Senhor Umbral".
- **New `Feat` hooks:**
  - `resolveTargetedLifeSteal`
  - `resolveHealingReceivedAdjustment`
  - `resolveHealingDealtBonus`
  - `resolveHealingReceivedBlessings`
  - `resolveDamageTakenReductionAgainst`
  - `resolveSpellEffectChains`
  - a sheet-aware `resolveDamageRetype`
- `SpellCastingResult#getGrantedEffectChains` — Correntes the caster's Talentos add to a cast, for the caller to
  chain.
- `HealingSource#healerFeatBonus`.

## Changed

- **Roubo de Vida now heals in combat.** `AttackDelivery` chains an `effect.RouboDeVida` stage right behind a landed
  hit's damage. It uses the attacker's `LifeStealService#getTotalLifeSteal` plus any Roubo de Vida granted against
  that target, and never heals more than the hit dealt. Existing sources such as Corruptor Sombrio's Roubo de Vida 1
  are now applied.
- `CombatantSheet#heal`:
  - reads the recipient's healing adjustment (not on Roubo de Vida);
  - adds the healer's Talentos to the healer's bonus;
  - grants the recipient's healed-Blessings once PV were actually recovered.

## Rulings / readings

- **Table rulings (2026-10-01):**
  - the player picks the tier at creation, and the Narrador changes it freely;
  - a rung's pick is made when that rung is first reached, and kept while the tier is lowered;
  - Resistência às Correntes +2 works like Resoluto's margin;
  - Umbral effects are the Magias of the Árvores Umbrais;
  - Armamento Vulcano Fiel gives +1 in total.
- ⚠️ **Readings marked on the constants:**
  - An Umbral *character* is one devoted to a Senhor Umbral or to A Esquecida.
  - "Devoto de Gaea/Surt'Eldur" means exactly that Divindade, not the paired ones.
  - Escuridão Profunda's "construções e cavernas" is a Cena terrain of `URBAN`/`CAVE`.
  - Undine e Haloi's "embarcação, rio ou mar" is an `AQUATIC` Cena.
  - Epona's "se movimentando" means having moved this Rodada.
  - Vulcano's "−1 to enemies' Margem Crítica" is one number of Resistência a Críticos.
  - Impacto Ymiriano's window covers the whole Rodada.

## API and client

- **aventyrs-api** stores `devotionTier` and `devotionPicks` on the character (Talento name, rung and encoded
  value). It refuses a pick naming no Talento de Devoção, and adds `PUT /api/character-sheets/{id}/devotion-tier`,
  which changes only the tier.
- **Client:**
  - The wizard's identity step offers the tier.
  - The hub shows the tier, and offers the owed rung picks, which also open right after buying a Talento de
    Devoção.
  - The GM console has a new **Devoção** screen, outside the Cena, where the Narrador raises or lowers each
    character's tier.
