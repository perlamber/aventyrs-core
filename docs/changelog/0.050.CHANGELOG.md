# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.0.50

**Rolamento Ofensivo's "por 1 Rodada" survives to the approached enemy's Turn.**

## Turn-start countdown

- `TemporaryEffect` takes an optional `countsDownAtTurnStart` (default `false`, so every existing
  effect is unchanged). Such an effect is advanced by `CombatantSheet#startTurn(int)` and skipped
  by `tickTemporaryEffects()`/`finishTurn()`.
- **Table ruling (2026-09-23):** "por 1 Rodada" on something done on the holder's own Turn lasts
  until their Turn begins in the next Rodada — cast in Rodada 4, it ends as their Rodada-5 Turn
  starts. Counted down at Turn end, it lapsed before anyone else acted.
- `AttackerGuard#defesasUntilNextTurn(attacker, bonus)` and
  `ActivationWindow(source, rounds, countsDownAtTurnStart)` opt in.

## Senhor da Briga

- Rolamento Ofensivo's +3 Defesas guard against the approached enemy, and Grande Mestre's
  one-Rodada Vantagem window, now count down at Turn start. Before, the guard expired at the end
  of the activator's own Turn and never reached the enemy's attack.
