# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.0.95

**Invocation plans** — step 3 of `docs/plans/spell-casting-audit.md` (core half).

## New

- **`magic.invocation.InvocationPlan`** — an invocation before anything is placed. It holds:
  - the creatures, at the caster's Graduação, with their powers rolled;
  - their exclusivity group;
  - their Duração, and whether Concentração holds them;
  - the extra PM.
- **`NatureInvocationService` plan methods:** `planAliado`, `planCancaoDeFlora`, `planExperimento`, `planOrgulho`,
  `planLaboratorio`, `planAnciente` and `planTotemPredator`.
  - Each Magia's rule now lives in its plan.
  - The Scene-placing methods (`invokeAliado` …) place a plan through `NatureInvocationServiceImpl#place`.
  - A client whose Scene lives on a server sends the plan there instead.
- **`NatureSummon.restore(kind, graduation, powers)`** — the creature a persisted summon describes.
