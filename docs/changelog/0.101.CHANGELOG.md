# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.0.101

**Habilidades de Competência, Phase 2** — Domínio do Mana (`docs/plans/skill-competency-wiring-plan.md`).

## New hooks on `SkillCompetencyAbility`

- **`resolveCriticalMarginIncrease(skillType, ctx, attackSource, holder)`** — the longest form; the 2-arg one
  delegates to it and `CriticalService` asks it. Ataque Preciso and `ArtesAprimorarComArteAbility` moved onto it.
- **`resolveSpellDamageBonus` / `resolveSpellHealingBonus` / `resolveSpellDurationIncrease(spell, caster)`** —
  summed by `SpellCastingService#castSpell` beside the Talentos' own.

## Habilidades now real (each +1 at the 5ª and 10ª Graduação em Domínio do Mana)

- **Letalidade Arcana** — Margem Crítica Menor +1/+2/+3 on the Conjuração (a Domínio do Mana roll) and on an attack
  a `Spell` delivers.
- **Conjuração Duradoura** — +1/+2/+3 Rodadas on a Duração counted in Rodadas. An Instantânea or a Concentração
  (reported as 0) is left alone.
- **Arcanismo Explosivo** — +2/+3/+4 on a Magia's dano (`primaryDamage`) and cura (`healingBonus`).
- **Concentração Inabalável** — nothing to do yet: Concentração breaks only on casting or attacking, never on
  damage. Documented on the constant.
