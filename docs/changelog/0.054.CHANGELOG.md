# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.0.54

**A foe presents a GD per Perícia, plus a general GD.** Before this a `MonsterTemplate`/
`MonsterSheet` carried one Grau de Dificuldade that only its attacks used
(`attackDifficulty`/`attackBonus`) and a separate flat Atenção (`perception`). A foe now authors a
GD for each Perícia it knows and a general GD for every other task. This groundwork is for foes
entering a Cena hidden: their Furtividade GD is what a watcher's Atenção roll must reach.

## New

- `monster.SkillDifficulty(DifficultyLevel level, int bonus)` — one GD a foe presents;
  `getValue()` flattens it (base threshold + bonus); `SkillDifficulty.DEFAULT` is Médio +0.
- `MonsterTemplate#getGeneralDifficulty()` (abstract), `#getSkillDifficulties()` (default empty),
  `#getSkillDifficulty(SkillType)` (the entry, else the general GD).
- `MonsterSheet#getGeneralDifficulty()`, `#getSkillDifficulties()`, `#getSkillDifficulty(SkillType)`,
  and `MonsterSheet.of(Character, Player, int, int, SkillDifficulty, Map<SkillType, SkillDifficulty>)`
  for restoring a persisted foe with its per-Perícia GDs.
- `AbstractMonsterTemplate` builder: `.generalDifficulty(SkillDifficulty)` and
  `.skillDifficulty(SkillType, SkillDifficulty)`.

## Changed

- `getPerception()` (template and sheet) is now derived: the `ATTENTION` GD flattened.
- `MonsterSheet.of(Character, Player, int, int, DifficultyLevel, int[, UUID])` keeps its shape, but
  the pair is now the **general** GD (it used to be the attack GD).
- `GenericMonster`: each archetype's old attack GD is its general GD, and its old perception is an
  explicit `ATTENTION` entry — every number it presents is unchanged.
- `Zumbi`: general GD Fácil +0; the Conjurador's "Bônus em Perícia de Ataque" now rides only the two
  attack Perícias' entries (before, the single attack GD carried it).

## Removed (breaking)

- `MonsterTemplate#getAttackDifficulty()`/`#getAttackBonus()`, `MonsterSheet#getAttackDifficulty()`/
  `#getAttackBonus()` — use `getSkillDifficulty(ATAQUE_CORPO_A_CORPO | ATAQUE_A_DISTANCIA)`.
- `AbstractMonsterTemplate` builder `.attackDifficulty(…)`/`.attackBonus(…)`/`.perception(…)`.
- `HidingService.DEFAULT_MONSTER_PERCEPTION` (14). **Behaviour change:** a foe that authors nothing
  now spots at its general GD — Médio, 18 — instead of 14.
