# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.0.89

**Profano and Sagrado.** A hit's sacred or profane nature, as a qualifier on its type. A Profano hit is still Físico
or Mágico, which is what RD/RM/RDS read.

## New

- `character.DamageSanctity` (`PROFANO`, `SAGRADO`).
- **`DamageDescriptor`** gains a `sanctity` component; its two-argument form still works. `#withSanctity` sets or
  replaces the nature.
- **`DamageScope`** gains a `sanctity` component, and `DamageScope.sanctity(s)` builds a scope for every hit of one
  nature. An immunity or Meio-Dano scope can now name it.
- **Reductions:**
  - `ModifierType#PROFANE_DAMAGE_REDUCTION` and `#SACRED_DAMAGE_REDUCTION`;
  - `DamageService#getSanctityDamageReduction` sums them from Habilidades, worn items and timed bonuses;
  - they apply to a descriptor-carrying hit of that nature.
- **On the hit:** `DamageInteraction#withSanctity`. `AttackDelivery` marks the head:
  - Profano when a triggered Corrente is a Toque Sombrio ("em substituição aos seus tipos");
  - otherwise the nature the weapon's socketed Pedra gives (`PowerStoneType#getOffensiveSanctity` — Opala Sagrado,
    Turmalina Profano, "em adição aos seus tipos").

  An immunity to that nature zeroes the hit; a reduction of it comes off. ⚠️ On this path it comes off after the
  other mitigation and any Meio-Dano.
- `character.services.SanctityMitigation` — the immunity and reduction for a Corrente's or curse's own raw damage.
  Used by Definhar ("Dano Físico Profano"), Veneno Vampírico ("Dano Mágico Profano") and Toque Sombrio's +1.
- `MonsterTemplate#getDamageImmunities` and `MonsterSheet#isImmuneToDamage`. A stat block names the damage it is
  immune to.
  - `Zumbi`'s Anatomia de Morto-Vivo Menor: "imunes a danos Profanos e Naturais".
- **Pedras do Poder:**
  - Opala Purificadora's defensive "Danos profanos reduzidos em -3";
  - Turmalina Obscura's base "Danos … Sagradas reduzidos em -3".
