# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.0.39

One addition: character creation can now say **which Talentos a character starts with**.

## Starting Talento slots — `CharacterCreationService`

Every character picks **2 General Talentos**, plus whatever its Raça's "Perícias e Talentos" clause
grants. Until now every race's javadoc listed that grant as a gap.

Three new methods on `CharacterCreationService`:

- `getStartingFeatSlots(Race)` — the slots in order: `DEFAULT_GENERAL_FEAT_SLOTS` (2) General
  slots, then `Race#getStartingFeatSlots()`.
- `getStartingFeatOptions(Character, StartingFeatSlot[, CharacterSheet])` — every authored Talento
  the character may put in that slot right now. Ask again after each pick: an earlier pick can
  satisfy a later Talento's `requiredFeat`.
- `grantStartingFeats(Character, List<Feat>[, CharacterSheet])` — grants `picks.get(i)` into slot
  *i*, **free of XP**. Throws `INVALID_STARTING_FEAT_SELECTION` for a wrong pick count or a pick its
  slot doesn't offer, and `FEAT_REQUIRES_CHOICE` for a bare choice-carrying constant. Not atomic.

New types in `org.aventyrs.core.feat`:

- `StartingFeatSlot(source, pools)` — `Source.DEFAULT` or `Source.RACE`; a Talento fits when any
  pool admits it and judges it eligible.
- `FeatPool` — `Categories`, `Named`, `EspecialistaTagged`, and `RacialOf(race, trees)`, which
  judges a racial Talento's Raça clauses against another race (Vampiro's life-race, a Mestiço's
  parent).
- `Feat#isEspecialistaTagged()` — true for `ESPECIALISTA_TECNOLOGICO` and the three
  `ARTESAO_DE_REGALIAS_*`, the Talentos whose header carries the `Especialista` tag.

Every playable race now declares its slots. Rules readings to know:

- **Humanos** get their "2 Talentos Gerais" on top of the default two — 4 in total.
- **Elfos'** "Arqueirismo" is read as `ARTILHARIA`.
- **Dólos** and **Flaminídeos** get their Talento Elemental twice (3 race slots).
- **Avianos'** Ossos Ocos is `MonstruosoFeat#OSSOS_OCOS`, a single-option slot.
- **Mestiços Elementais** (not Nascidos da Floresta) may swap their first slot for a Talento Racial
  from the trees their parent's own grant draws from.

## Breaking change

`HomemFera` takes a required second argument, `HomemFera.Criacao` (`HUMANOS`/`ELFOS`/`FEERICOS`),
which decides the tree of its first starting Talento:

```java
new HomemFera(HomemFera.EspiritoAnimal.WEMIC, HomemFera.Criacao.ELFOS);
```
