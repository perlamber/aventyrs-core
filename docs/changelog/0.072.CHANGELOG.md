# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.0.72

**Defeitos e Qualidades — Phase 1: the model and the creation rules.** `docs/rules/defeitos-e-qualidades.txt`
(imported from *v19 - Defeitos e Qualidades 20260715*); plan in `docs/plans/defeitos-e-qualidades-plan.md`.
Every Defeito/Qualidade level is authored and held, but **its effect is not wired yet** (Phases 2–3).

Table rulings (2026-09-28/29): optional for every character, no campaign switch; no Qualidade without a
Defeito — the Talentos-Gerais trade is kept but only beside a creation Defeito; sense clauses read every
Atenção roll; any Regalia is "Verdadeira"; Deficiência Física's limb→Perícia table; Iniciativa
Vantagem/Desvantagem is ±2 on the Scene-entry roll; "aleatoriamente" is rolled at creation and stored;
Restrição Moral narrative; curse suppression of Qualidades out of scope; Grave's "Habilidade de Ego" is a
second Vantagem de Ego.

Readings: a second Vantagem de Ego from a Superação needs no Ego threshold (the Superação is the
grant), only an Ego without one; a Superação "Habilidade de Atributo adicional" needs its Atributo at the
first-slot base (its "pré-requisitos"), Tendência Atlética Maior's does not; Precognição's Especialização
needs Atenção trained.

## New

- `org.aventyrs.core.defect`: `Defect`, `DefectSeverity` (Superar cost 3/5/7 EXP), `Quality`,
  `QualityClass`, `SuperacaoBenefit`, `QualitySource`, `HeldDefect`, `HeldQuality`, and the choice enums
  `Limb` (with its dependent Perícias), `Sense`, `VulnerabilityKind`, `EnergyKind`. `Defect#FREE_TEXT`.
- `feat.DefeitoFeat` (36) and `feat.QualidadeFeat` (24); `FeatCategory.DEFEITO`/`QUALIDADE`,
  `FeatCategory.Type#isCatalogued()`.
- `Character#defects`, `#qualities`, `getBonusAttributeAbilitySlots()`; `getFeats()` folds in their effects.
- `CharacterCreationService#applyDefectsAndQualities`, `getStartingFeatSlots(Character)`,
  `getTradableGeneralFeatSlots(Race)`, `MAX_QUALITIES`; `INVALID_DEFECT_SELECTION`.
- `StartingFeatSlot.Source.DEFECT`, `StartingFeatSlot.defect(boolean)`, `isGeneralOnly()`.
- `AttributeAbilityService#hasFreeBonusSlot`.

## Changed

- `grantStartingFeats` matches picks against `getStartingFeatSlots(Character)`.
- `grantAttributeAbility` falls back to a bonus slot when the Atributo's own are full.
- `FeatCatalog` excludes every non-catalogued type, not only the Antecedentes.
