# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.1.7.4

**Monster auras.** Phase 3 of `docs/plans/monster-manual-v19-revision-plan.md` (manual 20261015). Table rulings
2026-10-15: a pulse fires at the holder's Turn start, rolled once for everyone; Dádiva de Gaea heals the monster and its
monster allies only; Aura do Paradoxo lasts Concentração + 2 Rodadas, ending after 2 Rodadas with nobody else inside.

- `scene.MonsterAura` (radius, `AuraReach`, open-ended `AuraBonus`es and `wardedConditions` while inside, the holder's
  own PA while an enemy is inside, a lingering remainder after leaving, an empty-area end, reported Terreno Difícil,
  an exemption, and an `AuraPulse`), `AuraHit` and `AuraPulseResult`.
- `MonstrousAbility#resolveAuras(holder, context)` produces them — a passive aura always, an emitted one while its
  Efeito Ativo's state runs; `CombatantSheet#resolveMonsterAuras()` and `#isMonster()`.
- `Scene#refreshMonsterAuras(holder, holderContext)` (revoke-then-grant against `MonsterAuraLedger`, cleaned up on
  `removeParticipant`), `#getMonsterAuraCoverage`, `#pulseMonsterAuras(holder, holderContext, round, DiceRoller,
  distanceUd)` → hits the caller applies (`AuraHit#applyTo`) on the sheets it controls.
- `sheet.SustainedState` (a sourced state held by Concentração + N) and `sheet.ConditionWard` (immunity while held, read
  by `isImmuneToCondition`); `hasEffectFrom`/`removeEffectsFrom` see `SourcedState`, `SustainedState` and a sourced
  `LifeSteal`.
- Wired: **Aura do Paradoxo** (−2PA inside, +3PA while an enemy is, 1-Rodada linger, holders immune, Concentração + 2,
  empty end), **Aura Elemental** (1d6 Elemental / Apex 2d6 Mágico Elemental, Média), **Aura dos Ventos Caóticos**
  (Terreno Difícil, Média/Longa while flying) with **Invocar Tempestade**'s Cego + 2UD push (Abominação 2d6 Vento −1/UD),
  **Dádiva de Gaea** (Descanso Curto/Total heal and Medo/Possessão ward for the monster and its monster allies),
  **Presença do Ceifeiro** (1d6 Mágico Profano to living enemies, life-stealing), **Mestre das Marionetes** (−1
  Autocontrole every 2 Rodadas, Possuído at zero), **Flagelo de Lacerto**'s fear (Abalado in Média) and storm (2d6
  Natural to non-Brotos).
