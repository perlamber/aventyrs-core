# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.0.88

**Golpe Sobrenatural, Gana Elemental and Golpe Cataclísmico.** Escancarar Defesas and Explosão Cataclísmica now have
live consumers.

## New

- **Hooks on `Feat`:**
  - `resolveTargetDefenseOverride` — a Talento rolls its holder's attack against the DM "ao invés da DF".
    `AttackDelivery` re-reads every target's Defesa of that type: a foe's from its stat block, a sheet's through
    `DefenseService`.
  - `resolveActivationManaCost` — the PM a roll-activated Talento costs. It is reported on
    `InteractionResult#getActivationManaCost()` for the caller to spend, never deducted here.
- **Longer overloads**, each defaulting to its shorter form:
  - `Feat#resolveDamageRetype(…, holder, SkillRoll)`
  - `Feat#resolveCriticalHitEffectChains(…, holder, SkillRoll)`
  - `Feat#resolveDamageBaseIncrease(Character, Weapon, CombatantSheet)` — read by
    `DamageBaseService#getDamageBase(CombatantSheet, Weapon)`.
- **`AssassinoFeat#GOLPE_SOBRENATURAL`** — an opt-in on the roll:
  - allowed only on a Golpe de Finalização (the opposed target has lost half its PV), once per Rodada;
  - 1PM, reported;
  - rolled against the DM, for Dano Mágico;
  - its critical carries Escancarar Defesas.
- **`ElementalFeat#GANA_ELEMENTAL`** — `GanaElementalActiveAbility` (1PA, 2PD) opens a 2-Rodada window. While it is
  open, the holder's weapon attacks get Dano Base +2 and deal Físico Elemental of the holder's own element. ⚠️ "a Arma
  escolhida" is read as every weapon, since the activation records none.
- **`ElementalFeat#GOLPE_CATACLISMICO`** — on the holder's first attack of the Rodada while Gana is active:
  - Margem Crítica Menor +1;
  - rolled against the DM;
  - Dano Elemental (mágico);
  - on a critical, the Corrente – Explosão Cataclísmica.
