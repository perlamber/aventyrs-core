# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.0.81

**Ego spending — Phase 8: Autocontrole.** Plan in `docs/plans/ego-spending-plan.md`.

## New

- `character.services.AutocontroleEgoService` (+`Impl`) — each method pays one Autocontrole point of its type
  (through `useEgoPointsForEffect`, so Determinação Heroica follows; `NOT_ENOUGH_EGO_POINTS`, nothing spent).
  - Temporary: `halveMaleficio(sheet, effect)`, `grantProtection(sheet, RD|RM|RE, element)` (RA + the chosen one,
    one instance each, until the holder's next Turn begins), `recoverAsLongRest(sheet)`.
  - Permanent: `removeMaleficio(sheet, effect)` (+ Cena immunity to its kind), `negateDamageThisRound(sheet)`,
    `recoverAsTotalRest(sheet)` (PM/PD as a Descanso Total, every PV).
  - `applyDefence(sheet, defenceRoll, AutocontroleDefence)` — marks the defence roll.
- `ego.AutocontroleDefence` — `AVOID_CHAIN`, `IGNORE_MINOR_CRITICAL` (temporary), `AVOID_CHAIN_WITH_IMMUNITY`,
  `AVOID_CRITICAL_WITH_IMMUNITY` (permanent); `SkillRoll#withAutocontrole`/`#hasAutocontrole`. `AttackReceiver` keeps
  what's avoided from landing (and reports it as not triggered), and grants the immunities.
- `sheet.CenaImmunity#kindOf` (a Condição's type, an Efeito Crítico's type, else the effect's class) and
  `CombatantSheet#grantCenaImmunity`/`#isCenaImmune` — refused by `applyEffect`/`applyCondition` and left out of a
  defence's chain; dropped by `startNewScene()`.
- `CombatantSheet#halveEffectDuration(effect)` (rounded up), `#getRunningEffects()`, `#getDamageTakenThisRound()`,
  `#negateDamageThisRound()`, `#isDamageNegatedThisRound()`.
- `sheet.TimedElementalResistance` — RE held for a Duração, summed by `getElementalResistanceInstances`.
- `TemporaryBonus.untilTurnStarts(type, value, rounds, source)` — the "por 1 Rodada" ruling as a factory.
- `TranslatableMessages.NOT_A_RUNNING_EFFECT`.

## Changed

- `applyDamage` tracks the PV lost this Rodada (reset at `startNewRound()`), and takes none while the Rodada's
  damage is negated. `TemporaryEffect#shortenTo` may now shorten a running effect — Autocontrole's halving only.

## Rulings / readings

- Table rulings (2026-09-30): "reduzir a zero todo o dano sofrido em uma Rodada" is the current Rodada,
  retroactive; "tornar-se imune a ele" is to that same kind.
- ⚠️ Halving is rounded up, so it never ends a malefício — removing one is the permanent point's.
- ⚠️ "Ignorar os Efeitos de um Acerto Crítico Menor que você tenha sofrido" is read on the defence (the attacker's
  Menor = the defender's Falha Crítica Menor). A PV cost paid (`payWithVitality`) is a price, not damage suffered.
- "Receber RA" is one instance (−2), as are the RD/RM/RE that ride it.
