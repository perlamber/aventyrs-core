# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.0.57

**Healing the fallen, and the Curandeiro Título.** The rules limit healing on fallen characters, and
core now enforces those limits:
- A character in **Coma** recovers at most **1PV from each distinct heal effect**, counted as a total
  for the whole time they are in Coma. A real Descanso is the one exception: 1PV each time, but
  repeatable.
- A **dead** character is reached by no heal unless an Ability says so.

The two Abilities that bend these limits belong to the Curandeiro Título, which is added here complete —
every trait is real. The two that touch the limits:
- **Levantar os Caídos**: the holder's Magias and Habilidades de Curandeiro ignore the Coma cap on a
  Coma begun in the current Cena.
- **Curar os Mortos**: 2PD per heal that may reach someone dead for at most ½ Medicina e Cura Rodadas.

Rulings (table, 2026-09-25):
- A Corrente is one heal effect with its Magia.
- A Regeneração's whole budget is one effect, and it cannot raise the dead.
- Roubo de Vida is exempt.
- An Execução Real can never be undone.
- Benção de Boros adds no global "can't act at 0 PV" gate; only its doubled PM/PD cost is modelled.
- Domínio da Cura uses whichever of Medicina e Cura and Domínio do Mana has the higher Graduação, for
  effects only (not prerequisites).
- Transferir Vitalidade's PV are locked until a Descanso Verdadeiro.
- Transferir Rancor's +1d6 is a timed extra-dice bonus the attack roll reports.

## New

- `sheet.HealingSource`: which heal effect a recovery is. It is a record of a key, whether the key
  may repeat in Coma, the healer, the Magia and the Habilidade de Título. Factories:
  - `spell(spell, caster)`: keyed by name, because an `AlternateSpellVersion` has no `equals`;
  - `spellChain(chain, parentSpell, caster)`: shares the parent's key;
  - `titleAbility(ability, activator)`;
  - `rest(restType)`: repeatable;
  - `egoSpend(advantage, spender)`;
  - `regeneration(regeneration)`.

  It also has `isSpellOfType(MagicType...)`.
- `CombatantSheet#heal(int, HealingSource)` enforces the limits:
  - **Dead:** refused unless the target is not beyond revival **and** a Título of the healer's
    `claimRevival`s it.
  - **Coma:** capped at `COMA_HEAL_CAP` (1). An already-used non-repeatable key is refused outright.

  Both refusals leave a Sangramento running. The limit is judged on the status *before* the heal, so
  a reviving heal is not capped.
- `CombatantSheet#hasEnteredComaThisScene()`, `getRoundsSinceDeath()`, `getRoundsSinceFallen()`,
  `markBeyondRevival()` / `isBeyondRevival()`.
- `CombatantSheet#grantCharge` / `getCharges` / `consumeCharge`: single-use charges per source
  (Curar os Mortos, Curandeiro Veloz). They stack, and are dropped at `startNewScene()`.
- `CombatantSheet#payWithVitality(int)` / `getLockedDamage()` / `releaseVitalityLock()`: PV that only a
  Descanso Verdadeiro recovers. Every heal stops short of the locked part, and
  `RestService#applyRest(…, verdadeiro = true)` releases it.
- `CombatantSheet#receiveEgoLoan(EgoDomain, CombatantSheet lender)`: a temporary Ego point lent until
  the end of the Cena. At `startNewScene()` it goes back to the lender if unused, and is lost if used
  (the ally keeps its own points either way).
- `CombatantSheet#recordDamageDealt()` / `hasDealtDamageThisScene()`, recorded by
  `DamageService#notifyDamageTaken` on the damage's source.
- `AventyrTitle#bypassesComaHealingCap(HealingSource, CombatantSheet)` and
  `#claimRevival(HealingSource, CombatantSheet)`. Both return `false` by default, and only the
  healer's Títulos are asked.
- More `AventyrTitle` hooks, each neutral by default:
  - `resolveSkillRollBonus(SkillType, holder)`, read by `AbstractSkillInteraction`;
  - `resolveSkillSubstitute(SkillType, primary)`, read by the new `Character#getEffectiveSkill` /
    `getEffectiveGraduation`. The Domínio do Mana roll, MetamagicoFeat's DM bonus and a summon's
    Graduação now use these;
  - `resolveHealingBonus`, added by `heal` before halvings;
  - `resolveCastingDifficultyReduction` / `resolveCastingActionPointReduction` /
    `consumeCastingCharges`, read by `SpellCastingService`;
  - `resolveActivationActionPointReduction` / `consumeActivationCharges`, read by Título activation;
  - `resolveDeterminationCostMultiplier` / `resolveManaCostMultiplier`;
  - `permitsHitPointPayment` (for an ability and for a Magia);
  - `upgradesRests`, read by `RestService#applyRest`;
  - `resolveDamageTakenAllyBlessings`, granted by `notifyDamageTaken` to the victim's allies. These are
    resolved from whichever party's `SceneContext` is at hand.
- Título activation (`AbstractTitleAbilityInteraction`):
  - reports `InteractionResult#actionPointCost`: the trait's PA after reductions, floored at 1;
  - multiplies PD by the Títulos' multipliers (the subclass still sees the base figure);
  - accepts `TitleCostPayment.HIT_POINTS` among the choices to pay PD as locked PV.
- `SpellCastRequest#payManaWithHitPoints`. `SpellCastingResult#manaCost` (reported, multiplied) and
  `#vitalityCost` (for the caller to `payWithVitality`).
- `ModifierType.ATTACK_AND_CONJURATION_DIFFICULTY_REDUCTION` and `EXTRA_DAMAGE_DICE`.
  `InteractionResult#extraDamageDice` reports the second on an attack roll.
- `RestType#oneCategoryHigher()`, and `title.TitleCostPayment`.
- **Relayed healing, for a target whose real sheet lives in another client:**
  - `sheet.RelayedHealer` and `HealingSource#relay(boolean targetIsDead)` resolve the healer's half (healing
    bonus, Coma bypass, a revival permission spent now) on the healer's sheet;
  - `CombatantSheet#heal` honours a relayed source against the target's own state;
  - new `AventyrTitle` hooks `grantsComaHealingBypass` / `claimRevivalWindow` (the target-independent halves
    of the Coma bypass and revival hooks);
  - public target-side helpers `DonsDoCurandeiroInteraction#healTarget`/`source`,
    `EncantoRegenerativoInteraction#healTarget`/`source`, `CuraProtetoraInteraction#protect` and
    `TransferenciaInteraction#receivePoints`.

  aventyrs-game-client uses these to deliver Curandeiro's (and Santo's) heals to another player's
  character.
- New `TranslatableMessages` constants:
  - `HIT_POINT_PAYMENT_NOT_PERMITTED`;
  - `TITLE_ABILITY_ALREADY_USED_THIS_ROUND`;
  - `TITLE_ABILITY_TARGET_REQUIRED`;
  - `TARGET_ALREADY_AFFECTED_UNTIL_REST`;
  - `TARGET_NOT_WOUNDED`;
  - `TITLE_ABILITY_CHOICE_NOT_PERMITTED`.
- `title.curandeiro`, with every trait real:
  - `Curandeiro` (`ABENCOADO`, the "Centelha Abençoada" header);
  - `CurandeiroDespertar` (the touch) and `CurandeiroSpecialization` (Médico de Guerra, Mártir
    Altruísta);
  - `CurandeiroAbility`, `MedicoDeGuerraAbility` and `MartirAltruistaAbility`;
  - Interactions: `DonsDoCurandeiroInteraction`, `CurandeiroVelozInteraction`,
    `CurarOsMortosInteraction`, `CuraProtetoraInteraction`, `EncantoRegenerativoInteraction`, and
    `TransferirDeterminacaoInteraction` / `TransferirEssenciaInteraction` (sharing
    `TransferenciaInteraction`).

  `TitleCatalog#all()` lists Curandeiro. See `docs/curandeiro.md` for the per-trait caller contract.
- `SpellEffectContext` gains a `caster` component, with `of(boolean, CombatantSheet)`. `FRIENDLY` and
  `HOSTILE` are unchanged, and a `null` caster still returns them.
- `SpellHealingEffect` gains a 5-arg constructor taking the caster. `Sobrecura` gains
  `(CombatantSheet casterSheet, Spell parentSpell, int rolledDice)`.

## Changed

- **Transferir Determinação/Essência give the whole amount asked** (up to what the giver has). The ally
  keeps what it is missing and the rest is lost. Previously only what landed was spent. This lets a
  transfer to an ally on another client work, since the giver can't see what the ally is missing.
- **Cura Protetora applies its Blessing and no longer reports it**, so a caller can't grant it twice or
  misread its scope.

- **Every rules-named heal is now sourced**, and so limited:
  - `SpellHealingEffect` and `Sobrecura`;
  - Abençoado pela Luz's touch, which now reports the PV actually recovered, not the amount offered;
  - `RestServiceImpl#applyRest` (repeatable);
  - `EgoPointsServiceImpl#useEgoPointsForEffect` (PV only);
  - `Regeneration`.

  `heal(int)` stays unsourced and exempt, and so does `healFromLifeSteal`.
- `SpellCastingServiceImpl` passes the caster into the `SpellEffectContext`, so a cast heal reaches
  the caster's Títulos.
- `RealExecution` marks what it destroys beyond revival. Its "não poderá ser ressuscitado" is
  enforced now, not merely documented.
- Tests that damaged a blank character far past −max PV to exercise heal arithmetic now use sturdier
  characters or smaller figures. Those targets had been dead, and healing them is now refused.

## Callers

- Build a heal through the sourced overload. Pass the caster in `SpellEffectContext` /
  `SpellHealingEffect` / `Sobrecura`, or the fallen-healing Títulos never see the heal.
- Call `startNewRound()` and `startNewScene()` on every sheet at those boundaries. They drive the
  death window and "Coma begun in this Cena".
- Activate Curar os Mortos *before* the heal it pays for. One activation pays for one heal.
- **Persistence (aventyrs-api) has new per-sheet state it does not store yet:**
  - the used-in-Coma keys;
  - whether the Coma began this Cena;
  - Rodadas since death;
  - the beyond-revival mark;
  - charges;
  - locked Vitalidade damage;
  - open Ego loans;
  - Rodadas since falling;
  - "dealt damage this Cena".

  A sheet rebuilt by replaying `applyDamage` reads as having *just* fallen or died.
