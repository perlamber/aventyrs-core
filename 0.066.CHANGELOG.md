# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.0.66

**Spellcasting extensions** (plan Phase F). This release covers mimetizar with chosen Árvores and a
live grant, Talento reductions to a Magia's Mana cost, per-cast opt-ins, bonuses to a cast's
Conjuração roll, damage and Duração, and Magia storage. Several TODOs claiming "mimetizar has no
mechanism" were stale: `MimetizedSpell` and its casting service already existed.
Feat TODO lines: 268 → 256.

## Changed

- `MimetizedSpell` has **value equality**, and a PD cost of **0** is now valid: a Semente costs 0 PM,
  so paying "PD em substituição aos PM" makes it free. Only a negative cost is refused.
- `MimetizedSpell#requiredForm`: casting is refused outside that Forma
  (`MIMETIZED_SPELL_FORM_REQUIRED`).
- `Character#getMimetizedSpells()` is **live**: the stored list plus every held Talento's current
  `Feat#getGrantedMimetizedSpells`, deduplicated. So a rung gated on a Título Desperto appears once
  it is.
- A cast's Mana cost is now reduced by Talentos (floored at 1 unless it was free), and
  `SpellCastingResult` reports it.

## New

- `feat.ArvoresMimetizadasFeat`: chosen Árvores for Alma Feérica, Abençoada pelo Conclave and
  Aptidão Mágica Ampla.
- `feat.MagiasMimetizadasEscolhidasFeat`: chosen Magias for Aptidão Mágica Assombrosa, Suprema and
  Dracônica. `optionsFor(holder, rung)` lists what each rung offers.
- `Feat` hooks:
  - `resolveManaCostReduction(spell, caster, target, casterContext)`;
  - `resolveCastingActionPointReduction(..., activatedFeats)`;
  - `resolveCastingRollBonus(spell, character, activatedFeats)`;
  - `resolveSpellDamageBonus(spell, character, activatedFeats)`;
  - `resolveCastEffectDelayRounds(spell, character, activatedFeats)`;
  - `resolveSpellDurationIncrease(spell, character)`.
- `SpellCastRequest#activatedFeats`: the Talentos a caster opts into for one cast.
- `SpellCastingResult#effectDelayRounds`: reported only; the caller schedules the delay.
- `SpellCastingService#resolveActivationTime(spell, caster, round, activatedFeats)`.
- `magic.StoredSpell`, `magic.SpellStorageService` / `Impl`, and `CombatantSheet#getStoredSpells`,
  `#storeSpell`, `#removeStoredSpell`. Every stored Magia is cleared at any Descanso.
- Messages: `MIMETIZED_SPELL_FORM_REQUIRED`, `SPELL_STORAGE_NOT_GRANTED`, `SPELL_STORAGE_FULL`,
  `STORED_SPELL_NOT_HELD`, `STORED_SPELL_RELEASE_COST_NOT_PERMITTED`.

## Now real

- **Mimetizar:**
  - `ElficoFeat#ALMA_FEERICA`: Broto and Muda of one Árvore Natural, plus its Emergente with 2
    Títulos, paid in PD at the rung's Mana cost.
  - `GorgonaFeat#ABENCOADA_PELO_CONCLAVE`: Semente; Broto at 2PD with one Título; Muda at 3PD with
    two. Only in Forma Feérica.
  - `MetamagicoFeat#APTIDAO_MAGICA_AMPLA` (Semente, and Broto at 2PD), `#APTIDAO_MAGICA_ASSOMBROSA`
    (Muda, 3PD), `#APTIDAO_MAGICA_SUPREMA` (Emergente, 5PD), `#APTIDAO_MAGICA_DRACONICA`
    (Florescente, 5PD).
  - `FeericoFeat#ESPIRITO_DA_FLORESTA`: every Natural Broto, at 2PD.
  - `GnomoFeat#DUENDE`: every non-Profana Semente, free.
- **Mana cost:**
  - `MetamagicoFeat#ENGENHEIRO_DO_MANA`: −1PM.
  - `FadasFeat#ASPECTO_DA_BONDADE_NATURAL`: −1PM on Divinas/Naturais cast on the caster, allies or
    neutrals.
  - `FuriasFeat#ASPECTO_DA_DECOMPOSICAO_NATURAL`: −1PM on Profanas/Encantamentos cast on anyone
    outside the caster's sub-group.
- **Opt-ins:**
  - `MetamagicoFeat#CONJURACAO_RAPIDA`: −1PA, with Desvantagem on the Conjuração roll and damage.
  - `#PROCRASTINAR_CONJURACAO`: −1PA, and the effect is delayed 1 Rodada.
- **Bonuses:**
  - `GorgonaFeat#MARCA_DA_MALDICAO`: +2 Conjuração and damage on Magias Naturais.
  - `ElementalFeat#ARCANISMO_ELEMENTAL`: +2 damage on Elemental Magias of the holder's element, and
    +1 Rodada on Encantamentos of that element.
- **Storage:** `MetamagicoFeat#ARMAZENAR_MAGIA` and `#ARMAZENAR_MAGIA_SUPERIOR`.

## Readings

- ⚠️ Abençoada pelo Conclave's and Aptidão Mágica Ampla's Sementes are free. Neither clause states
  a figure, and both pay their Mana in PD.
- ⚠️ "Neutro" is a target in neither of the caster's `SceneContext` lists.
- ⚠️ The Aptidão ladder's "uma de cada Árvore" and Suprema's "que você conheça uma Muda de seu ramo"
  are not validated on the picks.

## Callers

- Offer the new choice types: `MagicTree` for three constants, `Spell` for three.
- Put an opted-in Talento on `SpellCastRequest#activatedFeat`, and hold an effect back
  `getEffectDelayRounds()` Rodadas.
- To store a Magia:
  1. Cast it as usual.
  2. `SpellStorageService#store` the result.
  3. `release` it later with an Ação Livre (or a Reação with Superior), and apply what comes back.
