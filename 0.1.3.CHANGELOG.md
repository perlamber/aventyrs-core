# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.1.3

**Bruxo — the reported clauses closed** (`docs/bruxo.md`).

## New

- **`SummonEnhancement#wings(rolledD6)`** — Benção do Vento do Leste's wings as a timed `GRANTS_FLIGHT` `Blessing`
  (1d6 + the Iluminado bonus Rodadas, sourced `WINGS_SOURCE` so a second pair renews), bought for
  `WINGS_ACTION_POINTS` (3PA).
- **`SummonEnhancement#cometTargetValue`**, `COMET_SKILL` (Ataque à Distância), `COMET_RANGE` (Distância Média) — the
  Nevasca comet's roll, made instead of the Conjuração roll, against the higher of the casting GD and the targets' DM.
- **`SummonEnhancement#damageEquipment`** — Raios do Nordeste's burst on "todos os seus equipamentos".
- **`Feat#halvesThroughImmunity`**, asked of the attacker by `DamageServiceImpl`: an immunity it pierces becomes a
  Meio-Dano. `EnhancedSummonFeat` answers it for a Fogo hit — Chamas do Norte's "inimigos imunes a fogo ainda sofrem
  metade dos danos".
- **`Bruxo#dismissFamiliar`** — the token left: its Subordinado is released; the familiar stays bound.

## Still open
