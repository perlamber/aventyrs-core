# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.0.65

**Área de Efeito resolved, area attacks, and retaliation dealt** (plan Phase E). An `AreaOfEffect`
now turns into hexes and the combatants in them, and a Talento can make an attack an area one.
Evasão answers an incoming area attack, and a reported `Retaliation` can be dealt in one call.
Feat TODO lines: 269 → 268.

Table ruling (2026-09-26): when an *attack* becomes "Área de Efeito – Explosão", it is a 1 UD
burst centred on the attack's target, covering the target and everyone adjacent. The rules size
no such Explosão.

## New

- `scene.grid.AreaFootprint`:
  - `covering(area, origin, aim, columns, rows)`: the hexes an area covers.
    - `CIRCULO`/`EXPLOSAO`: a disc around `origin`.
    - `LINHA`/`PENETRANTE`: a 1 UD line toward `aim`, origin excluded.
    - `CONE`: a wedge toward `aim`. ⚠️ Its 60° spread is an inference.
  - `occupants(hexes, MovementMap, excluded)`: who stands in them, less `excluded` (the caster).
- `AreaOfEffect.ATTACK_EXPLOSION`: the ruling above.
- `Feat#resolveAttackArea(attacker, attackSkill, attackSource, skillRoll)`: the area a Talento
  turns an attack into. Default `null`.
- `AttackTargetingService#resolveAttackArea(...)`: asked *before* the attack is built.
- `DeliveredAttack#areaOfEffect`: declares an area attack. The target cap no longer applies, the
  other targets take **full** damage (no additional-target Meio-Dano), and an area the attacker
  is not granted is refused with `AREA_OF_EFFECT_NOT_GRANTED`.
- `IncomingAttack#areaOfEffect`, and `SkillCompetencyAbility#resolveAreaOfEffectDefenseBonus`,
  added by `AttackReceiver` to the Defesa total against an area attack.
- `Retaliation#dealTo(attacker, source, sceneContext, damageService)`: applies the typed hit
  through `DamageService#applyDamage`, then the Malefício if PV were lost. Returns the PV lost.
- `TranslatableMessages.AREA_OF_EFFECT_NOT_GRANTED`.

## Now real

- `DuelistaFeat#ATAQUE_GIRATORIO`: an Arma or Ataque Desarmado attack whose roll names it becomes
  an Explosão. Its once-per-Rodada limit is the caller's, since a `CombatantAction` records no
  activated Talento.
- `MobilidadeFeat#INVESTIDA_SELVAGEM`: an Investida becomes an Explosão.
- `EsquivaEApararCompetencyAbility#EVASAO`: +3 Defesas against an area attack, +5 at 7 Graduações.

## Not built

- Outward damage at a Turn boundary. `TrollFeat#REGENERACAO_REATIVA_ESPINHOSA` is its only
  unblocked consumer, and `DraconicoFeat#AURA_DRACONICA` still needs a Sopro-use trigger.
- `OrquicoFeat#TREMOR`: its Efeito Ativo is a one-attack activation, which `ActiveAbility` is not.
- The two Investida Selvagem Aventyr Talentos: they need the count of enemies actually damaged,
  which is known only after the caller applies each chain.

## Callers

- To deliver an area attack:
  1. Ask `AttackTargetingService#resolveAttackArea`.
  2. Resolve the footprint around the target with `AreaFootprint`.
  3. Name the occupants as `additionalTargets` and set `areaOfEffect`.
- Flag an incoming area attack with `IncomingAttack#areaOfEffect(true)`.
- Deal a `DeliveredAttackResult`/`IncomingAttackResult` retaliation (`getRetaliation()`,
  `getOnHitRetaliations()`) with `Retaliation#dealTo`.
