# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.0.51

**Gigante Enfurecido, the third Título Aventyr — and the machinery a state-driven Título needs.**
Caller guide: [`docs/gigante-enfurecido.md`](docs/gigante-enfurecido.md).

## Gigante Enfurecido

- New package `title.giganteenfurecido`: `GiganteEnfurecido`, `GiganteEnfurecidoDespertar` (Frenesi),
  `GiganteEnfurecidoAbility`, `GiganteEnfurecidoSpecialization`, `TitaEnlouquecidoAbility`,
  `BerserkerAbility`, `GritoDeGuerra`, and one Interaction per activated trait. Registered in
  `TitleCatalog`.
- `sheet.Frenzy` / `FrenzyMode`: the Frenesi state. Its bonuses are folded into
  `CombatantSheet#getTemporaryBonus`.

## Costs and requests

- `EgoCost` + `AventyrTitleAbility#getEgoCost()`; `AbstractTitleAbilityInteraction#resolveEgoCost` /
  `onEgoSpent`. New refusal `NOT_ENOUGH_EGO_POINTS`.
- `TitleAbilityActivationRequest`: `choices` (multi-pick, `getChoices(Class)`) and `diceRoller`.
- `AventyrTitleAbility#isEligible(title, character)` for prerequisites naming a Talento.

## Sheet

- `getFrenzy`/`getOwnFrenzy`/`startFrenzy`/`endFrenzy`; `isCompelledToAttackNearest`,
  `treatsEveryoneAsEnemy`, `cannotDieFromNegativeHitPoints`, `treatsBeneficialSpellsAsHostile`,
  `isSkillUsePrevented`, `getConcentrationActionPointSurcharge`, `getElementalResistanceInstances`,
  `healFromLifeSteal`, `isAtOrBelowZeroHitPoints`, `isMinorCriticalAndChainSuppressed`,
  `floorNextDamageAt`.
- **Game time**: `passHours`, `oweHourlyEgoRecovery`/`settleHourlyEgoRecovery`,
  `applyEffectUntilTrueRest`/`completeTrueRest`; `RestService#applyRest(…, verdadeiro)`.
- `DelayedEgoGrant` takes a Rodada count, a guard, and a recovery flag.
- `Exhaustion`, `FrightfulCondition` (a fear that is an Encantamento and keeps its origin as it
  decays), `InflictedCondition`, `AreaDamage` (+ `AreaDamage#cataclysm`).
- `Blessing#reaching(Range)` and `Blessing#countingDownAtTurnStart()`.
- `InteractionResult`: `areaDamage`, `inflictedConditions`, `grantedFrenzy`, `frenzyRecipients`.

## Behaviour changes

- **A Bônus Variável now reaches the attribute total**: `Character#getEffectiveAttributeTotal(domain, sheet)`
  adds the sheet's `<ATTR>_BONUS`, so a Frenesi's or Sacrifício Ymiriano's Força reaches the melee
  dano term, and a Vigor bonus reaches max PV.
- `HitPointsService#getStatus(sheet)` stops at Coma for a sheet that cannot die.
- `DamageServiceImpl`: RE per element held on the sheet; `wouldDropToZeroOrBelow`.
- `AbstractSkillInteraction` refuses a Perícia the sheet forbids (`SKILL_USE_PREVENTED`).
- `MimetizedSpellCastingServiceImpl` refuses under Silêncio/Frenesi (`SPELL_CASTING_PREVENTED`).
- `SpellCastingServiceImpl`: Titã's +1PA, Frenesi Arcano (`SpellEmpowerment`), Esmeralda's Vantagem on
  damaging casts, Cataclismo `areaDamage` on instant Magias, `mustOvercomeMagicDefense`.
- `AttackDelivery`: a Frenesi Assustador fear suppresses Críticos Menores and Correntes while its
  Gigante is down.
- `TitleAttackModifiers#damageOverride` (Retaliação Furiosa).
- `ReactionTrigger`: `SELF_HIT_BY_SUCCESSFUL_ATTACK`, `ADJACENT_ENEMY_ATTACKS_OTHER`,
  `SELF_WOULD_DROP_TO_ZERO_HP`; `ReactionContext#pendingDamage`.
