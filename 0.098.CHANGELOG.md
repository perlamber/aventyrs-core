# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.0.98

**Subordinados from the Talentos** — the invocations plan's Phase 1 leftovers.

## New

- **Agnação Ancestral Superior's Peão is commanded for real.** `AncestralCounselService#perform(orc, ctx, pawnBenefit)`
  gives Vantagem em Perícias or Margem Crítica. It lasts until the holder's next Descanso. With no free Subordinado slot
  it is not granted (`pawnSubordinateGranted` false).
- **`SubordinateService#command(…, endsAtRest)`** — a Subordinado ended by a Descanso (`applyEffectUntilRest`).
- **`SubordinateService#summonShadowCounsel(holder, benefit, ctx)`** — Abraçado pela Esquecida, Fundamentalista:
  - a sombra conselheira Peão, Cavaleiro or Torre;
  - held by Concentração +1 Rodada (`Subordinate.sustained`);
  - once per Cena (⚠️ kept per combat);
  - 3PA, reported.
- **`sheet.Sustained#release()`** is public and generic: any sustained effect (the MaleficioWard, a sustained
  Subordinado) starts its trailing Rodadas through `TemporaryEffect#startCountdown`.
- **`CombatantSheet#restScopeOf(effect)`** — which Descanso ends a held effect, for a caller persisting it.
- `DevotoFeat#reached` is public.
