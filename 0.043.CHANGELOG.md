# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.0.43

**Santo's Efeito Base — Despertar — does something now.** All three of its clauses were already
written as real, tested arithmetic on the `Santo` class and consumed by nothing. Wiring them up took
three different mechanisms, and turned up two stale TODOs and one documented doctrine that needed an
exception.

## ⚠️ Breaking changes

- **Three `Santo` methods are renamed** into overrides of new `AventyrTitle` hooks:
  `getDefesasBonus` → `resolveBaseDefesasBonus`, `getPrimaryTitleAllyDefesasBonus` →
  `resolvePrimaryTitleAllyDefesasBonus`, `getIgnoreCriticalEffectDurationInRounds` →
  `resolveMinorCriticalImmunityRounds`. Same bodies, same numbers.
- **`CombatantSheet` gains two methods**: `ignoresMinorCriticalEffects(SceneContext)` and
  `resolveProjectedAuras(SceneContext)`. Any implementation not extending `AbstractCombatantSheet`
  must supply them.
- **Holding a Título is no longer Defesas-neutral.** A character holding a Santo now has +2 Defesas
  they didn't have before, which will move any test asserting an exact total for such a character.

## The base effect is now an `AventyrTitle` concern

Three new default hooks, all returning "this Título's base effect does nothing of the kind", so no
other Título is affected: `resolveBaseDefesasBonus`, `resolvePrimaryTitleAllyDefesasBonus`,
`resolveMinorCriticalImmunityRounds`. They live on `AventyrTitle` rather than
`AventyrTitleAbility` for the same reason `getBaseEffectDescription()` already did — the base effect
is a property of holding the Título at all, not a Habilidade.

## "+2 em suas Defesas" — scanned

`DefenseServiceImpl` gained a fourth source beside the three-source ability scan, equipped items and
the `TemporaryBonus` pool: every held Título's `resolveBaseDefesasBonus`. Broad `DEFESAS`-shaped, so
it lands on DF and DM alike.

- **Summed on both the `Character` and `CombatantSheet` paths.** Those two deliberately don't
  cascade, so a shared `sumTitleBaseDefesas` feeds both; adding it to one only would have put the
  bonus on half the callers.
- **Continuous, not windowed.** The "Duração 1 Rodada para cada Especialização e Suprema" sentence
  belongs to the crit-ignoring clause ("esta Habilidade"); "Você *também* recebe" starts a new one.
  A Santo with nothing acquired yet has a 0-Rodada window, so reading the Defesas half as windowed
  too would leave a freshly-Desperto Santo with no base effect whatsoever.

## The Título Primário clause — a real Aura, granted and revoked

"Aliados adjacentes a você recebem Bônus em Defesas iguais à metade dos Bônus que você receber em
função do Efeito Base deste Título."

- **`scene.ProjectedAura(ModifierType, int value, Range radius, String source)`** — a passive Aura a
  combatant projects onto nearby allies, with no activation, cost or Duração.
- **`CombatantSheet#resolveProjectedAuras(SceneContext)`** — what this combatant projects, read off
  `Character#getPrimaryTitle()` (the Primário gate is structural: a Santo in the Secundário slot
  projects nothing). It *describes*; it does not target — a sheet holds no back-reference to its
  Scene, so the context is there only to compute the amount.
- **`Scene#refreshProjectedAuras(holder, holderContext)`** — revoke-then-grant against a
  per-holder ledger, the same shape and for the same reason as `applyInitiativeBlessings` and
  `grantedBlessings`. Revocation is by reference (`removeEffect`), so an unrelated `DEFESAS` bonus
  from another source is never disturbed.
- **`Scene#addParticipant(sheet, initiative, group, distances)`** — a new overload that resolves the
  joiner's Auras as it arrives.
- **`removeParticipant`** now revokes in both directions: the Auras the leaver was projecting, and
  whatever anyone else's Aura was granting *them*.
- **`TemporaryBonus.openEnded(type, value, source)`** — a bonus with `null remainingRounds`, the
  open-ended shape `TemporaryEffect` already modelled. What ends an Aura's bonus is leaving range,
  not time passing, so it must not tick away at the Rodada boundary.

**`DefenseService` needed no change for this half** — a granted `DEFESAS` `TemporaryBonus` was
already summed on the sheet overloads. Which is exactly why it *must* be actively revoked:
`DefenseServiceImpl` trusts whatever sits on the sheet and knows nothing about distance.

### Why this one is granted when every other ally-facing passive is scanned

The `damage-and-combat` skill's "**Ally-facing passive grants are scanned, not granted**" rule still
holds, and now has a stated exception. Scanning works when the figure derives from the **recipient's**
own situation — Bastião dos Necessitados compares PV, which the recipient's context answers. This
one derives from the **holder's**: the amount is half of a bonus that counts *the holder's* adjacent
allies. A scan running from the recipient sees only its own neighbours and undercounts. Resolving
holder-side is the only way to get it right, and holder-side resolution has nowhere to put its
answer except onto the recipient — hence a grant, with `Scene` owning the revocation it already owns
for initiative Blessings. `ProjectedAuraTest#theAmountIsTheHoldersAdjacencyNotTheRecipients` pins
the difference.

**Deliberately not an `ActiveAura`.** That type is the provocation Aura — a binding ledger with a
forced attack target, a per-Rodada Desvantagem, a Descanso-scoped memory, a Rodada countdown, and
bindings that persist once made even after the foe leaves range. This needs none of it and wants the
opposite range behaviour. Its javadoc said "what an Aura *does* is not abstracted until a second one
exists"; the second now exists and wants a different shape, which is evidence the abstraction would
be wrong rather than that it is due.

## "Ignora Efeitos Críticos Menores" — the filter learns severity

- **`CriticalResult#isMinor()`** — a third accessor beside `isCriticalSuccess()`/`isCriticalFailure()`,
  crossing them rather than refining either. An "Efeito Crítico Menor" arrives as an
  `ACERTO_CRITICO_MENOR` on the attacker's roll (`AttackDelivery`) or a `FALHA_CRITICA_MENOR` on the
  defender's own (`AttackReceiver`); covering both is what lets one filter serve both directions.
- **`CriticalEffect#applicableTo(target, effects, criticalResult, sceneContext)`** — a longer
  overload, the 2-arg form delegating down with `null`s. Type immunity asks "which effect is this"
  and drops effects one at a time; severity immunity asks "how big was the critical" and drops the
  whole chain, because "Menor" names the critical that caused them rather than anything about the
  effects. A `null` `criticalResult` applies no severity rule — never "assume Menor".
- **`CombatantSheet#ignoresMinorCriticalEffects(SceneContext)`** — where the two sources meet: a
  held Título's window (`isWithinFirstCombatRounds`, the `InitiativeAdvantage#IMPETO` idiom) and a
  held Talento's unconditional `Feat#ignoresMinorCriticalEffects()`.
- **`MonstruosoFeat#ANATOMIA_UNICA`** ("Você é imune a Efeitos Críticos Menores") is now real. Its
  own TODO called it "the one clause in the catalog that would be *fully* served by widening
  `CriticalEffect#applicableTo` to consider severity" — that second consumer is what earned the
  widening.

Two of the three blockers Santo's TODO named turned out to be **stale**: the CriticalEffect
machinery did have a veto point (`applicableTo`, keyed on type rather than severity), and the
continuously-recomputed cross-character grant did exist (`resolveAllyAbsoluteDamageReduction`'s
scan, plus `Scene`'s revoke-and-regrant ledger). Only the `DefenseService` gap was accurate.

⚠️ Still TODO: `MonstruosoFeat#ANATOMIA_INCOMUM`'s "o primeiro Efeito Crítico Menor ... em cada Cena
de Combate" needs a per-Cena counter nothing tracks. And, as everywhere else in this core,
`refreshProjectedAuras` is **caller-driven** — nothing here watches for movement, so a consumer
calls it after any move or teleport, exactly as `Scene#refreshAura` and `Teleportation` document.
