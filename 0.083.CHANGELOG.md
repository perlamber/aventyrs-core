# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.0.83

**Ego spending — Phase 10: a PdN's Efeitos de Ego.** Plan in `docs/plans/ego-spending-plan.md`.

## New

- `CombatantSheet#isPdn()`, `#markAsExemplarPdn()`, `#recordPdnEgoUse(domain, type)`,
  `#drainImmediateEgoCompensations()`, `#drainCenaEndEgoCompensations()` — a PdN's Efeito de Ego spends nothing and
  is owed to every PJ as a temporary point in that Ego: at once for a temporary effect, at the end of the Cena for a
  permanent one. A `MonsterSheet` is always a PdN; any other sheet holding an Exemplar foe is marked.
- `EgoPointsService#payForEffect(sheet, domain, type)` — the one pay step the Sorte, Iniciativa and Autocontrole
  services share (a player spends; a PdN records what's owed). `#grantPdnCompensation(playerCharacters, domain)`
  hands the point over as a received point (source `EgoPointsService.PDN_COMPENSATION`).

## Changed

- **Only Exemplares use Efeitos de Ego** (table ruling, following `ego.txt`'s "apenas PdN Exemplares (quando
  inteligentes) podem usar"): `MonsterKind.REGULAR` allows none (was 2 per Cena, from `criacao-de-monstros.txt`);
  `MonsterSheet#checkEgoEffectAvailable` refuses a Regular. `MonsterKind.REGULAR_EGO_EFFECTS_PER_SCENE` removed.
- `useEgoPointsForEffect` on a PdN no longer spends its points (`MonsterSheet#recordEgoEffectUse` removed —
  `recordPdnEgoUse`).

## Rulings / readings

- Table rulings (2026-09-30): Exemplares only; a PdN spends nothing — the PJs receive a temporary point in that Ego,
  at once for a temporary effect, at the end of the Cena for a permanent one.
- Whether an Exemplar is intelligent is the Narrador's to judge; nothing checks it.
