# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.0.75

**Defeitos e Qualidades — Phase 4: Defeitos after creation.** Plan in `docs/plans/defeitos-e-qualidades-plan.md`.

## New

- `character.services.DefectService` (+`Impl`):
  - `grantDefect(character, defect, severity, choices)` — the Narrador imposes a Defeito during play: choices
    validated as at creation, no count limit, **no Benefício de Superação**. A Defeito already in force is
    *changed* to the new gravidade/choices, keeping its creation origin and Superação.
  - `getOvercomeCost(severity)` — 3/5/7 EXP, whatever the Defeito's origin.
  - `overcome(character, sheet, defect)` — Superar: spends the EXP and ends the effects. Nothing changes when
    it is refused (`DEFECT_NOT_HELD`, `NOT_ENOUGH_EXPERIENCE`).
- `HeldDefect#overcome` (a 7th record component; the 6-argument constructor still builds one in force),
  `#isActive()`, `#asOvercome()`, `#changedTo(severity, choices)`, `HeldDefect.duringPlay(…)`.
- `Character#getActiveDefect(Defect)`, `#imposeDefect(HeldDefect)` (the unvalidating mutator beneath the service).
- `TranslatableMessages.DEFECT_NOT_HELD`.

## Changed

- `Character#getFeats()` folds in only Defeitos in force; an overcome one **stays on record**, so what its
  Benefício de Superação gave (a Talento slot, a Habilidade de Atributo slot, the picks) is kept.

## Rulings / readings

- The progression lock is the caller's (`Campaign#requireProgressionAllowed`) for Superar; imposing a Defeito is a
  Narrador's act and may happen mid-Sessão. "Contexto e Oportunidade" are the table's to judge.
- Table rulings (2026-09-29): Superar keeps the Benefício de Superação; imposing a Defeito already held changes
  it rather than adding a second copy.
- The Narrador's optional "Benefício de Superação como premiação" for a long-held mid-campaign Defeito is not
  modelled.
