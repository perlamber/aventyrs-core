# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.0.53

**Terreno Difícil, Reposicionar, and Entre as Pernas.** Table rulings (2026-09-23): a difficult hex
costs 2UD; Reposicionar is 1UD, an Ação Livre, provoking no Reações; Investida and Reposicionar are
refused from, or along a path entering, Terreno Difícil.

## Movement geometry (`scene.grid`)

- `HexGrid#neighbours(position, columns, rows)` — bounds-safe, never constructs an off-board
  `GridPosition`.
- `MovementMap` (board extent, difficult hexes, occupants), `StepRules` (`canPass`, `canStop`,
  `isDifficult`, `enterCost`, `avoidingDifficultTerrain()`), `MovementPathfinder` (`reachable`,
  `pathTo`), `MovementPath` (`steps`, `cost`, `touchesDifficultTerrain`).

## Terreno Difícil (`MovementTerrainService`)

- `DIFFICULT_TERRAIN_COST` = 2; `stepRules(mover, map, moverContext, movementIndex)`.
- Ignored through four new hooks: `AttributeAbility#ignoresDifficultTerrain(int)` (Movimento Livre,
  first movement), `Feat#ignoresDifficultTerrain(Character, CombatantSheet)` (Cavalo de Chifres),
  `Item#ignoresDifficultTerrain()` / `PowerStoneType#ignoresDifficultTerrain()` (Rútilo
  Subterrâneo), `Race#ignoresDifficultTerrainOnLand()` (Pequenino's Sempre Veloz).
- `EnvironmentalState` gains `inDifficultTerrain` and `sharingSpaceWith` (the 4-arg constructor
  stays); `Scene#buildContext` gains an `EnvironmentalState` overload.

## Manoeuvres

- `ChargeService#canCharge(sheet, weapon, sceneContext)`; `begin` refuses
  `CHARGE_IN_DIFFICULT_TERRAIN`. The two-argument `canCharge` is now a default delegating with `null`.
- `Manoeuvre#REPOSICIONAR` + `RepositionService` (`DISTANCE_UD` 1, `ACTION_COST` Ação Livre;
  `REPOSITION_MOVEMENT_PREVENTED`, `REPOSITION_REQUIRES_FREE_ACTION`,
  `REPOSITION_IN_DIFFICULT_TERRAIN`). `MovementReactionService` never reports reactors to one.

## Senhor da Briga — Entre as Pernas

- `AventyrTitle#passesThroughEnemySpaces()`, `#mayShareSpaceWith(holder, occupant, enemy)`, and a
  `SceneContext` overload of `resolveCriticalMarginIncrease` (called by `CriticalServiceImpl`).
- Trespass enemy spaces as Terreno Difícil; stop in a larger enemy's space (anyone's under Malícia);
  +1 Defesas per Categoria of difference; Malícia's +2 Margem Crítica Menor Defensiva while sharing.
