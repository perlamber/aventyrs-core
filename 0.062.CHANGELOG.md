# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.0.62

**Four small hooks, each with at least two Talentos waiting on it** (plan Phase B): Iniciativa from
a Talento, a permanent Ego point from a Talento, vetoing an implied Condição, and a Reação ledger.
Feat TODO lines: 286 → 281.

## New

- `Feat#resolveInitiativeBonus(Character)`: a flat Iniciativa bonus, summed by
  `InitiativeService#getTotalInitiative`. Default 0.
- `Feat#resolveInitiativeBlessings()`: Blessings for winning initiative, now scanned by
  `InitiativeBlessingService` beside Vantagens, Habilidades de Atributo and Habilidades de
  Competência. Default empty.
- `Feat#resolveEgoBonus(EgoDomain, Character)`: permanent Ego points. Default 0.
- `Character#getEffectiveEgoTotal(EgoDomain)`: `EgoValue#getTotal()` plus every held Talento's
  `resolveEgoBonus`. Read by the permanent Ego pool ceiling, `InitiativeService` and
  `MoralHerdadaAbility`'s Fama. `EgoValue#getBase()` readers (Ego-ceiling Pré-requisitos) do not.
- `Feat#suppressesImpliedCondition(implier, implied)`: vetoes one implication edge in
  `CombatantSheet#getActiveConditions`. The same Condição applied directly, or implied by a
  different one, still lands. Default false.
- The Reação ledger:
  - `CombatantSheet#spendReaction()` and `#getReactionsSpentThisRound()`, reset by `startNewRound`
    and `startNewScene`;
  - `ReactionsService#getRemainingReactions(sheet, turnNumber[, sceneContext])`: total less spent,
    floored at 0.

## Now real

- `EscudeiroFeat#ESCUDO_VELOZ`: +2 Iniciativa with an equipped Médio or Pesado Escudo.
- `MobilidadeFeat#PORTA_ESTANDARTE`: + half the higher of Destreza and Carisma to Iniciativa (its
  PA/Movimento half stays TODO).
- `MobilidadeFeat#LIDERAR_O_AVANCO`: +2PA for 2 Rodadas on winning initiative.
- `MobilidadeFeat#INICIATIVA_APRIMORADA`: +1 permanent Iniciativa.
- `SobrevivenciaFeat#SORTE_DE_MOSES` / `#DETERMINACAO_DE_MOSES`: +1 permanent Sorte / Autocontrole.
- `ArtesMarciaisFeat#DOMINAR_ARTE_MARCIAL_SUBMISSAO`: not Desprevenido because of Caído.
- `DuelistaFeat#COMBATER_AS_CEGAS`: not Desprevenido because of Cego, and its price —
  Desvantagem on every Perícia roll made while Cego.

## Changed

- **A Reação is spent.** `ActiveAbilityService#activate` refuses a `REACTION` ability once no
  Reação remains this Rodada (`NOT_ENOUGH_REACTIONS`), and spends one on success. A Título
  activation priced as a Reação records one too, but does not refuse (it has no turn number).
  `ReactionOptionsService` reports an option unaffordable once none remain, so a Santo who already
  reacted is no longer offered Guarda-Vidas as affordable.
- `InitiativeService` and `ReactionsService` now scan `SkillCompetencyAbility#allFor` (racial and
  Talento-granted Habilidades de Competência too), not only the acquired list.

## Callers

- When resolving any other Reação yourself (an attack provoked by movement, a Defesa taken as a
  Reação), call `CombatantSheet#spendReaction()`, and gate on
  `ReactionsService#getRemainingReactions`.
- A display of an Ego's total should read `Character#getEffectiveEgoTotal`, not
  `getEgos().getEgo(domain).getTotal()`, or it will miss a Talento's permanent point.
