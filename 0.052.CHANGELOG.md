# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.0.52

**Persisting a Gigante between sessions.**

- `HourlyEgoRecovery` is public; `CombatantSheet#getHourlyEgoRecoveries()` and
  `#restoreHourlyEgoRecovery` let a caller persist and restore the Autocontrole a Frenesi still owes
  back by the hour, banked hours included.
- `CombatantSheet#isExhausted()` reports Uno com a Ira's exhaustion so it can be persisted and restored
  with `applyEffectUntilTrueRest(new Exhaustion(source), RestType.CURTO)`.
