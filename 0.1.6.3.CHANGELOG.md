# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.1.6.3

**Intelecto Superior is real**: Memória Eidética's Especialização and Dominar Padrões' Habilidade de Competência are
picked at creation and granted at the holder's first / third finished Sessão (table ruling, 2026-10-10: the
character's own count).

## Added

- **`Character#getCompletedSessions()` / `#recordCompletedSession()`** — the Sessões this character has finished,
  0 for a new one. The caller ending a Sessão advances it once per participant, **before**
  `CharacterSheet#applySessionEndAcquisitions`, and persists it.
- **`Quality#INTELECTO_SUPERIOR` declares its picks** (`resolveChoices`): a Especialização of a Perícia trained at
  that moment, not yet held; at Maior also a Habilidade de Competência of any Perícia, trained or not. Validated by
  `CharacterCreationService#applyDefectsAndQualities` like every other Qualidade pick — a Maior owes both.
- **`QualidadeFeat#INTELECTO_SUPERIOR_MENOR`/`_MAIOR` grant them through `Feat#getGrantedSkillTraits`** once
  `getCompletedSessions()` reaches `MEMORIA_EIDETICA_SESSIONS` (1) / `DOMINAR_PADROES_SESSIONS` (3). Derived like a
  `PendingAcquisition` — nothing is materialized, so `Character#getSpecializations` and
  `SkillCompetencyAbility#allFor` simply start reporting the pick.

## Changed

- An `INTELECTO_SUPERIOR` held without its picks is now refused at creation (`INVALID_DEFECT_SELECTION`).
