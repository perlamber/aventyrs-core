# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.0.46

**The whole Santo Título is re-authored from V19** (`docs/rules/titulos.txt`, imported this
release from *V19 Títulos 20260919.docx*). Every trait was authored from the previous revision,
and V19 changes almost all of them — so this is a **behaviour change**, not a documentation pass.
**Every Santo trait is activatable now**: the five that were unwired are closed, which needed five
new mechanisms.

## Why

Comparing the imported document against the code showed the enums' `description` strings and
formulas predated it. Several already-shipped interactions were therefore wrong rather than merely
stale: `SacrificioYmirianoInteraction` implemented a double-activation clause V19 does not have,
and `GloriaRelampejanteDeTeslaInteraction` granted an RA half V19 does not have either.

## Renamed

Two Habilidades are renamed after their V19 headings. Both propagate through code and tests.

| Was | Now |
| --- | --- |
| `AbracadoPelaEscuridaoAbility.ESPINHOS_DE_GAEA` | `ESPINHOS_VENENOS_DE_GAEA` |
| `AbencoadoPelaLuzAbility.PELE_ROCHOSA_DE_EPONA` | `CORPO_INDESTRUTIVEL_DE_EPONA` |

## Every Santo trait is activatable now

All eight activated Habilidades/Supremas and both Especializações report an interaction class.
Five of them were unwired before this release; closing them needed five new mechanisms, each
built for the clause that actually asked for it.

## New: Placidez de Undine, Rancor de Haloi is activatable

`PlacidezDeUndineRancorDeHaloiInteraction` pays its 2PD and **reports** what the one attack the
clause requires gains, on a new `InteractionResult#empoweredAttack` carrying
`title.EmpoweredAttack` (Margem Crítica +2, Roubo de Vida 3, Roubo de Determinação 2, and
`CriticalEffectType.OFERENDA_MALDITA` as an additional Efeito Crítico).

This is what got past "this one delivered attack" scoping, and it needed **no change to
`AttackDelivery`/`AttackReceiver`**: the activation resolves *before* the attack exists, so the
caller reads the report and folds it into the attack it then builds — the same pre-roll ordering
that makes `GUARDA_VIDAS`'s interception work.

Reported rather than applied on purpose: `sheet.LifeSteal` counts down in Rodadas and would keep
stealing for the rest of the Rodada, and Margem Crítica Menor has no `ModifierType` or
`TemporaryBonus` path at all. Still TODO: Roubo de Determinação has no mechanism (only Roubo de
Vida does), and the per-target "imunes a ela por 2 Rodadas" window has no Rodada-scoped ledger
(`markAffectedUntilRest` is until-Rest).

## New: Fúria dos Deuses

`FuriaDosDeusesInteraction` makes Abraçado pela Escuridão's own per-attack effect real, at all
three prices: 1PV buys Vantagem on the attack roll, 3PV eases its GD by one nível, and 5PV keeps
that reduction and adds 1d6+Vigor of dano. The player names the `Tier` on the request; the base
class charges its PV and refuses a self-fatal one.

This is what closed the **"one-time roll effect bought with a resource"** gap for a Título trait:
the spend happens before the roll it modifies, so it is an ordinary activation reporting an
`EmpoweredAttack`, and nothing reaches into a resolution in flight. The "Corrente de Efeito" the
text names needs no chain either — "o dano deste ataque aumenta 1d6+Vigor" is a flat addition to
the same dano roll, reported as dice plus flat for the caller to roll.

`title.EmpoweredAttack` grew to cover it, and is now the general per-attack bundle: attack-roll
bonus, GD reduction, extra dano dice and flat, reach, Margem Crítica, Roubo de Vida/Determinação,
and an additional `CriticalEffectType`. Three producers, every field inert unless its producer
sets it.

## New: Espinhos Venenos de Gaea, and a retaliation that is really computed

The thorns are modelled as a **state**, not a listener: the activation grants a round-scoped
`ModifierType.RETALIATION_DAMAGE` Blessing worth the holder's Vigor, and both attack orchestrators
read it off the defender on any Ataque Corpo-a-Corpo, reporting a `combat.Retaliation` (damage,
`DamageDescriptor`, and the Malefício that rides along). Melee only, judged by the Perícia; and
reported whether or not the attack landed — "atacarem", not "acertarem". Only the Veneno half is
conditional on damage actually being dealt.

Still **reported, never dealt**: this core computes damage only one way, so the caller applies it
against the attacker's own sheet — which is where it belongs anyway, since the attacker's RD/RA
should judge thorns like any other incoming hit.

**`ConditionType.ENVENENADO` is no longer inert.** It carries a `ModifierType.LIFE_MULTIPLIER` -1,
read by `HitPointsService#getLifeMultiplier(Character, CombatantSheet)`, so a poisoned character's
maximum PV really falls. ⚠️ The catalogue's own wording says "Multiplicador de **Bônus Base**" (a
stat that exists nowhere) while the clause inflicting it says "Multiplicador de **Pontos de
Vida**"; the concrete clause was taken as authoritative — worth confirming against the core
rulebook.

## New: Furor de Sylph, and a grant scoped to a count of attacks

`CombatantSheet#grantEnhancedAttacks`/`getRemainingEnhancedAttacks`/`consumeEnhancedAttack` is a
per-source budget for a clause that names no Duração at all. Deliberately **not** a
`TemporaryBonus` — one only counts down in Rodadas, so expressing it that way would have meant
inventing a figure the rules never state. Nothing clears it at a Rodada or Turn boundary, and
re-activating replaces rather than banks.

The "+1PA" is **scanned off that budget** rather than granted, through the new
`AventyrTitleAbility#resolveActionPointBonus` summed by `ActionPointsServiceImpl` — so it lasts
exactly as long as the attacks do and needs no revoke.

Still TODO, and none of it this clause's own gap: *Alcance Estendido* is not in the Obra-Prima
catalogue; the 1UD push and Reposicionar need geometry; and the +1d6 belongs on an attack this
activation — an Ação Livre funding several later ones — is not adjacent to.

## New: Corpo Indestrutível de Epona, and an effect whose figure changes per hit

`sheet.PeleDePedra` negates the first damaging hit outright, then contributes RDS 5 decaying -2
per hit. Neither half is a stat: no `ModifierType` says "reduce to zero", and a `TemporaryBonus`
holds one figure for its whole Duração. `DamageServiceImpl` therefore consults it **by type** —
the one place it reads a concrete effect rather than summing a `ModifierType`.

Read **last**, after RD/RA/Meio-Dano, so "o primeiro ataque que lhe *causaria* Danos" is judged
against what mitigation already left: a blow those turned aside never spends the negation. ⚠️ It
is spent inside `calculateFinalDamage`, which is therefore **not a pure query** while a Pele de
Pedra is held — deliberate, since `DamageInteraction` splits mitigation from application and a
hook in the latter would miss every real attack.

## Orgulho Elduriano is an Efeito de Encantamento, and that reshaped it

"Esta Habilidade é um Efeito de Encantamento" is real now, and it moved the state off the Scene.
The Aura is an **emitter**: as enemies enter its radius — through movement or a teleport, on the
next `refreshAura` — each is *cast* a `sheet.ForcedTargeting`, an `sheet.Enchantment` living on
**their own sheet**. `ActiveAura` no longer tracks who is bound or who has attacked whom;
`getForcedAttackTarget`, `auraHalvesDamage` and `recordAttack` all read the attacker's own effect.

**`CombatantSheet#applyEnchantment` is the single door**, and every decision in it is a fact about
the recipient rather than the caster:

- **Immunity** — `Character#isImmuneToEnchantments()`, from the new `Race#isImmuneToEnchantments`
  (Fada, Fúria, Górgona) minus `Feat#suppressesEnchantmentImmunity` (`GorgonaFeat
  #MARCA_DA_MALDICAO`'s "não possui a Característica Racial Imunidade a Encantamentos"). Three
  racial javadocs cited this exact gap as their blocker; it is closed. An immune foe takes nothing
  **and costs the Aura none of its budget** — it never caught them.
- **Duração** — a *harmful* Encantamento lands at half Duração while the recipient wears both an
  Armadura and an Escudo Ungido, which makes `SantoAbility#PROTECAO_UNGIDA`'s halving clause real
  for Encantamentos. Halved **at application only**; `TemporaryEffect#shortenTo` is package-private
  so nothing can halve a running countdown.

That door *is* "efeitos **diretos**", so the indirect half of every immunity clause ("ainda podem
ser alvos de efeitos indiretos, como sofrer danos de armas encantadas") needs nothing — an
enchanted weapon's damage never arrives there.

Each caught foe counts down **their own** Duração, so a foe entering late gets a full one rather
than the Aura's remainder. A bound foe keeps the compulsion out of range; the one thing that ends
it early is the caster leaving the Scene, which lifts every compulsion they cast.

## Proteção Ungida's dual mode

Now `variable(2)`: 2PD blesses one kind of item, 3PD blesses an Armadura and an Escudo at once,
gated on holding any Santo Suprema and on actually wearing both. A Variável cost makes the amount
mandatory, which is the right guard for a two-mode ability — there is no safe default between
blessing one item and two.

`sheet.Ungido` records which `ItemCategory`s are blessed, which is what makes "Enquanto utilizar
uma Armadura e um Escudo Ungido ao mesmo tempo" answerable at all. Kinds, not copies. The
Duração-halving that state unlocks is still blocked on an Encantamento/Maldição classification and
a public duration mutator.

## Two mechanism changes

**RDS is RD, not RA.** `AventyrTitleAbility` grows `resolveDamageReduction` /
`resolveAllyDamageReduction`, the RD twins of the RA pair, scanned by `DamageServiceImpl`. They
take the granting `AventyrTitle` — a figure like "1+ Metade das Habilidades de Santo que você
possuir" is a fact about the holder's own Título, which an enum constant cannot know. Both are
adjacency-scoped, so `DamageService#getTotalDamageReduction(target, damageType, source,
sceneContext)` is the new overload that sees them; the existing overloads delegate with `null` and
never do. No Santo Habilidade grants RA any longer.

**The provoking Aura deals Meio-Dano.** `Scene#getAuraAttackPenalty` becomes
`Scene#auraHalvesDamage`, and `DeliveredAttackResult`/`IncomingAttackResult` report
`auraHalvesDamage` in place of `auraPenalty`. The attack roll and the GD are untouched now; what
changes is that the chain head is marked `DamageInteraction#halvingDamage()`. `ActiveAura` gains
`maxTargets`, since V19's PD buy foes rather than Rodadas, and `Scene#refreshAura` stops binding
once an Aura is full.

## Compatibility

Breaking for callers naming either renamed constant, `Scene#getAuraAttackPenalty`, or the
`auraPenalty` accessors. `AbencoadoPelaLuzInteraction`'s constructors no longer take a
`RestService`. `SantoSpecialization#resolveShortRestHealAmount` is replaced by
`resolveTouchHealAmount(AventyrTitle)`, and `AbracadoPelaEscuridaoAbility#resolveDurationFromPvSpent`
is gone (V19 leaves Espinhos no PV-scaled Duração).

`SantoAbility#PROTECAO_UNGIDA` is `variable(2)` now, so **every activation must name its PD
amount** — `interaction.applyTo(sheet)` no longer works for it. `title.EmpoweredAttack`'s
canonical constructor grew five components; the two factories `ofCriticalAndSteal` and `ofRoll`
are the intended way to build one.

`CombatantSheet#countActivationsThisTurn` keeps working but **loses its only consumer** with
Sacrifício Ymiriano's double-activation clause. The mechanism stays built and tested.
