# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.0.103

**Habilidades de Competência, Phase 4** — the activated abilities (`docs/plans/skill-competency-wiring-plan.md`).

## New

- **`skill.CompetencyActions`** — the Habilidades de Competência that are actions. Each resolves its roll against the
  GD its rules text names and applies, or reports, the effect; an effect on a sheet the caller does not hold is a
  separate method the owner's client runs.
- **`CombatantSheet#studyDefensesOf`/`hasStudiedDefensesOf`** — whose Defesas this combatant studied, counted down at
  each Rodada and cleared with the Cena; read by `AbstractSkillInteraction` on every Perícia de Ataque.
- **`CombatantSheet#owePendingRestBonus`/`takePendingRestBonus`/`getPendingRestBonus`** — a bonus owed to the next
  Descanso's PV, PM and PD, paid by `RestService#applyRest`. Not stacking: a second one replaces the first.
- **`Character#getTrainedCompanions`/`trainCompanion`** and **`skill.empatiaselvagem.TrainedCompanion`** (a name and a
  Cavaleiro/Peão/Torre `SubordinateBenefit`) — lasting, persisted by the API (table ruling).
- **`Blessing#untilCombatEnds()`** — open-ended until `CombatantSheet#endCombat` ("até o final da Cena", per combat).
- **`TranslatableMessages.COMPETENCY_SPECIALIZATION_REQUIRED`**.

## Habilidades now real

- **Estudar Defesas** — `estudarDefesas`: Esquiva e Aparar against GD Difícil; on a success, every attack the holder
  makes against that target is one nível easier for 2 Rodadas.
- **Esconder Outros** — `esconderOutros(holder, total, specialization)` → the ally's `Hidden`, one nível below the
  tier the roll reached as an Especialista (table ruling), keeping its excess; Maestria da Ocultação or Infiltrador
  only. `esconderOutros(ally, hidden)` applies it. Adjacency is the caller's.
- **Milagreiro** — `rollMilagreiro` (GD Difícil), `milagreMaior` (the base Corrente margin) and `applyMilagreiro` on
  the patient: a Descanso Curto's PV, and its PM/PD with Milagre Maior. Once per patient until a Descanso Longo,
  failed attempt included — a `COMPETENCY:` rest-scoped use on the patient's sheet, so it persists with the others.
- **Medicina Alternativa** — `rollMedicinaAlternativa` (GD Médio); the caller rolls one 1d6 (PV, PM and PD alike —
  table ruling) and owes it on the patient's sheet.
- **Aliado da Natureza** — `trainCompanion` (Empatia Selvagem against GD Difícil) keeps the creature;
  `canCallCompanion`/`callCompanion` command one per Cena as a Subordinado, replacing one called before.
- **Espalhar Emoções** — a success under Comunicação, Mentir ou Omitir or Intimidação grants Vantagem on Persuasão
  rolls under the same Especialização until the combat ends (table ruling). The target and distance are the roller's.
- **Espalhar Reputação** — `espalharReputacao`: Artes against the Narrador's GD, raised to Médio when lower. The
  listeners' disposition stays the Narrador's.
- **Dom Bárdico** — unchanged; already real through a Perícia roll with a GD (Phase 0).
