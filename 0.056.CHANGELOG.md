# Changelog — aventyrs-core 0.0.56

**Saquear, and what equipping costs mid-combat.** The table authored a looting rule on 2026-09-24
(nothing in `docs/rules/` covers it): in combat, on their Turn, a character may Saquear a defeated
foe for 2PA, and everything the foe *carries* (never what it has equipped) goes to the Campanha's
shared bag. From there, any participant claims an item instantly and for free. Equipping an item
mid-combat costs 1PA per item. When combat ends, whatever the defeated foes still carry goes into
the bag for free.

Core gates and prices both actions. It moves no items and holds no bag: the looter's client has
only a stand-in for the foe, and the bag is persistence's (aventyrs-api's `CampaignDocument#bag`).

## New

- **A fallen foe is Terreno Difícil.** `scene.grid.MovementMap` gains a `defeated` component (the
  ids of occupants who are down, per `LootService#isDefeated`; the old 4-arg constructor keeps it
  empty) and `isDefeated(CombatantSheet)`. In `MovementTerrainService#stepRules`, a defeated enemy
  no longer blocks `canPass`, and its hex `isDifficult` (2UD to enter, closed to an Investida or
  Reposicionar). `canStop` is unchanged: you cross the body, you don't stand on it.

- `character.services.LootService` / `LootServiceImpl`:
  - `LOOT_COST` (2PA);
  - `getLootCost(SceneContext)`: the cost in combat, `NONE` outside it;
  - `canLoot`/`requireLootable(SceneContext looterContext, CombatantSheet target,
    CharacterStatus targetStatus)`;
  - `static isDefeated(CharacterStatus)`: `FALLEN`, `COMMA` or `DEAD`. It reads the status tier
    rather than live PV because a remote foe's PV are unknown to the looter's client.
- `sheet.CharacterSheet.EQUIP_COST_IN_COMBAT` (1PA) and `static getEquipCost(SceneContext)`:
  `NONE` outside combat. `equip(Item)` itself stays unpriced, and taking an item off is free.
- `TranslatableMessages.LOOT_TARGET_NOT_AN_ENEMY`, `LOOT_TARGET_NOT_DEFEATED`.
